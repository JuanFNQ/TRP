## The account

The first-person controller is built and, wherever a recording can show it, compared with traces recorded from the running game: sixteen scripted recordings (2026-10-07 to 2026-10-12), four vault traces, and thirty-nine recordings made by hand (2026-10-12).

- From the fourth recording on, every scenario replays with the game's movement state and the body animator's state on every frame (60 scenarios and 70,700 frames in the thirteenth).
- Distances are within 4.4 mm. The eye is within a millimetre or two in most scenarios and within 0.2 mm through the plain jumps; its worst gap, 4 to 7 cm for a moment, is in the recorded sprint (the fourth recording's crossed a dip and a kerb).
- Stamina is the game's to four decimal places, the legs run dry included (the fourteenth recording).
- Recoil was gone over shot by shot (179 shots): the same least and most, means within 1.5%, the climb through automatic fire within 2%.
- The legs are the game's bone for bone (0.2 mm, 0.1 degrees); the arms' solver, given the game's own goals, is within 0.02 degrees and 0.2 mm.
- Vaulting and climbing: three of four traces have the game's state on every frame (vaults within 6 mm, the climb within 3 cm).
- The weapon's animator, run as a state machine beside the weapon's logic in the replay, is the game's on every recorded frame (461,415 of them, the fourth recording to the fourteenth): its state, the state it is entering, whether it is in a transition and how far through the state it is (to four decimals), through every shot, burst, dry fire, reload, switch of fire mode and look at the magazine on record.
