Without the curtain the range and the weapon were missing for the first 1.7 s, and a weapon first drawn later was missing again. So the run begins with the weapon in the picture behind the curtain, and the curtain goes when the renderer has nothing left to build: 1.8 s with DirectX 12 and 0.45 s with `--backend vulkan` on the machine this was measured on. It costs nothing once it is over. Still a moment late the first time they are seen: the upper body from outside (`F4`), a thing put on the ground, and whatever is first worn in a run that began bare.
- Recoil's turn about its pivot is applied in the weapon's own axes; the game's form differs in the second order (see the spec).
- The recoil's random numbers come from a seeded generator (`--seed`), not the engine's.
- The arms are drawn on the weapon rig's own arm bones, which have the body's lengths and which the arms' solver places; the rest of the body is drawn on a skeleton of its own, and the two meet at the collarbones.
- The step-aside keys are the install's bindings (the lean keys with `Left Alt`), as every key is; that the lean's own binding does not then lean is the game's input manager's doing (`docs/spec/settings.md`).
- The sidestep never refuses: the game's check for something in the way or no ground to that side needs collision.
- The character is taken as fully fed and watered throughout, where in the game both fall through a raid and move stamina's refill and drains by a few percent; a replay reads both off the recording.
- What the Endurance and Strength skills do to stamina at their full level (half as much again in the legs and in every refill, three tenths off a jump, a fifth off aiming) are figures written in the game's code, not in the install's data: `crates/trp-sim/src/stamina.rs`.
- Carried weight is the equipment's sum (nothing, for the bare character the app starts with); `--weight` overrides it for the whole run. Skills are taken as untrained.
- How a part is oriented on its bone is a rule read off the data (see `attach_part` in `crates/trp-data/src/weapon.rs`), not yet checked against the game's code.
- `--play <clip>` and `--at` show one clip of the weapon's by its name, in place of the weapon's animator: for looking at a clip, not for play.
