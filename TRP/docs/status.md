**Works**

- **Reading the install:** Unity 2022.3 bundles directly (archives, serialized files, type trees, cross-bundle references, streamed resources); meshes, materials and textures; animation clips; animator controllers in full; SPT's item database; the game's settings object and the player prefab's tuning; the scene files that hold the game's screens. A weapon is put together as the game does, from its base prefab and the parts of its stock preset. The AKM is the first weapon, the Benelli M3 Super 90 the second, the FN Five-seveN and the Remington Model 700 the third and fourth, and five more follow for the kinds those are not (below). The run's own weapon is built at the start and any other when it is first hung on a slot; `--weapon` says which the run begins with, by its id or a part of its name.
- **The body's animator** (`body-animator.md`): the game's 22-layer controller run as the engine runs it, with its states, blends, conditions, blend trees, synchronised and additive layers, root motion and curves. It decides the movement state, supplies the motion and places the ribcage the view hangs from.
- **The body, drawn:** clothing skinned to the animator's skeleton. In first person the legs and the hands are drawn and the upper body and head only cast shadows, as in the game; from outside (`--view body`, the free camera) the whole figure. Worn items (armour, vest, backpack, what goes on the head) by the mesh for what they are worn over.
- **The character as others see it** (`third-person.md`): the simulation's body and weapon motion take a point of view (`trp_sim::PointOfView`), the game's own switch. For eyes other than the character's own: the body breathes and leans into a walk and a sprint's turn; its own weapon bone carries the weapon standing, walking and sprinting alike, the weapon's motion riding on it; the upper arms are the body's and the hands are solved onto the weapon from there; a weapon put away and drawn has the body's own reach for it, begun by a mark of the weapon's clips; in a reload the torso turns to the vest as the left hand's marker nears the weapon's marker for it; the weapon neither sways, breathes nor bobs, a shot kicks it back without turning it, the sights coming up lift it a little by a pose of the prefab's, and it turns away from an obstacle by a second set of the prefab's figures; nothing is squeezed, and the ribcage is where the animation has it. In the app every view from outside is that (`F4`, `F5`, `--view side|body|watch`).
- **The head's free look** (`free-look.md`): the look's key held, the mouse turns the head and not the view (0.125 degrees a count, with no sensitivity after it); the camera takes the head's turn on top of everything else and does not follow the weapon's climb meanwhile; looking down is cut back as the head turns aside; with the sights up the head turns seven tenths as far and the field of view goes back to the hip's while the mouse has the head; the key up, the head is back in half a second. The neck's and the head's bones each take half of the turn, for the shadow in first person and for the figure from outside.
- **Movement** (`movement.md`): walking in eight directions with the speed wheel, the inertia carried weight adds, the sprint with its wind-up and its stop, jumping and landing, falling (which has no state of its own in the game), the continuous stance, the lean, the sidestep, turning on the spot, lying down with the dive, the crawl and the roll, and the view's slower turning in some states. Untrained and unburdened: walk 2.82 m/s, sprint 5.24 m/s, standing jump 0.39 m. The game's quirks are kept (the spec lists them).
- **The character's own collision** (`movement.md`): the game's rules, not the engine's, for a line of ground (the replay) and for boxes (the app's range), and the lock that stops a walk that gets nowhere.
- **Vaulting and climbing** (`vaulting.md`): the jump key held with forward takes the character over a low obstacle or up onto one, by the game's measuring, choice and clips.
- **Stamina** (`stamina.md`): the three pools, every drain and refill, the skills, hunger and thirst as factors, and what running out does. Carried weight can change while running.
- **Weapon motion** (`weapon-handling.md`): the springs, sway, the walk bob, the lean's roll, aiming speed and field of view, breathing with tiredness and a held breath, the weapon carried by the body in a sprint and in the body's hand in a crawl, the turn-away from what the weapon would stick into (with the sights it lowers), and the box that keeps the camera out of the world.
- **Recoil** (`weapon-handling.md`): strength and direction from the weapon, its parts and the round; the climb and return; the ramp of a burst; the kick back; the camera's kick.
- **Firing and reloading** (`firing.md`): single shots, automatic fire, bursts, dry fire, the magazine and the chambered round, reloads, the fire mode, the look at the magazine, being drawn and put away, and what takes the aim away. The weapon's logic is the game's own operations run on the weapon's own animator: which state follows which, how long each lasts and how two are blended is the install's data, and what is drawn is that animator's pose, the selector's and the hammer's layers included. Stock AKM: 603 rounds a minute, a reload in 3.3 s, drawn in 0.96 s, put away in 0.82 s.
