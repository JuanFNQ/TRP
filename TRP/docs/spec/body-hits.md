# Body hits

What a bullet does when it meets a character: the character's hit colliders, what the bullet does at one, the armour it meets, and the damage that reaches health. Read from the game's code on 2026-10-20 (`BodyPartCollider`, `ArmorPlateCollider`, `PlayerBones.SetArmorPlateCollidersState`; `Shot`'s `CreateFragments`, `IsShotDeflectedByHeavyArmor`, `IsRicocheted`, `IsTargetPenetrated`, `ApplyPenetratedDegradation`, `CreateBulletFragments`, `CreateDeviatedFragment`, `AvoidAdditionalDamageWhereCollidersIntersect`, `IsParentHasSameBodyPart`; `BallisticsCalculator.UpdateShots`; `Player`'s `ApplyShot`, `ProceedDamageThroughArmor`, `SetShotStatus`, `TryGetArmorResistData`, `IsShotDeflectedByHeavyArmor`, `IsBleedingBlockedByLightArmor`, `ApplyDamageInfo`, `RecalculateEquipmentParams`; `LocalPlayer.ProceedLocalAbsorbedDamage`; `DamageInfo`'s making from a shot; `ArmorComponent`, `CompositeArmorComponent`, `ShotSharedMethods.RealResistance`, `ArmorResistanceData`; `Inventory.GetPutOnArmorsNonAlloc`, `OrderLockedLast`; `ItemExtensions.GetItemComponentsInChildrenNonAlloc`, `ContainerExtensions.MergesWithChildren`, `SlotSerializer`; `Vest`, `ArmoredEquipment`; `RepairableComponent`; `ActiveHealthController`'s `ApplyDamage`, `BluntContusion`, `DoWoundRelapse`; `SkillManager`'s vest buffs), from the install (the player prefab's hit colliders and their scripts' figures, `probe player-scripts BodyPartCollider` and `ArmorPlateCollider`; `globals.json`'s `BodyPartColliderSettings`, `armor.class`, `ArmorMaterials`, `BluntDamageReduceFromSoftArmorMod`, `SkillsSettings.HeavyVests` and `LightVests`; the items' armour figures and slots), and from .NET's published sort (`List<T>.Sort`, the reference source's `ArraySortHelper`). Set beside the user's recordings of 2026-10-18 (`local\traces\courses3`, recorder 0.11.0: `3-bot-shots`, 16 hits on a Scav; `3-bot-armour`, 29 on Tagilla and 13 on the user's PMC, each with the damage and penetration as it came, the damage as the armour left it, and health before and after) where it says so. The game's `ArmorDummy` (a class of an older rule: `136 − 5000 / (36 + durability %)`, its own chances, a fixed 1 of damage when blocked) is not the characters' path and is not followed.

## The hit colliders

The player prefab carries **27 body colliders** (`BodyPartCollider`, layer 8, triggers) on its bones, each of a **body part** (head 0, chest 1, stomach 2, left arm 3, right arm 4, left leg 5, right leg 6) and a **collider type** (`EBodyPartColliderType`): the head's sphere (`HeadCommon` 0) and capsules over it (`ParietalHead` 12, `BackHead` 13, `Eyes` 15, `Jaw` 16) and two boxes (`Ears` 14); the neck's two spheres (`NeckFront` 17, `NeckBack` 18, of the chest); the chest's boxes (`RibcageUp` 1 on the third spine bone, `SpineTop` 21, the side boxes `RightSideChestUp` 19 and `LeftSideChestUp` 20); the stomach's (`RibcageLow` 26 on the second spine bone, `SpineDown` 22, `Pelvis` 3, `PelvisBack` 23, `RightSideChestDown` 24, `LeftSideChestDown` 25); a capsule on each upper arm, forearm, thigh and calf (`LeftUpperArm` 4, `LeftForearm` 5, `RightUpperArm` 6, `RightForearm` 7, `LeftThigh` 8, `LeftCalf` 9, `RightThigh` 10, `RightCalf` 11). Their shapes and places are the prefab's (read with the ragdoll's: `trp_data::ragdoll`, physics spec).

And **12 plate colliders** (`ArmorPlateCollider`, boxes), each also of a part and a collider type, and of a **plate** (`EArmorPlateCollider`, flags): `Plate_Granit_SAPI_chest` 1 and `Plate_Korund_chest` 64 (chest, over `RibcageUp`), `Plate_Granit_SAPI_back` 2 and `Plate_6B13_back` 128 (chest, over `SpineTop`), and the sides, of the stomach over `RibcageLow`: Granit's left high 4, left low 8, right high 16, right low 32, Korund's left high 256, left low 512, right high 1024, right low 2048. A plate collider is **on only while armour is worn over it**: the plates of every armour piece worn and not worn out (durability not below the smallest float) together (`RecalculateEquipmentParams`, made again when a piece is worn out by a hit).

**Their figures** (`BallisticCollider`'s: penetration level, penetration chance, ricochet chance, fragmentation chance, deviation chance, deviation, and the body's damage share `penetrationDamageMod`): the prefab gives every body collider `20, 0.3, 0, 0, 0.441, 0.056` and 0.1 (the torso's boxes a level of 100 and a share of 0; `SpineTop`, `SpineDown` and `PelvisBack` no deviation at all), and as the character is set up each body collider takes its **level, chance and share from `globals.json`'s `BodyPartColliderSettings`** by its type: the head's five 15, 0.3, 0.57; the neck, upper arms and thighs 10, 0.3, 0.62; the forearms and calves 5, 0.3, 0.67; the torso's twelve 100, 0, 0. A plate collider keeps the prefab's: level 0, chance 1, share 1. Every one of them is made of `Body` (2). Recorded: the forearm's `5, 0.3, 0, 0, 0.441, 0.056`, the upper arm's level 10, the chest plate's `0, 1, 0, 0, 0.441, 0.056`.

## The armour worn

**What is armour**: a vest (a rig) or an armoured thing (a helmet, a face cover, eyewear, a body armour, a plate, a built-in insert) whose template's `MaxDurability` is above 0 and its `armorClass` above 0 (`Vest`, `ArmoredEquipment`); a plate carrier with class 0 is none, and a cap of class 0 none. An armoured thing's armour on a hinge (a face shield, a visor) counts only while it is down (`CompositeArmorComponent`: its toggle on).

**A piece's figures**, its item's: the class (`armorClass`, written as a number in a string), the durability (its own; a new one's the template's `Durability`) and the **template durability** (the template's `MaxDurability`), what it is made of (`MaterialType`: `BodyArmor`, `Helmet`, `GlassVisor`…), its armour material (`ArmorMaterial`, whose `Destructibility` is in `globals.json`'s `ArmorMaterials`: aramid 0.1875, steel 0.525, titan 0.4125, UHMWPE 0.3375, ceramic 0.6, combined 0.375, aluminium 0.45, glass 0.6), its type (`ArmorType`: none, light, heavy), its **blunt throughput** (`BluntThroughput`), its ricochet figures (`RicochetParams` x, y, z) and what it covers: its collider types (`armorColliders`) and plates (`armorPlateColliders`). A piece held in an **armour slot** of a carrier (a slot whose prototype is the plate slot, `64479fdf9731c8fadc0642c1`) covers only the collider types both its own and the slot's lists name, and the plates both masks have; the slot also says whether it is **locked** and whether it lowers a blunt hit (`bluntDamageReduceFromSoftArmor`).

**The list a hit goes through** (`GetPutOnArmorsNonAlloc`): the things in the headwear, face cover, eyewear, tactical vest and armour vest slots, in that order, each with every piece in the slots it merges with, depth first (the thing, then its slots in order: a slot merges when it says so, `_mergeSlotWithChildren`, or, saying nothing, when the thing holding it merges, `MergesWithChildren`); then **sorted**: a piece in an armour slot that is not locked before everything else (`OrderLockedLast`: −1 for those, 1 for the rest), by .NET's own sort: two pieces swapped only if the first sorts after the second; three by swapping the first and second, the first and third, the second and third, each only if the earlier sorts after; four or more by an insertion sort (so the order among equals is kept but for three). Tagilla's (recorded kit): the front plate (Termite, class 6) and the back plate (steel, class 4), unlocked, then the welding mask, then the soft front and back inserts, locked.

**A piece's resistance** to a penetration (`RealResistance`): with `d` its durability over its template durability, in hundreds, the **real resistance** `(121 − 5000 / (45 + 2d)) × class resistance / 100` (the class's resistance from `globals.json`'s `armor.class`: 10 a class, class 0 one), and its **factor** `clamp(penetration / (real + 12), 0.6, 1)`. Its **chance of being gone through** (percent; `GetPenetrationChance`): nought where the real resistance is the penetration plus 15 or more; `0.4 × (real − penetration − 15)²` where it is the penetration or more; 100 where it is the penetration less 15 or less; else `100 + penetration / (0.9 × real − penetration)`.

## A bullet at a body collider

These come into the ballistics spec's "What a hit does" (its steps' order), for a body or plate collider of a character. The draws are the number at the bullet's seed (ballistics spec, "The random numbers"), one and the same for every step and every piece.

1. **Deflected by heavy armour** (`IsShotDeflectedByHeavyArmor`): the angle between the way back and the face's normal between 42.5° and 80°, fewer than two ricochets before, and the wearer has the Heavy Vests skill's elite buff: a heavy piece covering the collider (a plate collider by its plate, else by its type), not below half its current greatest durability nor below 0.2 of its template's (`RicochetChanceHVestsCurrentDurabilityThreshold`, `…MaxDurabilityThreshold`), with the number at the seed below `RicochetChanceHVestsEliteLevel` (0.05): a ricochet straight off the face, as the ballistics spec says.
2. **A ricochet** (`IsRicocheted`, a forward hit): the collider "deflects" the bullet when, going through the list, a covering piece **deflects** it: its ricochet x above nought, the angle above its z, and the number at the seed below `lerp(x, y, inverse_lerp(90, z, angle))`: the bullet is then **deflected by** that piece, and the going through stops. A piece covering it that does not deflect, the bullet not yet blocked: if its durability is above nought and its chance of being gone through at the bullet's penetration, less 100 times the number at the seed, is below nought, the bullet is **blocked by** it (`SetShotStatus`). Deflected and fewer than two ricochets before: the ricochet as the ballistics spec makes it (the collider's own ricochet chance nought, so its damage and penetration the hit's times `min(1, the bullet's chance) × 0.5`).
3. **Through** (`IsTargetPenetrated`): a backward hit on any body or plate collider, always; a forward one when not blocked and the bullet's penetration, times the factor of the first covering piece where one covers it, is above the collider's level.
4. **The bullets that follow** (fragments, or the bullet on, turned: ballistics spec) take the collider's share, and on a forward hit each is then **worn by the armour** (`ApplyPenetratedDegradation`): the first covering piece's factor at the new bullet's penetration, on its damage and penetration.
5. **Not twice** (`AvoidAdditionalDamage`): a bullet whose hit is on a character's collider, of a shot line where an earlier bullet hit the same character and an earlier bullet's forward hit was on the same part (a plate collider only the same plate of the same part; a plate and a body collider never the same), does no damage: it is hit and flies on as any other.

**When the hit is delivered** (`UpdateShots`): right after its bullet's step, before the bullets it made fly; so the armour's wear from one hit is there for the next.

## The damage

**Only a forward hit, and only on the living** (`BodyPartCollider.ApplyHit`, `Player.ApplyShot`). The hit's figures (`DamageInfo` made from the bullet): its damage (nought for a bullet that does not hit twice), penetration, armour damage share, by whom it was deflected or blocked, and the round's stamina burn and bleedings' deltas (`StaminaBurnRate`, `HeavyBleedingDelta`, `LightBleedingDelta`).

**Through the armour** (`ProceedDamageThroughArmor`): for each piece of the list covering the collider, in order: where a piece before it blocked or deflected the bullet, the damage times its blunt throughput (a heavy piece's times one less the Heavy Vests' blunt reduction); else it **takes the hit**, and is then marked as the one that blocked or deflected it if it was. A piece taking the hit (`ArmorComponent.ApplyDamage`): only for a weapon's damage or a grenade's fragment; a glass visor shatters (not built); a worn-out piece (durability nought or less) does nothing; a bullet deflected by any piece has its damage, armour damage share and penetration halved first. Its **blunt** share is its throughput (a heavy piece's times one less the Heavy Vests' reduction), times `BluntDamageReduceFromSoftArmorMod` (0.6) where its armour slot says so; its resistance is taken at its durability before the hit and the hit's penetration `p`:

- **gone through** (neither blocked nor deflected by this piece): on a plate collider the damage made nought (the bullet that goes on brings it to the body collider behind); the damage and the penetration times the factor; its **wear** `p_after × armour damage share × clamp(p / class resistance, 0.5, 0.9) × destructibility`; a light piece's against a blow cut by the Light Vests' reduction;
- **blocked or deflected by it**: its wear `p × armour damage share × clamp(p / class resistance, 0.6, 1.1) × destructibility`; the damage times `blunt × clamp(1 − 0.03 × (real resistance − p), 0.2, 1)`; the stamina burn times `3 / √blunt` (blunt above nought).

The wear is at least 1, taken off the durability, never below nought. (A repair buff's damage reduction: not built, no buffs.) An **invincible balaclava** worn (its template, `ItemTemplateIds.InvincibleBalaclava`) makes any damage nought.

**What the hit was** (`PlayerHitInfo`): its material `HelmetRicochet` if deflected, else the material of the first piece that took it, else `Body`; **gone through** unless blocked, or if deflected. The armour's share (`DidArmorDamage`, written by the local player): the damage before less after.

**To health** (`ApplyDamageInfo`): a wound's relapse by the damage (health spec); the bleeding **blocked** where the wearer has the Light Vests' elite buff and a light piece not worn out covers the collider type; the damage to the part with the round's deltas on the bleedings' odds (health spec, "Damage": over-damage, pain, bleeding, fracture); a **blunt contusion** is asked for with nought absorbed, so a shot never makes one (`BluntContusion` wants 10 on the head: SPT's `LocalPlayer`, player and bots alike, does not change `ApplyShot`). A weapon's own side effects (`SideEffectComponent`), the hit's push on the speed and stamina (`ApplyHitDebuff`), the voice and the body's flinch (`ShotReactions`): not built (the dummy stands).

**The skills** (`SkillManager`): Heavy Vests' blunt reduction 0.004 a level (`BluntThroughputDamageHVestsReducePerLevel`), its deflection the elite buff; Light Vests' blow reduction 0.006 a level, its bleeding block the elite buff. All nought untrained (each rule held at 50, elite above it, since 0.103.0; before, a level of 51 counted as 51). The dummies are untrained; the player's own levels (the options' SKILLS page) have nothing to act on here, nothing hitting the player's armour.

## What a body's hit shows

(`EffectsCommutator.PlayHitEffect`, read 2026-10-20; the rest as the ballistics spec's "What a hit shows and sounds".) A hit a living character took (its forward hits: `ApplyShot` gives back what the hit was) plays the effect of **the material the hit was** (`Body`, the first piece's `BodyArmor` or `Helmet`, `HelmetRicochet` when deflected) from the character's point of view; one that went through then looks for a wall behind (a ray from the hit point along the bullet's way, 5 m at most, `DRAW_BLOOD_ON_WALLS_MAX_DISTANCE`) and puts blood on it if it is a ballistic thing that is not a body (`EmitBloodOnEnvironment`; not built), and one blocked and marked silent plays no sound. Any other hit on a body (from behind, or on the dead) plays its collider's own material, `Body`, and a ricochet off it its whine as off anything. The body's entry (the effects' list): blood drops (`Legacy Shaders/Particles/Multiply (Double)`), a big puff of blood (`Alpha Blended`), a splash (`Legacy Shaders/Particles/Multiply`), a small puff (`Particles/Smoke Distorted`), no decal, the `body_flesh` bank; armour's: a white dust splash, a small spray and sparks, `armor_hit`; a helmet's and a ricochet off one: sparks, a puff, the pool's light, `helmet_hit` and `helmet_ricochet`. **The two blood shaders** (disassembled from the install, `probe shaders`): `Multiply` blends the frame by the colour it writes (`Blend Zero SrcColor`), `1 + a × (colour × picture − 1)` with `a` the picture's alpha times the particle's; `Multiply (Double)` by twice it (`Blend DstColor SrcColor`), `0.5 + a × (2 × colour × picture − 0.5)` on the colour (its alpha not doubled); their soft variants fade `a` by the depth behind as the others do. A bleeding character leaves drops on the ground under it every so often (`UpdatePlayersBleedings`, `EmitBleeding`, within 10 m below; not built).

**No flinch** (read 2026-10-26: `Player.ShotReactions`, `Player.Create`, `RootMotion.FinalIK.HitReaction`, `OffsetModifier.ModifyOffset`, `ObservedPlayerView`): a character hit from the third person is meant to give FinalIK's `HitReaction` the hit (its full-body IK's effectors pushed along the hit's way by curves, for its part or collider), and an infected one on the simplified skeleton its body animator's `Hit` trigger instead. But `Player.Create` makes every character with its `HitReaction` switched off (`enabled = false`), and an offset modifier switched off adds nothing; the only code that switches it on is `ObservedPlayerView`, the online game's view of another player. A bot of the single-player game is a `Player`: **it does not flinch**. The recordings agree (`local\flinch_still_s138.ps1`, `flinch_series_s138.ps1`, the bones as drawn, written as the camera draws: the recorder's `OnPreCull`): Tagilla in `3-bot-armour` took a burst of seven hits standing (`Idle`, frames 3614 to 3663), his ribcage turning against his pelvis 0.1 to 0.3 degrees a frame with his look and no more after a hit than before; every hit in `3-bot-shots` was answered by the bot's own mind (a run begun, the look turned and kept) within 0.05 s, never by a swing that comes back. So nothing is built (the user, 2026-10-26, by multiple choice: "Keep it as SPT").

**The shooter's hit sound** is the hit's own effect's sound at the hit point (above; the ballistics spec): `ArmorHitSoundPlayer`, a sound of an armour hit for the shooter, waits on `BulletSoundsUtils.HitArmor`, which nothing in the game calls. Recorded (`3-bot-shots`): the bots' hits played `body_flesh`'s `body1` to `body6` with a `_distant` second clip, at 0.76 to 0.79 of their bank, and `bodyarmor2_close` to `bodyarmor4_close` at 0.8; the app's on the dummy (`--start dummy --yaw -2 --pitch 6`, two shots): `body_flesh` at 0.777 and 0.754, 3.1 m off.
