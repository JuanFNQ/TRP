# The kinds of weapon the first four are not

Tarkov's weapons counted, and one weapon built of each kind the AKM, the M3 Super 90, the Five-seveN and the Model 700 do not cover. Written from a reading of the game (0.16.9.5, SPT 4.1.6 assembly) on 2026-10-13, from each weapon's own animator (`probe states <id>`) and its item in the database. **No recording has any of these weapons: everything here is from the code and the install, and what is "seen" below is this simulation on the install's animators.** Class and field names are the game's. `firing.md`, `shotgun.md`, `pistol.md` and `bolt-action.md` hold for these too; this is what is added.

## Every weapon, counted

`probe weapons` reads every weapon item of the database: **163 items, 149 with a stock preset** (the StG 44 among them), in 97 different sets of clips and 91 different sets of states. Which logic a weapon runs on goes by three words of its template (`ReloadMode`, `BoltAction`, the item's class) and nothing else: **128 of the install's 148 run on the four logics already built**, and the other twenty are five kinds:

| a self-loader filled through its action | 4 | TOZ SKS | `574d967124597745970e7c94` |
| a revolver | 5 (the MTs-255 shotgun is one) | Chiappa Rhino 50DS | `61a4c8884f95bc3b2c5dc96f` |

The barrels' five are the two MP-43s, and three with one barrel: the MP-18, the FN40GL and the RShG-2, **whose reload is another operation, not built** (and the rocket launcher has a firing of its own). **Every one of the 149 run whole** (2026-10-14, after the AS VAL crashed the app: every weapon was checked). `cargo test -p trp-sim --test sim every_weapon::` assembles each weapon the crates hold, finds its skinned parts' bones in its own tree, and runs its logic on its own animator: found, drawn, aimed and fired, reloaded both ways (a magazine, and the quick reload; loose rounds where the magazine stays in), and put away, each time to rest; all 149 pass. In the app, each was the run's weapon, fired, reloaded, aimed and captured (`local\every_weapon_app_s3g.ps1`): all 149 ran to the end with no error. Only the AS VAL and the VSS have a part drawn on bones of their own, their hammer spring, which had stopped the renderer.

## Two that needed nothing new

**The SV-98** is the Model 700's firing on the AKM's reload: the bolt is worked when the trigger is let go (`bolt-action.md`), and the magazine is changed (`firing.md`). Its template says the bolt has to be opened for a reload (`MustBoltBeOpennedForExternalReload`), which the logic already answered. Seen: found and drawn, `OUT TO IDLE ARM` (2.02 s, nine and one); a shot, `FIRE`, then `FIRE RELOAD` on the release; reloaded with a round chambered, `RELOAD START`, `OUT`, `IN`, `END` (3.06 s, ten and one); from empty, `OPEN CHAMBER` first and `RELOAD CLOSE CHAMBER` last (5.00 s, nine and one).

**The SKS** fires as any self-loader and is filled as the Model 700 is: with the bolt open (`MustBoltBeOpennedForInternalReload`), a round at a time, **the chambered round thrown out first** (`ReloadInternalMagWithOpenBoltOperation.RemoveAmmoFromChamber`). Seen: `RELOAD START INTERNAL`, `RELOAD IN INTERNAL`, `RELOAD END INTERNAL`; eight and one filled to nine and one in 4.61 s (two rounds in, the chambered one on the ground).

## A cylinder

**There is no chamber apart.** The cylinder is the weapon's magazine (`CylinderMagazine`), and each of its chambers is a slot of it (`camora_000` onward) that holds a round, the case of one fired, or nothing. The weapon keeps which chamber is under the hammer (`CurrentCamoraIndex`) and whether the hammer is down (`Weapon.CylinderHammerClosed`).

- **Which chamber fires** (`GetCamoraFireOrLoadStartIndex`): with the hammer cocked, the one under it; with the hammer down, the next round the cylinder, since the trigger turns it before the hammer falls.
- **A shot** (`FireCylinderMagOperation.PrepareShot`): the round is taken out of its chamber and its case stays there; **nothing is thrown out**. The cylinder's index goes on by one, except for the first shot of double action from a cocked hammer. The animator is told the index (`CamoraIndex`, `CamoraFireIndex`), and from a hammer that is down the state `DOUBLE_ACTION_FIRE` is played outright.
- **After the shot** (the mark `AddAmmoInChamber`): the hammer is left down in double action and cocked again in single action (`DoubleActionFireModeFloat`). Single action shows as `FIRE`, `FIRE ARM`, `IDLE`.
- **A press on a chamber with no round** is a dry press: the cylinder turns all the same where the action turns it (double action with the hammer down, single action with it cocked), and the hammer is left as the mode leaves it.
- **Switching to single action** cocks the hammer (`SetFireModeForCylinderMagazine`): the cylinder turns on if it was down, and the animator's `HammerArmed` is set off.

**The reload** (`ReloadCylinderMagOperation`): at its start the free chambers are listed from the one in turn round, and those holding cases; the animator is told how many cases there are (`ShellsInWeapon`, `AmmoCountForRemove`), which comes out first (`CamoraIndexWithShellForRemove`) and which chamber is loaded first (`CamoraIndexForLoadAmmo`). Then by the marks:

| Mark | What it does |
|---|---|
| `RemoveShell` | one case is out, and the next is named; **with no round left in the cylinder every case goes at once** |
| `AddAmmoInChamber` | a round shows in the hand for the next free chamber, if one is in reach |
| `AddAmmoInMag` | it is in; another follows, or `CanReload` is taken away: no chamber left, no round in reach, or the trigger pressed meanwhile |
| `MagHide` | the cylinder is shut: the rounds shown are the weapon's from now, and with the hammer cocked the first loaded chamber comes under it |

Seen on the Rhino: two cases out one by one and two rounds in, `RELOAD START`, `REMOVE SHELLS`, `RELOAD IN`, `RELOAD END` (7.54 s); from empty `RELOAD START ALL`, `RELOAD IN`, `RELOAD END` (10.11 s, six rounds).

**A drum a spring turns** (`SpringDrivenCylinder`, the M32A1's) differs in three things: its index stops at the last chamber where a revolver's goes round; a dry press does not turn it; and a reload begins again from the first chamber. Seen: six shots chamber after chamber, a seventh press dry, and from empty `RELOAD START ALL`, `RELOAD ARM 5`, `RELOAD IN`, `RELOAD END` (13.32 s).

**Turned by the chamber's key** (Ctrl and R; built 0.208.0): a revolver's cylinder turns on by one chamber at rest (`RollCylinderOperation`: the Rhino's `RELOAD START`, `ROLL CYLINDER`, turned at its `MagHide`), and barrels give up their first loaded barrel's round to the vest or the pockets (firing spec, "The magazine's and the chamber's keys").

## Barrels loaded one by one

The weapon has a chamber for each barrel and no magazine. A press fires **the first barrel that holds a round**, and in the mode `doublet` every loaded barrel together (`FireOnlyBarrelFireOperation`); the cases stay in the barrels. Seen: `FIRE`, `IDLE` (0.28 s), two shots from the one press in `doublet`.

**The reload** (`ReloadMultiBarrelOperation`) puts a round into every barrel that holds none, as far as the rounds in reach go. The animator is told which barrel (`ChamberIndex`: the barrel's number, or with every barrel loaded at once their count), how many cases there are (`ShellsInWeapon`) and how many rounds (`AmmoInChamber`). **That count is the rounds once loaded**: the game's inventory puts the new rounds into the barrels before the hands begin (`ReloadMultiBarrelResult.Run`), and the weapon is counted after it. It is this that takes an empty weapon out of its resting state at all: the MP-43's animator leaves `IDLE` for a reload with a round "chambered" or a case in a barrel, and its third way out, for a weapon with neither, asks for counts below zero and can never be taken. Then the marks: `RemoveShell` takes every case out, `AddAmmoInChamber` has the rounds in, `AddAmmoInMag` ends it.

Seen: empty and loaded with two, `RELOAD START`, `RELOAD IN`, `RELOAD END` (4.51 s); with cases in the barrels `RELOAD THROW` comes before `RELOAD IN` (5.78 s).

## Found, and what it is filled from

- **Found in a container** (`Firearm::found`; no round in the chamber): a magazine, a tube or a cylinder is full and the chamber empty, so the SV-98 and the SKS work their action as they are first drawn. **Barrels are chambers and nothing else: they are empty**, and the MP-43 has to be loaded. A cylinder has no chamber apart, and is found full.
- **The loose rounds in reach** are those the weapon's chamber takes (`ReloadWithAmmo`), the first of its barrels (`ReloadBarrels`), or for a revolver its cylinder (`ReloadRevolverDrum`, by `CylinderMagazine.CheckCompatibility`): `trp_sim::inventory::rounds_in_reach`.

## Built (2026-10-13)

- `chambers`: the cylinder and the barrels, each chamber `Empty`, `Round` or `Shell`; the two firings and the two reloads above. `firearm` hands them the trigger, the reload key, the fire mode and the marks, and has `found` and `carried`. `Firearm::magazine` counts the rounds in all the chambers, and `chamber` is never set.
- `trp_sim::firearm_settings`: `cylinder` (the chambers of the magazine fitted, and whether a spring turns it) and `barrels` from the database; the modes `doubleaction` and `doublet`.
- `kinds`: the five, each on the install's own animator (the figures above).
- The app: the five lie in the crate with two stacks of their rounds each (`WEAPONS`), `--weapon <id or part of a name>` begins with one in hand. Each drawn, fired and reloaded in a capture; with `B` pressed first, the Rhino's second shot plays `DOUBLE_ACTION_FIRE` and one press empties both of the MP-43's barrels.

## One barrel loaded alone, and the rocket launcher (read 2026-10-14, to make every weapon selectable)

Read from `ReloadSingleBarrelOperation`, `ReloadSingleBarrelResult.Run`, the idling operation's `ReloadBarrels`, `FirearmHandsInputTranslator.ReloadBarrels`, `RocketLauncherFire`, `FirearmController.IsAiming` and `SetAimingInAnimator`, `FirearmsAnimator.SetAiming`, `SetAimingIn` and `SetAimingOut`, `Idling.ProcessRemoveOneOffWeapon`, and from the install's controllers of the MP-18 (`61f7c9e189e6fb1a5e3ea78d`), the FN40GL (`5e81ebcd8e146c7080625e15`) and the RShG-2 (`676bf44c5539167c3603e869`). With these three, **every weapon of the install that has a stock preset runs** (148, and the StG 44).

**One barrel** (`ReloadMode` `OnlyBarrel` with a single chamber: the MP-18 and the FN40GL) fires as the MP-43 does (`FireOnlyBarrelFireOperation`, "Barrels loaded one by one"). Its reload is another operation, chosen by the barrels' count (`ReloadBarrels`):

- **The key** takes the rounds within reach that the chamber takes, and with none does nothing. Unlike the MP-43's, it is taken **whatever the barrel holds**: empty, a case, or a live round.
- **The exchange is made as it begins** (`ReloadSingleBarrelResult.Run`): a live round in the barrel goes to the first free place in the vest's and the pockets' grids, the smallest grid first, or with none is taken out to be thrown down; the new round goes into the barrel.
- **The animator is told** (`Start`) how many cases there are (`ShellsInWeapon`), whether there was a live round (`AmmoInChamber` 1, else 0), `CanReload`, and `Reload`. The MP-18's `IDLE` leaves for `RELOAD START EMPTY` with neither, else for `RELOAD START`, which goes on to `RELOAD THROW` for a case or `RELOAD OUT` for a round, then `RELOAD IN` and `RELOAD END`; the FN40GL's has a start for each of the three.
- **The marks**: `DelAmmoChamber` (`AmmoInChamber` as the barrel stands); `RemoveShell` (the cases are out); `ShellEject` (`CanReload` again; the live round thrown down where it found no place; `AmmoInChamber` 0); `MagShow` (the round in the hand); `AddAmmoInChamber` (`AmmoInChamber` as the barrel stands, `CanReload` down, **and the reload is over**: the weapon idles from there, where the MP-43's goes on to `AddAmmoInMag`).

**The rocket launcher** (the RShG-2, of the class `RocketLauncher`; `IsOneoff` is false in this install, and it is `OnlyBarrel` with one chamber, its rocket):

- **Its own firing** (`RocketLauncherFire`, chosen for a `RocketLauncher` before every other test): the trigger starts it; the animator's `Fire` follows the trigger; the sights are put down as it starts; at `FiringBullet` the rocket leaves and the chamber is empty; at `FireEnd` the trigger is let go and the weapon idles. Nothing cuts it short.
- **It fires from the sights only.** Its `IDLE` leaves for `FIRE` (and, further down its list, `QUICK FIRE`) only with `IsAimingFloat` over 0.9, the trigger and a rocket in it; `AIM OFF`, taken on `AimingOut`, comes after `FIRE` in that list, so the sights coming down as the firing starts do not stop it.
- **The sights and the animator**: whenever the sights change (`IsAiming`), the game raises `AimingIn` and lowers `AimingOut` with them up, the other way with them down, and sets `PrevIsAimingFloatState` to 0 or 1 the other way about (`SetAimingInAnimator`); for any weapon but a rocket launcher it also sets `IsAimingFloat` to the sights (`FirearmsAnimator.SetAiming`). The launcher's `IsAimingFloat` is the curve its clips `AIM ON` and `AIM OFF` carry. None of the other weapons built has any of these four in its controller's ways or blends.
- **Its controller has no state to reload in**, and nothing in the game's code plays its `FIRE TO OUT`, whose mark `DropWeapon` would throw it away (a used weapon is thrown away by the idling operation only if it is `IsOneOff`, which this one is not). Fired, it stays in the hands empty. The reload key would start the single barrel's operation, which its animator would never answer: the app gives it no rockets to reload with.

### Built (2026-10-14)

- `trp_sim::firearm::chambers`: the single barrel's reload (the barrel replaced whatever it holds; `Event::RoundUnloaded` for a live round taken out, at `ShellEject`); the rocket launcher's firing (`FirearmSettings::rocket_launcher`); the four parameters of the sights told to the animator on every change, for every weapon (`Firearm::step`).
- The app: the round taken out goes to the vest or the pockets where it has room, else to the ground; **the rocket launcher is found with its rocket** (`Firearm::found`, the app's choice as a full magazine is: it cannot be loaded), and its crate holds no rockets; every weapon with a stock preset lies in the crates of weapons.

### Not built, and open

- The rocket and the launcher's grenade as things fired (the M32A1's likewise), and their sights' ranges (the `Levering` layer's `SightingRange`).
- **Compared with nothing**: no recording has any of the three. The figures are the install's controllers run by the simulation.

## A weapon with no chamber (read and built 2026-10-24)

Nine weapons list no chamber (`Chambers` empty) and take a detachable magazine: the PPSh-41, the RPD and RPDN, the M60E4, the M60E6 and its FDE, the Uzi, the AA-12 Gen 1 and Gen 2. The game's `Weapon.HasChambers` is false for them, and every step that would touch a chamber is skipped (`FirearmSettings::no_chamber`; the five revolvers' and launchers' cylinders and the RSP-30's barrels are other cases). None of the nine's hands animators has an `AmmoInChamber` at all, and all nine have every malfunction switched off.

- **The trigger** fires while the magazine holds a round (`Idling.SetTriggerPressed`: a chamber's round, or with none, the magazine's count above nought); else the dry click. **The round fired comes straight off the magazine** at the shot (`GetAmmoForSingleShot`, `ProcessInventoryOperationsForSingleShot`: popped to nowhere), and nothing waits in a chamber: the weapon holds its magazine's rounds and no more. The animator is told the magazine's count and never a chamber's.
- **Held on**, automatic fire goes on while the magazine holds a round after the shot (`InternalOnFireEndEvent`); with it empty the run ends and the trigger, still held, clicks once (no bolt catch on any of the nine). A burst goes on only while a chamber holds a round, so it would stop after one shot: none of the nine has a burst.
- **A reload** chambers nothing, and so is not over at `MagIn` (that early end is a chambered weapon's): it ends at the animation's rest (`IdleStart`). Putting a magazine in from the inventory likewise chambers nothing, and ends at `MagIn`. The animator chooses its own charging by `Armed` (the M60's `RELOAD START CHARGE` when not armed; `RELOAD CHARGE END` after `RELOAD IN` when the magazine holds rounds and it is not armed).
- **The draw** takes no round. The weapon keeps its own `Armed` (`Weapon.Armed`, set by the clips' `Arm` and `Disarm` marks) and the animator is given it as the weapon is drawn (`SetAnimatorValues`); the M60's draw arms it (`OUT TO IDLE ARM`) where it is not. A weapon carried from the start is armed; one found is not (`PROVISIONAL`: as one with a chamber is found with it empty; the loot's own `Armed` is not read).
- **A round into the chamber from the inventory** is refused (`Idling.Execute`, the input translator's `Rechamber`, `UnloadChamber`, `LoadAmmoToChamber`): the item has no `patron_in_weapon`. Unloading takes the magazine's rounds alone, as for every weapon.
- **The chamber check** is allowed (no chamber test): the M60's plays `CHECK CHAMBER` (its cover opened, the belt looked at, closed: 4.53 s), as the animator has it.
- **The rounds it takes** are its own magazine's cartridges' (`Chambers` gives none): a kit fitted to it keeps rounds its magazine takes.
- **Not built**: the case thrown at the eject's mark (the round moved to the port and thrown there at once; the app puts every case at the port at the shot); a mark whose condition reads a parameter the animator lacks, which the game reads as nought (`RELOAD CHARGE END`'s `AddAmmoInChamber`, `INV TO RELOAD`'s `OnBoltCatch`): such marks are not delivered here (the reload ignores the first in any case; the second would set `BoltCatch` during a magazine moved from the inventory). The recorder writes no `ammo` for these weapons (it reads the first chamber's round): the replay takes the fitted magazine's last stack from the recorded equipment.

**Compared with the running game**: the M60E6 FDE on `courses4`'s seven basics and `courses5`'s course 6 (kept as `r17`, `r18` in the regression). Before: it fired none of the game's 3, 2 and 19 rounds. Now the magazine's count, the hands animator's state, `Armed` and `Fire` the game's on every frame of all ten scripts; the held fire's 19 rounds 0.11 s apart; the recoil's turn within 0.19 degrees rms on three single presses (largest 0.49), 0.08 (0.17) on two aimed, 0.40 (1.60) on the 2 s held aimed run; the camera's 0.04, 0.08 and 0.24. The checks' four seconds of the chamber check, and the selector's look refused meanwhile, the game's state for state. Not compared: a reload (`04-reload` carried no spare box: nothing happened in the game, and the hands' state there is off on 94% of frames, as before), the other eight weapons (on no recording), the draw's arming.

## The rounds and cases seen in chambers of their own, and the magazine check (read 2026-10-30, built 0.150.0)

Some weapons showed no calibre in first person (the double-barrelled shotgun, the MP-18, the revolvers); both are built: the rounds drawn in the barrels and the cylinder, and the words of the magazine check.

**Where they are drawn** (`Firearms.OnCreatePoolObject`, `SetupPatronInWeaponTransform`, `SetupShellPortTransform`, `SetRoundIntoWeapon`, `MoveAmmoFromChamberToShellPort`, `TransformTools.FindAllTransformsWithNameContains`, `FindChildsByNameRecNoneAlloc`). Each chamber has a point in the weapon's model: the objects under the weapon's root whose names have `patron_in_weapon`, on or off, found breadth first and numbered in that order (a barrel's number, a cylinder chamber's). A weapon with more than one point is a weapon of several chambers, and its shell ports are then every object whose name has `shellport`, found depth first without looking inside one found, numbered the same way; a weapon of one point has the first object named `shellport` that is on, as every other weapon (firing spec, "Spent cases"). In the install: the MP-43s have two points and two ports in their barrels (`patron_in_weapon_000` and `_001`, `shellport_000` and `_001`; a plain `shellport` on the weapon comes third, which no barrel's number reaches); the revolvers five or six of each in the cylinder (from `_001`), the M32A1 six; the MP-18 one point and one port. Each port lies where its point lies. A round at a point is the round's own model, its `full` objects on, a quarter turn about x. At the shot it goes to the port of the same number as its case (its `shell` objects on) and stays there until a reload throws it.

**When.** These are the game's own moments, not the logic's (which takes a round out at the press, `PrepareShot`, and counts the rounds in at the reload's end):

- **As the hands are made**: a round at the point of each chamber that holds one (`Chambers`, a cylinder's `Camoras`), and a case at the port of each that holds a fired one's (`ShellsInChambers`).
- **A shot**, at the mark `FiringBullet` (`FireOnlyBarrelFireOperation.OnFireEvent` for barrels, the MP-18's too, its reload mode being `OnlyBarrel`; `FireCylinderMagOperation.OnFireEvent` for a cylinder): the round of each chamber fired goes to its port.
- **The barrels' reload** (`ReloadMultiBarrelOperation`): at `ShellEject` every case is thrown where the weapon held cases as the reload began (`StartSpawnAllShells`, with 0.33 of the player's velocity); at `RemoveShell` any case still at a port is taken away unthrown (`RemoveAllShells`); at `MagShow` a round shows at the point of each chamber being loaded, the reload's rounds in turn (`OnMagAppeared`). The MP-43's `RELOAD THROW` has `ShellEject` at 0.748 of its clip and `RemoveShell` at 0.828, so its cases are thrown.
- **A single barrel's reload** (`ReloadSingleBarrelOperation`): at `ShellEject` a live round in the barrel goes from its point (it is put in the vest or on the ground as a thing, "One barrel loaded alone"); with none, a case is thrown with the whole of the player's velocity (`StartSpawnShell(Player.Velocity)`); at `RemoveShell` any case left is taken away; at `MagShow` the new round shows at the point. The MP-18's `RELOAD THROW`: `ShellEject` at 0.667, `RemoveShell` at 0.677.
- **A cylinder's reload** (`ReloadCylinderMagOperation`): at each `RemoveShell` the case of the chamber named for it (`CamoraIndexWithShellForRemove`) is thrown with 0.33 of the player's velocity (`StartSpawnShell`); **with no live round in the cylinder, every case at once**, given one metre a second straight down in place of the player's velocity (`RemoveAllShells`: `StartSpawnAllShells(Vector3.down)`). At each `AddAmmoInChamber` that loads one, a round shows at the point of the chamber it goes into (`SetCamoraIndexForLoadAmmo`), the reload's rounds in turn (`AmmoPack.GetAmmoToReload`).
- **A thrown case** flies as a one-chamber weapon's (firing spec, "Spent cases": `SpawnExtractingCartridge`): from where its port has carried it, turned as it was there, by the weapon's shot settings; the move in its force is the first port's from where that was as the weapon was made (`_shellPortsTransforms[0]`, `_initialShellPortPosition`), and the forward it flies by is the first port's too.

**The magazine check** (`Idling.CheckAmmo`) asks for the weapon's magazine (`Weapon.GetCurrentMagazine`, its `mod_magazine` slot's). **Barrels have none: the MP-43s and the MP-18 are refused the check**, which neither plays nor names anything (their animators' `CHECK` is the chamber check's). A revolver's magazine is its cylinder: the check names its count as a weapon of folding mechanism does ("Full" only full, "Empty" at none, else the count: inventory spec, "What the ammunition's panel says"), the count being the live rounds in its chambers (`CylinderMagazine.Count`; a case is not one: the shot takes the round out of its chamber, `RemoveAmmoInCamora`, and keeps the case apart, `ShellsInChambers`) over its chambers (`MaxCount`). **The round it names is the one in the chamber that fires next** (`GetFirstAmmo(!CylinderHammerClosed)`, `GetCamoraFireOrLoadStartIndex`: with the hammer cocked the one under it, with it down the next round the cylinder, a drum a spring turns always the one under it), and none where that chamber holds no round.

**Built**: `trp_sim::firearm::Shown` (what the model holds at each chamber's point and port, at the moments above; `Firearm::chambers_shown`), `Event::RoundShown` (a reload's round at a chamber, its place among the reload's rounds) and `Event::CaseThrown` (a chamber's case thrown, with its share of the player's velocity or straight down); the check refused for barrels (`Firearm::check_magazine`). `trp_data::weapon::Weapon::shell_ports` (the ports of a weapon of several chambers). In the app (`MAGAZINE ROUNDS` for the rounds, `SPENT CASES` for the cases, as before): every point of the rig numbered (`magazine_rounds::find_hands_chamber`), a round drawn at each the logic shows (`show_chambered`), a case at each port (`cases::fly_cases`), thrown as a one-chamber weapon's. **The rounds' kinds are the app's** (the logic's chambers have none, and the item's are not kept with them: `ChamberKinds`): a reload's round is the one the reload takes in its turn (the rounds in reach in the order taken, as they were when it began: `trp_sim::inventory::rounds_in_reach_kinds`); one there as the hands are made is the item's own in that chamber, else the magazine's top one, else the weapon's own kind; a case keeps its round's. The live round a single barrel's reload takes out is put in the vest or on the ground as its own kind (it was always the weapon's own). The check's words for a revolver are the logic's count over its chambers and the kind of the round in the chamber in turn (`sight_panels::magazine_words`).

**The item kept with the chambers**. The game's item holds a live round in each loaded chamber, a barrel's (`Chambers`) or a cylinder's (`Camoras`, slots of the cylinder that is the weapon's magazine: `CylinderMagazine.Count` counts them), and nothing of a case; its hands are made from it. Ours now holds them so: **a cylinder's rounds lie in its `camora_` slots**, one a chamber, never as a magazine's loose cartridges (`trp_sim::inventory`: a cylinder filled, counted, its rounds put in, loaded and unloaded there; a loadout's cartridges for one go into its chambers), and the weapon in hand's item and its logic are kept together (`inventory_view::settle`): a weapon newly in hand, or its item changed by the inventory since they last agreed, and the logic takes the item's rounds as its hands come to rest (`Firearm::hold_rounds`: a chamber without one keeps its case); otherwise what the logic does (the round taken out at the press of a shot, the rounds counted in at a reload's end, the round a single barrel's reload takes out) goes into the item, each round of the kind kept for its chamber. So the inventory writes a revolver's count and a double barrel's as they stand (in the game's words for a magazine: `GetAmmoCountByLevel`, not overridden by the cylinder), unloading gives back the rounds there are, and a weapon taken in hand again shows its rounds' own kinds. A reload's rounds no longer go into a revolver's item as loose cartridges besides (they went in, and never out, so its count only grew). `PROVISIONAL`: the order the inventory fills and empties a cylinder in (the first empty chamber, the last loaded one), not read.

**The cases kept with the weapon's item** (read 2026-10-30, built 0.151.0; two weapons of one build share them). **The weapon keeps its cases, apart from its rounds** (`Weapon.ShellsInChambers`: a round's template a chamber, as many as its chambers or its cylinder's, `CylinderMagazine.MaxCount`): a chamber's round lies in its slot (`Chambers`, `Camoras`) and its case on the weapon, so **a chamber may hold both**. The inventory never touches the cases: a round put into a fired chamber while the weapon is put away lies beside the case. Where they are set and cleared:

- **The shot**: a cylinder's case at the press, with the round taken out (`FireCylinderMagOperation.PrepareShot`: `RemoveAmmoInCamora`, then `ShellsInChambers[_camoraIndex]`); barrels' rounds come out of their slots at the press (`SingleShot`) and their cases are the weapon's at `FiringBullet` (`FireOnlyBarrelFireOperation.OnFireEvent`). A case already there is replaced, on the weapon and at the port (`SetPatronInShellPort` returns the old one to the pool).
- **The hands made** (`FirearmController.Setup`): a round at the point of each chamber with one, a case at the port of each the weapon holds, both where a chamber has both.
- **The draw** (`SpawnOperation.Start`, just after): the case of every loaded barrel is taken away, unthrown (`RemoveShellInWeapon`), where the weapon has two barrels or more (`Weapon.IsMultiBarrel`, `Chambers.Length > 1`): **a single barrel keeps its case beside its round**. For a cylinder the loop runs over the chambers numbered below its count of rounds (`i < cylinderMagazine.Count`), not over all of them: a loaded chamber numbered at or past the count keeps its case.
- **The reloads**: the barrels' clears every case at `RemoveShell` (thrown at `ShellEject` first, "When" above); a single barrel's over a live round clears the weapon's case as it begins (`HasOldAmmoInChamber`) and the model's at `RemoveShell`, unthrown (`ShellEject` throws a case only with no live round: `HasShellsInChamberBarrelOnlyWeapon`); a cylinder's clears the case it removes at each `RemoveShell` (`_shellIndexForRemove`), or all of them with no live round left; its free chambers are those without a round (`GetFreeCamorasIndexesFromCurrentActiveIndex`) and its cases those the weapon holds (`GetShellsIndexes`), whether or not a round lies beside them. The hands' load of one round into a cylinder's chamber clears that chamber's case at its `RemoveShell` (`LoadCartridgeToCamora`); the load into one of the weapon's own chambers, a barrel's among them (`LoadCartridgeToChamber`, chosen by the chamber's number), clears every case at its `RemoveShell` (whether the barrels' clips have that mark is not read).

**Built**: `trp_sim::inventory::Item::shells` (`chamber_cases`, `set_chamber_case`; the case's kind); the logic's `Chamber` a round and a case apart; `Firearm::hold_rounds(rounds, cases, made)` (made: the item's as they are, then the draw's clearing, `cases_drawn_away`, which `Firearm::draw` does too; else the inventory's move in hand: a chamber given a round loses its case, unthrown, `PROVISIONAL` until the hands' own operations); `Event::CaseLeft` (a fired round gone to its port: the case's kind is its, `ChamberKinds::case`).
