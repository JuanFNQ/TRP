# Gestures and phrases: the hand signs, the voice's phrases, their menu and keys

The game lets the character make a hand sign (eleven of them: There, Hold, Greet, Flip off, Thumbs up, Thumbs down, Follow me, Rock, Scissors, Paper, OK) or say a phrase in its voice (a command, a reaction, a call of a contact, a mumble...), chosen from a full-screen menu (Y twice), from a list held open by Y, from a prompt said by a tap of Y, or by a function key bound to it. Read 2026-11-06 from the client's code (`GesturesQuickPanel`, `GesturesMenu`, `GesturesDropdownPanel`, `GesturesBindPanel`, `GamePlayerOwner`, `Player.ShowGesture`, `Player.Say`, `MovementContext.SetInteractInHands`, `AnimatedInteractionsModel`, `BaseSpeaker`), every call followed to a caller on the offline player's route (SPT's `LocalPlayer`); the layout, sprites, colours and clips from the install through the probe (the commands at the end). Nothing here was compared with a recording. TRP's choices: all three pieces built; F1 to F12 all the binds' (the app's free camera and watched view moved to the debug panel, whose own key moved from F1 to `` ` ``); all eleven hand signs shown (the game shows the profile's, seven in this install); the binds made in the in-game menu alone (the options' copy, built and taken out the same day).

## The keys

Three bindings share Y in the install (`Control.ini`): `Mumble` pressed twice (`DoubleClick`), `MumbleDropdown` held (`Continuous`), `MumbleQuick` let go (`Release`). The input manager decides between them (settings spec, "How a key becomes a command"): the double press takes Y first, and the other two wait for it to fail (0.3 s, `DoubleClickTimeout`). So, read from that model and not recorded: **two taps** open the menu, and two more shut it with whatever is under the pointer chosen (bound `DoubleClick`, the binding gives its command on the second release and never its end); **a hold** opens the list 0.2 s after its command; **a tap** says the prompt's phrase, about 0.3 s after Y went down.

**The function keys are the game's own** (`ControlSettingsGroup.ReservedKeys`, joined after the player's bindings and hidden from the settings): `F1` to `F12` each given on its release, `DoubleF1` to `DoubleF12` on a double press of the same key. A plain key says or shows what is bound to it; the double press does the same **aggressively** (below). Nothing happens on a key with nothing bound, and none of them works while the menu is open.

## The menu (`GesturesMenu`)

**Opened** (two taps of Y, `GesturesQuickPanel.ToggleBindPanel`): it appears whole on that frame (nothing fades or moves): every group, every hand sign, the bind panel; the world is blurred as the inventory blurs it (rising over 0.35 s; falling as long when it shuts), the panel of actions on what is looked at is hidden, and the situational phrases (below) are checked afresh. With the game's tactical mode set to hold, a lit light on the weapon goes off (`ReleaseTactical`).

**While open** it takes every command but five (`MakeScreenshot`, `ShowConsole`, `ToggleInventory`, `ToggleTalk`, `StopTalk`) and the menu's own key, so nothing the character does is heard (no firing, no function keys, no quick phrase), sets the axes to nothing (no walking, no look, no slow lean), and frees the cursor. A key held as it opened is not let go of by it.

**Shut** by: a left click on a hand sign, a phrase or a bound key's slot (that thing chosen, then played); a **middle click** on a hand sign or a phrase (chosen aggressively); the menu's key again (whatever hand sign or phrase is under the pointer chosen, else whatever slot is, else nothing); Escape (nothing chosen; the interface's `MenuEscape` sound, the menu's only sound). Shutting shows the action panel again at once and lets the blur fall. A right click on a hand sign or a phrase that is not situational opens the binding menu (below) and leaves the menu open.

**The hand signs**: one hexagon each, in the order of their numbers (`EInteraction`: There 1 to Follow me 7, Rock 200, Scissors 201, Paper 202, OK 203), each with its icon (the install's `static icons`, `GestureSprites`: `gesture_that_way`, `gesture_stop`, `gesture_hi`, `gesture_fuckoff`, `gesture_ok`, `gesture_bad`, `gesture_follow`, `gesture_PlayStone`, `gesture_PlayScissors`, `gesture_PlayPaper`, `gesture_Okay`) kept to its shape in a 56 by 53 box, and its name below or beside it (the locale's word for the enum's name, upper case: THERE, HOLD, GREET, FLIP OFF, THUMBS UP, THUMBS DOWN, FOLLOW ME, ROCK, SCISSORS, PAPER, OK). The game shows the profile's unlocked ones (`CustomizationSolver.GetAvailableGestures`; this install's storage gives seven by default and the profile unlocks none); the app shows all eleven (by choice). None is ever greyed.

**The phrases**, in nine groups in this order, each phrase by its `EPhraseTrigger` name's words (upper case):

| Group | Kind | Phrases, in order |
|---|---|---|
| COMMAND | common | FIRE, HOLD FIRE, SUPPRESS, SPREADOUT, TAKE COVER, KNIVES ONLY, REGROUP, HOLD POSITION, Attention! (`Look`), GO GO GO, GO FORWARD, GET BACK, FOLLOW ME, COVER ME, GET THE LOOT, STOP, SILENCE, ON YOUR OWN |
| REACTION | common | ON POSITION, ON LOOT, GOOD WORK, ROGER, REPEAT, READY, GOING, NEGATIVE, BAD WORK, COVERING, CLEAR, DON'T KNOW |
| HEALTH STATUS | common | DEHYDRATED, EXHAUSTED, the four hurts (HURT), BLEEDING, BROKEN LEG, BROKEN HAND, all situational |
| CONTACT | common | RIGHT FLANK, IN THE FRONT, ON SIX, SNIPER, Scav, LEFT FLANK |
| ENEMY | common | ENEMY HIT, KNIFE KILL, NOISE, LOST VISUAL, SCAV DOWN, ENEMY DOWN |
| HELP | common | NEED FRAG, NEED SNIPER, NEED AMMO, NEED HELP, NEED WEAPON, NEED MEDKIT |
| TEAM STATUS | common | DOWN, HIT, A RAT!, FRIENDLY FIRE |
| (mumble) | situational, always open, unnamed | MUMBLE |
| (situational) | situational, always open, unnamed | LOOT KEY, LOOT MONEY, LOOT WEAPON, LOOT, LOOT BODY, LOOT THIS, LOCKED DOOR, CHECK HIM, OPEN |

A phrase is listed only if the voice has a bank for it (or it is the mumble); a group with none of its phrases in the voice is drawn at 0.3 of its alpha and takes no clicks. **A common group shows only its name until the pointer is over it** (its whole 330 by 240 box), and then its glow and its phrases too; the two situational groups are always open. **A situational phrase** (one the prompt has a priority for, but NEED AMMO) is shown only while its situation holds, with a white "!" beside it; it cannot be bound, and a click on it says it only while its situation holds.

**Under the pointer**: a hexagon's ground `small_group_back` becomes `small_group_back_selected`; a phrase's `subgroup_back` becomes `subgroup_back_selected2` and its words go from (0.714, 0.757, 0.780) to black; a slot's ground and words swap to their kind's other pair; a group shows its glow (`group_selected`) and phrases.

**The bind panel**: twelve slots, two columns of six, F1 at the top of the right column and F2 of the left (a key whose `ECommand` number is even goes right, odd left), each with the key's name on a tab between the columns and the name of what is bound (the hand sign's or the phrase's words; F1 says MUMBLE until changed). A slot holding a hand sign is gold (0.694, 0.624, 0.404 at 0.784) with light words (0.839, 0.890, 0.914), any other black with (0.714, 0.757, 0.780); a slot empty, or holding a situational phrase not active, or a phrase the voice lacks, is at 0.3 of its alpha and takes no clicks (checked as the menu opens). A click on a slot plays what it holds. (Read only: the game counts its four later hand signs, 200 to 203, as phrases in a slot, so bound they would show black and greyed; the app shows them as hand signs, since it shows them at all.)

**Binding** (`GesturesMenu.ShowContextMenu`, `Bind`): a right click opens the game's ordinary context menu at the pointer with twelve entries, one a key, each naming what that key holds now, or, if it holds nothing, the key ("F7"), with the key on its tab. Choosing one binds: the thing leaves any key that had it, the key's old thing is dropped, and the whole set is kept at once. The game keeps it in the registry (PlayerPrefs, `BindCommands`); nothing being kept there on this machine, the game's default holds: **F1 says the mumble**. The app keeps its binds in its own options file.

## The quick prompt and the hold-Y list (`GesturesQuickPanel`, `GesturesDropdownPanel`)

**A situational phrase is active** while its situation holds, and only if the voice has it: by **the whole body's health** in percent (every change of health): HURT NEAR DEATH from 0 to 15, HURT HEAVY over 15 to 40, HURT MEDIUM over 40 to 70, HURT LIGHT over 70 and under 90; by **the effects** (as one starts or turns residual): BLEEDING for any bleeding, BROKEN LEG for a fracture of either leg, BROKEN HAND of either arm, DEHYDRATED, EXHAUSTED; by **what is looked at**: a loose key LOOT KEY, money LOOT MONEY, a weapon or a knife LOOT WEAPON, any other loose thing LOOT, a body LOOT BODY and CHECK HIM, a container LOOT THIS, a door OPEN, and LOCKED DOOR as well if it is locked or shut, another player COOPERATION; in an exit's zone EXIT LOCATED; and for **5 s after a situation** the game raises: the character's own unasked phrase (a reload's, a grenade's), a reload asked with no magazine to find (NEED AMMO), a weapon's known malfunction (JAMMED for a slide whose kind is known, else WEAPON BROKEN: at the shot and again 150 ms on, at every reminder, and when a weapon with one is drawn; not at the look at its kind: `malfunctions.md`, "What the shooter is told"). Each has a priority (`PhrasePriorities`): an enemy's grenade 100; a grenade, a friend down, a reload, NEED AMMO 95; the four hurts 90; WEAPON BROKEN 88; COOPERATION 87; JAMMED 86; BLEEDING 85; the two fractures 80; EXIT LOCATED 77; OPEN and CHECK HIM 75; the loots, LOCKED DOOR, DEHYDRATED, EXHAUSTED 70.

**The prompt** (bottom left, "BROKEN LEG [Y]"): shown while any phrase is active, the top one by priority (a tie goes to the name last in the alphabet), its words the phrase's, its tab the `MumbleQuick` key's name, with a small down arrow (`gesture_down_icon`) beneath. **A tap of Y** says it (asked, not aggressive) unless the list is up, or was shut less than 0.3 s ago, or the prompt's last phrase is still playing; the phrase's priority drops by 10, so another tap moves to the next. When the voice starts it the prompt **blinks** for the clip's length (its alpha 1, 0, 1, 0, 1 at sixths of a second, held to the second, looping: `PhraseAlphaAnim`), then stays lit; a phrase that came from a 5 s situation is then dropped. A phrase the voice refuses (busy with one as important) never blinks and stays.

**Holding Y** opens the list after 0.2 s: one row for each active phrase, ordered by number, growing up from beside the prompt, the chosen row light with its "Y" tab; the wheel moves the choice (down one row a notch down, up a notch up, wrapping) and is not the character's meanwhile; nothing else is taken: **the character walks and looks while Y is held**. Letting go shuts it and says the chosen phrase if it is still active (no blink); the tap waits 0.3 s more. The action panel hides while the list is up. (Read only: the list also files a "none" entry the first time it fills, so on opening the first row looks chosen and letting go says nothing; a notch down chooses it for real.)

## A hand sign shown (`Player.ShowGesture`)

The same route as a door's or a head switch's gesture (interactions spec, "Whose animation it is"): `MovementContext.SetInteractInHands` asks the body's gestures' model, which plays it on the body's `InteractionsLayer` by `IsInteraction` and `InteractionIndex`. The arms' own animator plays nothing (its old left-hand gesture states, `LActions.GESTURES`, are reached only by a route nothing calls).

| Hand sign | Game number | Animator's | Clip, length |
|---|---|---|---|
| There | 1 | 1 | `tp_gesture_01_there`, 1.13 s |
| Hold | 2 | 2 | `tp_gesture_02_hold`, 1.33 s |
| Greet (`Friendly`) | 3 | 3 | `tp_gesture_03_friendly`, 1.30 s |
| Flip off (`GetOff`) | 4 | 4 | `tp_gesture_04_getoff`, 1.23 s |
| Thumbs up (`Ok`) | 5 | 5 | `tp_gesture_05_ok`, 1.07 s |
| Thumbs down (`No`) | 6 | 6 | `tp_gesture_06_no`, 1.27 s |
| Follow me (`ComeWithMe`) | 7 | 7 | `tp_gesture_07_come`, 1.13 s |
| Rock | 200 | 36 | `Gestures_rock`, 3.37 s |
| Scissors | 201 | **38** | `Gestures_scissors`, 3.37 s |
| Paper | 202 | **37** | `Gestures_paper`, 3.37 s |
| OK (`AllRight`) | 203 | 39 | `Gestures_OK`, 2.23 s |

Scissors and Paper change places between the game's numbers and the animator's. Each state is left at its clip's end; none has an event (no sound). The clips carry the curves that hand the left arm and the weapon to the body (`First_Person_Curve_Weight`, `Hand_Left`, `Elbow_Left`, `Shoulder_Left`, 0 up to 1 and back), so in first person the left arm makes the sign while the right hand keeps the weapon (five of them move the right elbow too, four the right shoulder).

**Refused**: mounted (or a stationary weapon in hand); in a sprint; while the thing in hand's animator is not at rest (a reload, a shot, a draw, an inspection, a heal, a throw, an open inventory...); in a jump or its landing (the hand signs alone); and while a door's or the take's gesture keeps the queue shut. Not refused: aiming (the sights stay up), crouched or prone, leaning, blind fire, walking. **Queued**: a hand sign asks nothing of the queue, so it waits behind whatever plays, and one asked five times plays five times. **Cut** with the rest of the gestures (but operations) by the inventory opening, the sprint starting, a magazine reload asked and a magazine check asked (`ForceStopInteractions`). A playing hand sign keeps the bipod and the backpack's drop from starting; it does not stop firing.

## A phrase said (`Player.Say`)

`PlayPhraseOrGesture`: a number that is a hand sign's is shown; any other is a phrase, said (asked, at once, every tag allowed, aggressive or not) if it is not situational or its situation holds. **The mumble** becomes the fight bank (`OnFight`) if aggressive or the character is aware, else the mutter bank (`OnMutter`). **The tags**: the health's (`Dying`, `BadlyInjured`, `Injured`, `Healthy`, as the app's hurt phrases have them), and `Combat` if aggressive or aware, else `Unaware` (the solo tag is the speaker's own). **Awareness** is a time: a shot of the character's own makes it aware for 15 s, a hit by an enemy for 20 s, a bullet flying by for 5 s and more. The speaker then plays it as it plays the app's breaths and grunts (sound spec): busy for the clip's length and 0.1 s, refusing what is not strictly more important than what plays and cutting what is less; the voice's importances: commands, reactions, health, help and team calls 2, contacts and enemies 3, the fight and mutter banks 0 (so a mumble is refused while a breath plays, and a command cuts it). No phrase has a time of its own before it may be said again. The voice is first person, unplaced, as the grunts. **COOPERATION** alone also shows the Greet hand sign (said by the prompt when looking at another player: none here).

## The options' copy (`SettingsGesturesMenu`; not built, by choice)

The settings' Control tab shows another copy of the menu behind its GESTURES toggle, laid out otherwise (groups in a grid, the hand signs in rows, the bind panel at the bottom right), without the groups that hold only situational phrases and with every situational phrase off. A click there says the phrase in the voice through the interface's sound (the mumble the fight or the mutter bank at even odds); a hand sign does nothing. Binds made there are kept with the screen's Save.

## Built

- **The hand signs** (`trp_sim::interactions`): the eleven as `Interaction`s, their animator's numbers (Scissors 38, Paper 37) and the game's (200 to 203), asking nothing of the queue; `Movement::set_interaction` refuses a hand sign in a jump and its landing. **The forced stops** the app lacked for every gesture: a sprint asked where the health lets it (`enable_sprint`), and, before the weapon is told, a plain magazine reload (out of blind fire; refused while an operation still plays) and a magazine check (the same) (`controller`).
- **The voice** (`trp_sim::speaker`): `say` (the mumble's switch, the health's and the awareness's tags), `aware` (a shot of the character's own 15 s, an enemy's hit 20 s: a weapon's, an explosion's, a fragment's), the hurt's and the berserk's tags now by the awareness (they were always unaware). The app reads every bank's tags and lengths at the start, the sounds of a bank the first time it is said, opening the voice again for it (`trp_data::sound::VoiceReader`; `voice_sound`; measured: all 94 banks' sounds 85 MB in 0.21 s, the voice's bundles kept open 188 MB at the end of a run).
- **The keys**: F1 to F12 and their double presses as the game's reserved bindings (`Controls::generate`), hidden from the settings; Y's three as the install has them. While the menu is open the bindings give only its own key and the inventory's, push no axis and turn nothing (`Bindings::only`); while the list is up the wheel is the list's (`Bindings::wheel_taken`).
- **The menu, the prompt and the list** (`gestures`): laid out from the install's places at 1080 lines (above), drawn with its sprites (`sharedassets44.assets`) and icons (`resources.assets`, `static icons`), its colours and Bender; drawn afresh when what it shows changes. The blur over 0.35 s, the cursor freed, the action panel hidden; Escape with `MenuEscape`. The binding menu is drawn by the app (twelve 115 by 15 lines, each key's name on its tab), not the game's context menu prefab. The binds are kept in the options' file (`"gesture_binds"`).
- **The situations**: the whole body's health, the effects (bleeding, the fractures, dehydration, exhaustion), what is looked at (a loose thing by its class, a body, a container, a door, locked or shut), and 5 s after a reload begun, a grenade's pin pulled, a reload with no magazine found, a malfunction made known at the shot (again 150 ms on), reminded of, or on a weapon drawn (`Controller::phrase_situations`; until 0.225.0 the look raised it and the shot and the draw did not, so the prompt first showed at the first trigger after a jam). `PROVISIONAL`: looked at once a frame, a fresh look or a new effect restoring a phrase's priority; a residual effect counts as holding.
- **Not built**: the options' copy (by choice); COOPERATION and EXIT LOCATED (no other player, no exit on the range); the enemy's grenade and a friend down (no such events); the list's "none" entry; the hand signs' and the phrases' events for bots; the click that plays a phrase in the options' copy.

**Compared with the game: nothing.** Every rule above is read from the client's code and the install; the hand signs were seen in the app (the Greet's left hand off the weapon, the right on it: `local\shots\gsign_s216.png`), the menu, a group opened, the binding menu, the prompt (`local\shots\gmenu_*_s216.png`, `gmenu_play_s216.png`), and the phrases heard in the log (a tap's HURT LIGHT, F1's mumble as the mutter, a bank first said playing 7 ms after it was asked while the voice stayed open; about 0.2 s since it is opened again). Not run: a take of the game to set the timings of Y's presses beside (a tap's phrase 0.3 s after the key, the list's 0.2 s), a hand sign's look against a recording, the aggressive phrases, the jammed and broken weapon's prompt.
