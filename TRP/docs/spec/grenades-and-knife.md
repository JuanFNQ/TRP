# Grenades and the knife

What the hands do with a grenade and with a melee weapon, and what a grenade thrown does. Read from the game's code on 2026-10-17 (`Player.BaseGrenadeHandsController`, `GrenadeHandsController` and its operations, `QuickGrenadeThrowHandsController`; `Player.BaseKnifeController`, `KnifeController`, `QuickKnifeKickController`, `KnifeCollider`; `Grenade`, `Throwable`, `GrenadeFactory`, `ExplosionSharedMethods`; `PlayerInputTranslator`, `GrenadeInputTranslator`, `QuickUseSelectorUtil`; `PhysicalBase`, `Physical`), from the install (the item database; the hands' animators, read by `probe states <bundle>`), and set beside recordings of 2026-10-17 where it says so (`local\traces\courses2`, course 2: recorder 0.10.0). A grenade and a knife, each by its key.

## Taking them in hand

- **A grenade** comes into the hands from a quick slot, as medicine does (`Player.SetQuickSlotItem`, `TryProceed`): the grenade's own slot, bound to `G` in this install (`EBoundItem.ItemG`; `QuickUseSelectorUtil`: the key down chooses, as `4` to `0` choose theirs, and let go takes it), or any of `4` to `0` it is bound to. The player is then given a grenade controller (`GrenadeHandsController`). **There is no quick throw for the player**: the controller for one (`QuickGrenadeThrowHandsController`) is made only for a player driven over the network (`ClientPlayer`). Recorded so: `G` took the F-1 into the hands (`2-grenade-quick`).
- **Which grenade `G` takes** (`PlayerInputTranslator`, `PressThrowGrenade`; `FastAccessGrenadeItemView`): the equipment's top priority grenade. The bar's grenade view sets it as the raid's screen is shown, and again when one is gone, to the first of the vest's and the pockets' grenades (their top level, examined) by kind (`ThrowType`: frag, flash, stun, smoke, gas, incendiary, sonar); the game's grenade selector (the key held) sets it otherwise. Only without one (the view never shown) does the key take a grenade at random, not the same kind twice running where it can help it. The view shows that grenade.
- **The knife** is the sheath's slot, `U` in this install (`SelectKnife`: `SetSlotItem` with the sheath; `KnifeController`). **The quick stroke** is `U` twice (`QuickKnife`, a double click; `PlayerInputTranslator.method_4`): with the hands able to act, no stationary weapon, nothing that cannot be broken off in hand, no knife in hand already, a knife in the sheath and the stamina for a stroke (below), a controller of its own is made (`QuickKnifeKickController`) that strikes once and gives the hands back.
- After a throw the grenade is gone from the inventory (the throw is an inventory operation that takes it out), and the hands go back to what they held before, as after medicine used up; after the quick stroke likewise (its "used" call).

## The grenade's hands

**The rig.** A grenade's item prefab (the F-1's: `assets/content/weapons/f1/weapon_grenade_f1_container.bundle`) is a weapon prefab of its own kind (`GrenadePrefab`), with the arms' animator, the bones the parts thrown hang from (`ThrowingParts`: the grenade, its pin, its lever), and the grenade to be thrown (`GrenadeItself`, a `GrenadeSettings` object with its body, collider and the offset it leaves the hand at). Made in hand (`Setup`): the animator moved on by a sixtieth of a second, the throwing parts shown, the animator told the point of view.

**The animator's parameters** (`probe states <bundle> parameters`, the F-1's): `Active`, `Inventory`, `QuickFire`, `GrenadeFire` and `GrenadeAltFire` (whole numbers: 0 idle, 1 hold, 2 throw), `FireMode` (6 thrown, 7 planted), `Look`, `CheckChamber`, `IdleTime`, `IdleVar`, `UseLeftHand`, `LActionIndex`, `HandReady`, `LeftHandProgress`, `Patrol`, `SpeedDraw`, `Gesture`, `GestureIndex`.

**Its states** (the F-1's `Hands` layer; each clip's length, the marks in it at their share of the state):

| State | Clip | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `SPAWN` | 0.033 s | to `OUT TO IDLE` when `Active` and not `QuickFire` (`FireMode` under 7); to `Q FIRE` with `QuickFire` |
| `OUT TO IDLE` | 1.333 s | the draw: `Draw` sound 0.121, `Safety` 0.595, **`WeapIn` 0.921** (in hand); to `IDLE` at its end |
| `IDLE` | 0.333 s, looping | `IdleStart`; to `FIRE START` when `GrenadeFire` > 0, `ALT FIRE START` when `GrenadeAltFire` > 0, `IDLE TO OUT` when not `Active`, `OPEN INVENTORY`, `LOOK`, `CHECK` |
| `FIRE START`, `ALT FIRE START` | 1.333 s | the pin pulled (`Pin` 0.241, `Lever` 0.483); then the hold |
| `FIRE IDLE`, `ALT FIRE IDLE` | 3.0 s at half speed | the hold; to `FIRE` (`ALT FIRE`) when the value is 2, with `Active` and no inventory; to `FIRE ABORT OUT` (blend 0.25 s) when it falls under 1, or not `Active`; to `FIRE ABORT INV` when the inventory opens |
| `FIRE` | 0.9 s | the throw from above: `Throw` sound 0.301, **`FiringBullet` 0.499** (the grenade lets go), `SndFuse` at the grenade 0.553, `FireEnd` 0.963; to `SPAWN` |
| `ALT FIRE` | 0.9 s | the throw from below: `FiringBullet` at **0.355**, `FireEnd` 0.963 |
| `FIRE ABORT OUT`, `FIRE ABORT INV` | 1.733 s | the pin put back; back to `IDLE` (or the put-away, or the inventory) |
| `IDLE TO OUT` | 1.033 s | the put-away: **`WeapOut` 0.968** |

So a throw takes at least the pin's 1.333 s and then 0.449 s (high) or 0.320 s (low) of the throw's clip from the button going down.

**Its logic** (`GrenadeHandsController` and its operations):

- **Drawn** (`SpawnOperation`): `Active` on; the body's animator told a weapon one cell wide and a reload's float of 1 while it draws; at `WeapIn` the grenade is idle (`Idling`, `FireMode` set to thrown) and the reload's float back to 0.
- **Idle** (`Idling`): the left button down (`TryHighThrow`) pulls the pin for a throw from above (`HighThrowOperation`: the character says its grenade line, `GrenadeFire` to 1, the throw waiting); the right (`TryLowThrow`) for one from below (`LowThrowOperation`, `GrenadeAltFire`). `B` switches to planting a tripwire where the grenade allows it and the character has a kit (`PlantTripwireOperation`: not built).
- **Waiting** (the button held): the button let go (`FinishHighThrow`, `FinishLowThrow`) throws: the value to 2. Nothing else the buttons do is heard. The inventory opened puts the pin back where the grenade may be hidden in a throw (`CanBeHiddenDuringThrow`, the F-1's true): both values to 0 and the grenade idle again. The weapon's key (the hands asked for something else) puts it away at once where it may be hidden, else waits for the throw.
- **The throw**: at `FiringBullet` the value goes back to 0, the grenade in the hand is hidden and the grenade is thrown (`Throw`, next section) with **no time spent on its fuse**: the player's throw always passes 0 (`SpawnGrenade` → `Throw(0, low)`), so the fuse begins when the hand lets go, however long the button was held. Recorded: 0 in all six throws of `courses2`. At `FireEnd` the use is over and the hands go back.
- **Put away** (`Remove`): `Active` off, the body's reload float 1; at `WeapOut` the next thing comes.
- **The buttons are bindings of their own** (read 2026-11-01, built 0.170.1; `ControlSettingsGroup`'s generated keys, `KeyGroup.CreateFrom`, `InputKey.GetKey`, `GrenadeInputTranslator`): whenever the bindings are read the game makes `HighThrow` of the trigger's keys and `LowThrow` of the aim's, each held (`Continuous`) whatever the binding it is made of, after all the others, and each with keys of its own (`ignorePool`: its own pressed state and owner, so it neither takes a key from the trigger or the aim nor gives one up). The grenade hears these alone (`TryHighThrow`, `FinishHighThrow`, `TryLowThrow`, `FinishLowThrow`): with the aim bound as a press (as this install has it), the throw from below still goes as the right button comes up. The settings screen does not list them. Until 0.170.1 the app threw by the trigger's and the aim's own bindings, so with the aim a press the pin stayed out until the button was pressed again.
- **The next hands after a throw come at `FireEnd`, which comes as the throw's way back begins** (read 2026-11-01; `courses8`'s `grenade-throws-from-outside`): the throw's states leave for `SPAWN` at 0.75 of `FIRE` and 0.722 of `ALT FIRE`, and a state being left gives up every mark it has not yet given, at once (`AnimationEventsContainer.CheckEvents`: in a transition to another state it fires all up to the end; `marks` has done so since it was built), so `FireEnd` (0.963 in the clip) is delivered then: 0.330 s after the let-go from below and 0.226 s from above, the game's 0.336 and 0.230 to the frame. The marks are delivered in the hands' late update (`EmitOnDemand`, `ArmsLateUpdate`); `FireEnd` hands the hands back (`HighThrowOperation.OnDropFinishedAction`, `_onUsedCallback`: the thing held before, else the first weapon in the slots, else the empty hands, `SetFirstAvailableItem`), and in the same call the grenade's controller is destroyed, its rig returned to its pool, and the empty hands made (`DestroyController`, `CreateController`: no wait, no load). The let-go puts the grenade out of the inventory at once (`Player.ThrowGrenade`, a discard: no `ThrowOperation`, which is a dropped thing's); that changes no hands. The app's and the replay's hands change at `FireEnd` as the game's.
- **Who may throw**: only with the hands able to act (`CanThrow`: `StateIsSuitableForHandInput`); the look (`L`) plays `LOOK`.
- **A button pressed during the draw is not heard**: the draw's operation does nothing with the fire input (its base, `ProhibitedCall`), and the key gives its command once as it goes down (`ToggleInputKeyCombination`: `TryHighThrow` down, `FinishHighThrow` up), so a button held through the draw does not pull the pin; it must be pressed again once the grenade is idle.

## The throw

`BaseGrenadeHandsController.Throw` for the player (a bot's differs):

- **The strength** is the hard settings' `GrenadeForce` (11.55 in the install; the recorder's header) times 0.66 for a throw from below, or 1 plus Strength's throwing buff (`StrengthBuffThrowDistanceInc`) for one from above.
- **The direction** is down the drop bone (`-up` of the body's `WeaponRoot`, which on the frame of the throw is the look turned back 90 degrees about its x, so its down is the look: in all six recorded throws of `courses2` the force less the player's velocity lies within 0.02 degrees of that frame's look, `bin/grenade_knife`) five times over, plus, below half the hands' stamina, a random unit vector times how far under half it is; normalised. Without Throwing's elite buff the strength is also multiplied by `lerp(0.4, 1, handsStamina + 0.5)` (1 at half the hands' stamina and above). Since 0.103.0 the high throw's `1 + Strength's throw buff` and Throwing's elite go by the skills' levels (`skills.md`), as a knife's blow goes by Strength's melee buff (`1 +` it, the damage the log writes); a throw's stamina cost (`ConsumeAsMelee` of `GrenadeHighThrow` or `GrenadeLowThrow`, less Throwing's buff, nothing at its elite) is not built.
- **The force** is that direction times that strength, **plus the player's velocity**.
- **The place**: the grenade bone's place plus its turn times the grenade's offset (`GrenadeItself.Offset`); if a line from the body's forward surface to there meets the static world, the place is pulled back to 10 cm short of what it met. Its turn is the grenade bone's.
- **Which place** (measured): the grenade bone where the frame of the throw draws it, with the first person's squeeze along the view on (the rig squeezed about its root, as drawn), and the offset turned with it at its full length (the offset is not squeezed: `position + rotation * Offset`). The rig is that frame's: the clip at the time the frame's update left it (the mark is passed in that update), the moved bone where that frame put it. Recorded (`bin/grenade_knife`, the rig posed by our animator at the recorded state and time and hung on the recorded moved bone): all six throws of `courses2` (two from above, standing and walking; two from below; one of each seen from outside, where nothing is squeezed) within 0.1 mm of the place and 0.01 degrees of the turn. With the squeeze taken off the bone, the place is 13 to 17 cm off along the view; with the frame before's clip time the turn is 4.5 degrees off.
- **Recorded** (`courses2`, `2-grenade-high`, standing, the hands' stamina full, Strength's buff 0.16 in the header): the force's length is **13.398 = 11.55 × 1.16**; the second, thrown walking, 16.43, the walk's velocity added.
- The grenade is taken out of the inventory (`ThrowGrenade`, an inventory operation), the Throwing skill's action counted, and the grenade made (`CreateAndRegisterGrenade`): a copy of the prefab's `GrenadeItself` at that place and turn, on the `Default` layer, its collisions with the thrower's capsule ignored, given the force as an **impulse** (`AddForce(force, Impulse)`: its velocity becomes force over mass). Recorded: the velocity one physics step on is the force over 0.6, less that step's gravity, to the third figure.

**The grenade's body** (the F-1's, as the game set it up: `grenades_new` of `courses2`, recorder 0.10.0): a rigid body of **0.6 kg**, drag 0.1, angular drag 0.1, continuous dynamic collision, a sleep threshold of 0.005, a top spin of 7, a depenetration speed of 10; a capsule 3.5 cm in radius and 15 cm long along its z, of the material `metal` (bounciness 0.1, both frictions 0.4, both combined by their average). Each collision of any strength (an impulse over √0.2) plays its collision sound and may set off a grenade that explodes on contact (`MinTimeToContactExplode`, -1 for the F-1: never).

**The fuse** is the grenade's `explDelay` less the time already spent (0 for the player's): **3.5 s for the F-1**. Its item has a second field, `ExplDelay`, of 3; the game reads the first. Recorded: all six blasts 3.500 to 3.508 s after the grenade was made (a frame's length at most).

## The blast

`Grenade.Explosion`, `ExplosionSharedMethods.Explosion`:

- The explosion's effect (`ExplosionEffectType`, the F-1's `Grenade_new`) at the grenade, and the grenade object destroyed.
- **Who it reaches**: every player within the largest of twice the greatest distance (`MaxExplosionDistance`, the F-1's 7 m), the blindness's and the contusion's reach.
- **Fragments aimed at a player**: as many as the fragment count over the sphere's area at the player's distance, times 2 (`count / (4π d²) × 2`, at most 3), handed out in pieces of 0.3 to 2, each a shot of the grenade's fragment round from the blast at a point near the player's chest (0.8 m up, scattered by half a metre over the piece). **Random fragments** then make the rest up to 30 (or the count, if fewer): in random directions, three in four turned up for a blast on the ground (under 0.3 m above the terrain), down for one in the air (no terrain within 4 m). The fragments are the thrower's shots in the ballistics. Recorded: 30 a blast, the player too far to be aimed at.
- **Where it is** (read again 2026-10-17, night, `Grenade.Explosion`, `ExplosionSharedMethods.AffectPlayersShared`): the grenade's place raised 8 cm (`_defaultShift`); the effect is shown at the grenade's own place.
- **The shake** (`ForceEffector.GrenadeShake_CO`): for the player's own character, by `inverseLerp(3 × greatest distance, least / 2, distance)` (the F-1's 21 m and 1.5 m) where above nothing: its springs kicked a tenth of a second apart, the waits growing by a tenth each, the kicks shrinking by a quarter, for three quarters of that strength in seconds.
- **Damage to a player** within the greatest distance or the contusion's reach: for each of the body's hit colliders (and armour plates), a line from the blast to it; anything it meets that the fragment cannot pierce stops it (no damage to that collider; the contusion is then reduced by the wall), what it can pierce takes its level plus 2 from the fragment's penetration. The damage is `inverseLerp(MaxExplosionDistance, MinExplosionDistance, distance) × Strength` (the F-1's 7 m, 3 m, 80) times a circular ease-in of the penetration left, the largest of a part's colliders taken for that part, and armour worn for a part damaged by the `ArmorDistanceDistanceDamage` curve. Each collider's line is from the blast to its object's own place (not its shape's middle), and only the world's solid and terrain colliders are tried (`HighPolyWithTerrainMask`: not the bodies). The damage is health's own (`ApplyEnvironmentalDamage` → `Player.ApplyDamageInfo` → `ApplyDamage`), of the kind `GrenadeFragment` (`Grenade.GetDamageInfo`), as any damage with its bleeding's chances, pain and over-damage; armour takes none of it off the body, and is itself damaged by the armour curve (`ApplyExplosionDamageToArmor`: `(1 − inverseLerp(x, y, distance)) × z`, the F-1's 1 to 4 m, 25). **Contusion** (`DoContusion`): where a line reached the body, or the chest's line was stopped, `inverseLerp(y, x, distance) × z` of the contusion's figures (the F-1's 4 m, 1.5 m, 15) from the player's place (its feet); where every line was stopped, times the wall's absorption, `inverseLerp(0.4, 0.01, the wall's thickness)` (`WallContusionAbsorption`; the thickness from where the chest's line enters the wall to where the line back from the chest does); applied above 2, for that many seconds at a strength of 1 (health spec, "A contusion"). Players run by the game's bots are not contused. **The screen shakes** for the local player by how near it was (from three times the greatest distance in to half the least).
- **On any character** (read again 2026-11-03, 0.193.0; `AffectPlayersShared`, `Player.ApplyExplosionDamageToArmor`, `ArmorComponent.ApplyExplosionDurabilityDamage`): every living character the blast's overlap finds is walked alike, a bot as the player (but one whose settings say `GRENADE_DAMAGE_IGNORE`, and **a bot is never contused**). Its colliders are gathered in the prefab's order (`PlayerBones.BodyPartColliders`, then each `ArmorPlateCollider` whose object is on), and **a part's damage is given in the order its first collider comes** (the pelvis's Stomach, the legs, the chest, the arms, the head last: `_damagedParts`), so a head destroyed by the blast still lets every other part take its share. A line that goes through things (each with a chance and a level at most the fragment round's power) takes their levels, each plus 2, from the power, and the damage is times `CircularEaseIn(InverseLerp(0, power, power − levels))`. **Armour**: for each collider a line reached, `(1 − InverseLerp(x, y, its distance)) × z` of `ArmorDistanceDistanceDamage` (the F-1's 1 to 4 m, 25), kept by its collider type and plate (a later collider of the same writing over); each piece worn takes the sum of what it covers (`ShotMatches`) times its material's `ExplosionDestructibility` off its durability, never below nought, after the parts' damage and for a character found alive as the blast came (one it kills still takes it); a face shield's glass whose share is 0.4 of its template durability or more is first marked by the thrower, found among the living (body hits spec, "A face shield's marks"). A death by a blast pushes the body by nothing: the blast's damage has no way (`GetDamageInfo`: `Direction` nought).
- **When, for a bot** (measured, `courses3`'s `3-bot-blast`, below): its colliders where the frame before left its bones (the blast goes off before the frame's animation), its place (`Position`, for the distance and the aimed fragments) the blast's own frame's (it has moved). A character killed by the blast was still found by it: the game takes a dead one out of its list a frame after the death (`OnDeadCoroutine`).

## The fragments (`ExplosionSharedMethods.Explosion`, `SpawnRandomFragments`, `Grenade.GrenadeRandoms`, `PrecalculatedRandoms`; read 2026-11-03)

A blast's fragments are shots of the grenade's fragment round (`FragmentType`; `CreateFragment`) from the blast's place, made by the ballistics calculator as any shot is (`CreateShot`: its seed `Random.Range(0, 512)`, its speed the round's times the share given, `fireIndex` 1), the thrower's, so a fragment never meets the thrower's own body (`Shot.IsHitIgnored`: a shot's first fragment ignores its player's body colliders). Each fragment's way is taken from **the grenades' table** (`GrenadeRandoms`: `PrecalculatedRandoms(128, 1337)`, made as the game's world wakes: 128 `Random.onUnitSphere` from Unity's generator seeded 1337) at **the fragment's number**, one counter for the whole run (`GlobalFragmentNumber`, from nought, never set back), the table's place the number modulo 128.

- **Aimed**, first, at each character found (after the flash's effects, before the damage), in the overlap's order: its share `Clamp(count / (4π d²) × 2, 0, 3)` (`d` from its place), handed out while above 0.3 in pieces of `Clamp(left, 0.3, 2)`: the counter moved on first, then a fragment toward its chest, `place + 0.8 m up + 0.5 × table[number] / piece − blast`, at the round's speed.
- **At random**, then (a coroutine started at once): `Min(30, count) − aimed` of them, each the way `table[number]` (its height turned the other way for three in four, `i % 4 < 3`, where it points down for a blast on the ground or up for one in the air), at `0.5 + (number % 10) / 20` of the round's speed (45 to 85.5 m/s for a 90 m/s round), the counter moved on after; **nine a frame**, the first nine on the blast's frame. **On the ground** is a ray down within 4 m (`TerrainLowPoly`) meeting something within 0.3 m; **in the air**, meeting nothing.
- Each shot first moves on the frame after it is made, as every single shot (ballistics spec).

The app's (`trp_sim::fragments`, the app's `bullets`): made at every blast with fragments, the character (before its move of the frame, alive) and each living dummy (after its move) aimed at, the character first (`PROVISIONAL`: the game's order is its overlap's), the range below it the ground's test; flown by the app's ballistics, their hits on the range and the dummies played and delivered as any bullet's (`dummy::take`). The thrower's body is not in the range's world, so none meets it.

## The blast's sound (`Effects.EmitGrenade`, `Effect.Emit`, `Effect.PlaySound`, `BetterAudio.PlayAtPointDistant`, `SuperSourceDistant`; read 2026-10-18)

The grenade's effect (`ExplosionEffectType`: the F-1's `Grenade_new`; the install's grenades name five, `Grenade_new`, `Grenade_new2`, `Grenade_indoor`, `Flashbang` and a smoke's, which has no sound) is emitted at the grenade's own place, upward, as seen from outside (`EmitGrenade`: never the first person's sound), and its sound (`Effect.Sound`) plays 5 cm up from it, by the camera's distance from the grenade, not past the bank's rolloff.

- **A bank played from afar** (`Physical`, as those of `Grenade_new`, `Grenade_new2` and `Grenade_indoor`, 1000 m): through a source of two (`SuperSourceDistant`, `Audio/AudioSourceSuperSpatialBuiltinEQPrefab`; how it falls: below, not the prefab's logarithmic rolloff as read 2026-10-18), the bank's two clips by the distance as its blend points say (`Grenade_new`: close to 10 m, close and distant to 30, distant to 60, distant and far to 200, far beyond), each share on its own source, **after the sound's travel** (`Delay`: the distance over 340 m/s), at the bank's random volume and pitch, through the mixer group of its source kind (`Grenades`: `Guns/Gunshots`). Its spatial blend is passed as `Mathf.Clamp(0.2, 0.9, distance / 30)`, the value first: 0.2 is under the least, 0.9, so **0.9 at any distance** (a tenth of it not placed).
- **Any other** (`Flashbang`'s `zarya`, 300 m): `PlayAtPoint` on its kind's source group (`Grenades` too, a source of two), at once, with the full occlusion test (`OneShotFullOcclusion`). `PlayAtPoint` does not set the borrowed source's delay or spatial blend, so it plays with what its last use by `PlayAtPointDistant` left (at first no delay and a blend of 1).

**How it falls** (read 2026-11-04, recorded): every source of a group is made with a custom rolloff, the group preset's `SoundRolloff` over the rolloff distance (`NonspatialAudioMixer.InstantiateNewSource`), which overwrites the prefab's logarithmic one: every blast in `courses3` is recorded "Custom", from 1 m, to its bank's rolloff. The distant source's second source takes that mode at its start but keeps its own prefab's curve. **Outdoors** (the source and the listener not in one indoor room), a one-shot occlusion test starts the sound silent, its occlusion nought and its low-pass at 20 Hz, until the occluder's job gives its result (`MultiSourceSpatialAudioOccluder`). Neither is built: the app's blast falls as one over the distance past 1 m and is heard at once.

The banks' figures: `grenadeBank` (`Grenade_new`'s and `Grenade_indoor`'s, four clips in each list), `grenadeBank_rgn` (`Grenade_new2`'s, two), volume 1 and pitch 1 without spread; the `Grenades` group's preset in SPT's `settings.json` has an overall volume of 1.

## The blast's effect (`Effects.EmitGrenade`, `Effect.Emit`, `BasicParticleSystemMediator`, `ParticleSystemAdapter`; the effects' bundle; read 2026-10-18)

For a grenade, `Effect.Emit` lays a decal where it went off (the deferred decals where the hard settings have them, else `Decal`); then, the effect being seen from outside, it plays the **mediator's** effect at the grenade's place, plays each of the entry's **older emitters** (`Particles`) from the place moved `ParticlesShift` along the normal (each only where the camera's distance is within its `Distance`), adds a **light** from the pool (where `Light` is set, the camera within `LightMaxDist`) and plays the sound (above).

- **The mediator** (`BasicParticleSystemMediator`) holds copies of one effect and plays them in turn: the next copy moved to the place, turned to `LookRotation(normal)` (for a grenade, `Vector3.up`: the effect's z axis up), and played with every system under it (`Play(withChildren: true)`) but those another of them sets off: a sub-emitter never emits on its own (read from the engine's code, `UnityPlayer.dll` decompiled with Unity's public symbols: `ParticleSystem::MarkAllSubEmitters` sets each sub-emitter's flag of emission stopped, which `IsEmitting` tests, and `Play` on a system so marked only marks it playing, its own emission never started). `Grenade_new`'s and `Grenade_new2`'s have five copies, named `(1)` to `(5)`, listing the same systems with the same modules. A system of a copy played again while it still plays goes on as it was, only moved (`Play`: "If the Particle System is already playing, the system continues to play and this function has no effect", the 2022.3 scripting reference; the install is of 2022.3.43f1). The engine's `Play` agrees (decompiled): it sets the time to nothing and draws the start delay only for a system in its stopped state. `PROVISIONAL`: a system whose emission has ended with particles still alive taken as not yet stopped (where the engine stops one was not found).
- **`Grenade_new`** (the F-1, RGD-5, M67, the VOGs and the V40): seventeen systems under its root, each with its own place and turn below it. The fireball: `flash` (one particle of 3 m for half a second, `CustomFX/HDRFireParticle`), `flash_area` (one, drawn by nothing: its light alone) and `explosion_center` (four to seven of 0.2 to 1.5 m on a sheet of 12 by 7, `Particles/HSL Additive`, in the effect's own space). The smoke: the root (seven of 5 to 15 m from a cone, a fifth to half a second, on a sheet of 4 by 4, `Particles/Smoke Bumped`), `Effect Smoke Distorted` (15 to 25 of 4 to 6 m, out at 10 to 30 m/s, for 1 to 3 s), `Smoke Explosion stretched` (ten stretched along their way, from 0.84 m below the ground up within 18°, `Particles/Smoke Bumped`), and `long smoke` (10 to 20 of 1 to 4 m, for 10 to 30 s, from 0.3 s on, `Particles/Smoke Distorted`). - **Idle** (`Idling`): the left button strikes a **slash**, the right a **stab** (`Kick`), each only with the hands able to act and the stamina for a stroke (`Physical.CanMeleeHit`: the legs' stamina, or else the hands', can take the melee's consumption); without it the character is told it has none. After 300 s idle, the idle's own animation.
- **A stroke** (`Kick`): the stroke's kind kept (`LastKickType`), `Fire` or `AltFire` on, `Deflected` 0, the swing's speed set (`Physical.MeleeSpeed`: 1, or the stamina settings' `ExhaustedMeleeSpeed` with the hands exhausted). Every frame the blade's place is kept and the swing's direction eased toward its last step (by 0.9). At `FiringBullet` the stamina is spent (`ConsumeAsMelee`: the knife's `PrimaryConsumption` for a slash, its `SecondryConsumption`, so spelt in the item, for a stab; the ice pick's 10 and 13) and the blade made live for that stroke's reach (`PrimaryDistance` 0.6 m, `SecondryDistance` 0.65 m for the ice pick). At `FireEnd` both buttons' flags off and the hands idle (or what was asked meanwhile, a put-away). **The rest's start ends a stroke too**: the rest's state has a mark at its start, `IdleStart`, which a stroke still under way takes as its end (`Kick.OnIdleStart` calls the controller's `OnFireEnd`). So a button taken between one stroke's `FireEnd` and the end of its state starts a second stroke at once (the hands idle, the flag on), and the animator, which goes to the rest when the first stroke's state ends, meets that mark there before it can enter the new stroke: the flag goes off and the press is lost, its stamina never spent. Recorded (`courses2`, `2-outside-grenade-knife`, frame 6272): a left click 0.81 of the way through a stab; the operation `Kick` from that frame, `Idling` from the rest's first frame, and no slash until the next click.
- **The blade's sweep** (`KnifeCollider.ManualUpdateClient`, every frame while live): a box the blade collider's width and depth and a quarter of its length thick (half extents: half its width, a quarter of its length, half its depth; neither the size nor the centre scaled), about the collider's centre turned with the blade's object, moved back a blade's length along the player's look (`LookDirection`), and swept along the look by the stroke's reach (`MaxDistance`, set at `FiringBullet`) on the layers a blade hits (`_knifeMask`: `HitCollider`, `HighPolyCollider`, `TransparentCollider` and `Water`, triggers included; not `Terrain`), its touches put into two places (`BoxCastNonAlloc`); the first place whose collider is not the player's own (`AllCollidersNotYours`: its body parts' and plates' colliders) is **hit, once a stroke**.
- **Which touch comes first** (read 2026-11-09, 0.238.0, in the engine itself with Ghidra and in PhysX's own source, the version physx-sys carries). Unity's box cast asks PhysX for touches alone (`BoxCastInternal`: its filter, `CastFilter`, gives every shape on the mask hit type 1, a touch, and leaves out a trigger only when asked to), gathers up to 128 of them, and hands them on in the order PhysX gave (`SweepCallbackNonAlloc::processTouches`, copying until the knife's two places are full); a touch the box began inside comes with no point (written as nought). PhysX gives them by its query trees, the still things' tree first, then the moving bodies' (`NpSceneQueries::multiQuery`: the static pruner, then the dynamic, then the compound), each tree visited nearer child first ("push the one with the further center on the stack for later", `AABBTreeRaycast`), so not by distance. **A living character's hit colliders are among the moving bodies**: every ragdoll bone's rigid body is made, kinematic, as the character's object is made (`PlayerPoolObject.OnCreatePoolObject` → `CacheData` → `CreateRigidbodiesForRagdoll`), and its root carries a kinematic one too (`CharacterControllerSpawner.CreateRigidbody`: `WithRigidbody` and `WithKinematicOption` in both the player's mode and a bot's, `ApplicationConfig.CharacterControllerSettings`); the recorded corpse lists the root's (`PlayerSuperior(Clone)`, kinematic). As a character is set up its body colliders are moved to `HitCollider` (`Player.SetupBodyPartCollider`); a dead body's are on `Deadbody`, which the blade does not meet. **So a still surface within the sweep's reach takes the blow before a body, whether it stands in front of the body or behind it**; only with none in reach is a body met, the touches of the moving bodies then in their tree's order. The player's own colliders are moving bodies too and can take both places before another's (then nothing is hit).
- **When, and from where** (measured): the sweep runs on a frame before the hands' clips move on, so from the blade where the frame before left it, with the first person's squeeze off; a hit is the frame's that swept.
