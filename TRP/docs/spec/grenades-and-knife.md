# Grenades and the knife

What the hands do with a grenade and with a melee weapon, and what a grenade thrown does. Read from the game's code on 2026-10-17 (`Player.BaseGrenadeHandsController`, `GrenadeHandsController` and its operations, `QuickGrenadeThrowHandsController`; `Player.BaseKnifeController`, `KnifeController`, `QuickKnifeKickController`, `KnifeCollider`; `Grenade`, `Throwable`, `GrenadeFactory`, `ExplosionSharedMethods`; `PlayerInputTranslator`, `GrenadeInputTranslator`, `QuickUseSelectorUtil`; `PhysicalBase`, `Physical`), from the install (the item database; the hands' animators, read by `probe states <bundle>`), and set beside the user's recordings of 2026-10-17 where it says so (`local\traces\courses2`, course 2: recorder 0.10.0). The user's step (2026-10-16): "A grenade and a knife by their keys".

## Taking them in hand

- **A grenade** comes into the hands from a quick slot, as medicine does (`Player.SetQuickSlotItem`, `TryProceed`): the grenade's own slot, bound to `G` in this install (`EBoundItem.ItemG`; `QuickUseSelectorUtil`: the key down chooses, as `4` to `0` choose theirs, and let go takes it), or any of `4` to `0` it is bound to. The player is then given a grenade controller (`GrenadeHandsController`). **There is no quick throw for the player**: the controller for one (`QuickGrenadeThrowHandsController`) is made only for a player driven over the network (`ClientPlayer`). Recorded so: `G` took the F-1 into the hands (`2-grenade-quick`).
- **Which grenade `G` takes** (`PlayerInputTranslator`, `PressThrowGrenade`; `FastAccessGrenadeItemView`): the equipment's top priority grenade. The bar's grenade view sets it as the raid's screen is shown, and again when one is gone, to the first of the vest's and the pockets' grenades (their top level, examined) by kind (`ThrowType`: frag, flash, stun, smoke, gas, incendiary, sonar); the game's grenade selector (the key held) sets it otherwise. Only without one (the view never shown) does the key take a grenade at random, not the same kind twice running where it can help it. The view shows that grenade.
- **The knife** is the sheath's slot, `U` in this install (`SelectKnife`: `SetSlotItem` with the sheath; `KnifeController`). **The quick stroke** is `U` twice (`QuickKnife`, a double click; `PlayerInputTranslator.method_4`): with the hands able to act, no stationary weapon, nothing that cannot be broken off in hand, no knife in hand already, a knife in the sheath and the stamina for a stroke (below), a controller of its own is made (`QuickKnifeKickController`) that strikes once and gives the hands back.
- After a throw the grenade is gone from the inventory (the throw is an inventory operation that takes it out), and the hands go back to what they held before, as after medicine used up; after the quick stroke likewise (its "used" call).

## The grenade's hands

**The rig.** A grenade's item prefab (the F-1's: `assets/content/weapons/f1/weapon_grenade_f1_container.bundle`) is a weapon prefab of its own kind (`GrenadePrefab`), with the arms' animator, the bones the parts thrown hang from (`ThrowingParts`: the grenade, its pin, its lever), and the grenade to be thrown (`GrenadeItself`, a `GrenadeSettings` object with its body, collider and the offset it leaves the hand at). Made in hand (`Setup`): the animator moved on by a sixtieth of a second, the throwing parts shown, the animator told the point of view.

**The animator's parameters** (`probe states <bundle> parameters`, the F-1's): `Active`, `Inventory`, `QuickFire`, `GrenadeFire` and `GrenadeAltFire` (whole numbers: 0 idle, 1 hold, 2 throw), `FireMode` (6 thrown, 7 planted), `Look`, `CheckChamber`, `IdleTime`, `IdleVar`, `UseLeftHand`, `LActionIndex`, `HandReady`, `LeftHandProgress`, `Patrol`, `SpeedDraw`, `Gesture`, `GestureIndex`.

**Its states** (the F-1's `Hands` layer; each clip's length, the marks in it at their share of the state):

| State | Clip | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `SPAWN` | 0.033 s | to `OUT TO IDLE` when `Active` and not `QuickFire` (`FireMode` under 7); to `Q FIRE` with `QuickFire` |
| `OUT TO IDLE` | 1.333 s | the draw: `Draw` sound 0.121, `Safety` 0.595, **`WeapIn` 0.921** (in hand); to `IDLE` at its end |
| `IDLE` | 0.333 s, looping | `IdleStart`; to `FIRE START` when `GrenadeFire` > 0, `ALT FIRE START` when `GrenadeAltFire` > 0, `IDLE TO OUT` when not `Active`, `OPEN INVENTORY`, `LOOK`, `CHECK` |
| `FIRE START`, `ALT FIRE START` | 1.333 s | the pin pulled (`Pin` 0.241, `Lever` 0.483); then the hold |
| `FIRE IDLE`, `ALT FIRE IDLE` | 3.0 s at half speed | the hold; to `FIRE` (`ALT FIRE`) when the value is 2, with `Active` and no inventory; to `FIRE ABORT OUT` (blend 0.25 s) when it falls under 1, or not `Active`; to `FIRE ABORT INV` when the inventory opens |
| `FIRE` | 0.9 s | the throw from above: `Throw` sound 0.301, **`FiringBullet` 0.499** (the grenade lets go), `SndFuse` at the grenade 0.553, `FireEnd` 0.963; to `SPAWN` |
| `ALT FIRE` | 0.9 s | the throw from below: `FiringBullet` at **0.355**, `FireEnd` 0.963 |
| `FIRE ABORT OUT`, `FIRE ABORT INV` | 1.733 s | the pin put back; back to `IDLE` (or the put-away, or the inventory) |
| `IDLE TO OUT` | 1.033 s | the put-away: **`WeapOut` 0.968** |

So a throw takes at least the pin's 1.333 s and then 0.449 s (high) or 0.320 s (low) of the throw's clip from the button going down.

**Its logic** (`GrenadeHandsController` and its operations):

- **Drawn** (`SpawnOperation`): `Active` on; the body's animator told a weapon one cell wide and a reload's float of 1 while it draws; at `WeapIn` the grenade is idle (`Idling`, `FireMode` set to thrown) and the reload's float back to 0.
- **Idle** (`Idling`): the left button down (`TryHighThrow`) pulls the pin for a throw from above (`HighThrowOperation`: the character says its grenade line, `GrenadeFire` to 1, the throw waiting); the right (`TryLowThrow`) for one from below (`LowThrowOperation`, `GrenadeAltFire`). `B` switches to planting a tripwire where the grenade allows it and the character has a kit (`PlantTripwireOperation`: not built).
- **Waiting** (the button held): the button let go (`FinishHighThrow`, `FinishLowThrow`) throws: the value to 2. Nothing else the buttons do is heard. The inventory opened puts the pin back where the grenade may be hidden in a throw (`CanBeHiddenDuringThrow`, the F-1's true): both values to 0 and the grenade idle again. The weapon's key (the hands asked for something else) puts it away at once where it may be hidden, else waits for the throw.
- **The throw**: at `FiringBullet` the value goes back to 0, the grenade in the hand is hidden and the grenade is thrown (`Throw`, next section) with **no time spent on its fuse**: the player's throw always passes 0 (`SpawnGrenade` → `Throw(0, low)`), so the fuse begins when the hand lets go, however long the button was held. Recorded: 0 in all six throws of `courses2`. At `FireEnd` the use is over and the hands go back.
- **Put away** (`Remove`): `Active` off, the body's reload float 1; at `WeapOut` the next thing comes.
- **Who may throw**: only with the hands able to act (`CanThrow`: `StateIsSuitableForHandInput`); the look (`L`) plays `LOOK`.
- **A button pressed during the draw is not heard**: the draw's operation does nothing with the fire input (its base, `ProhibitedCall`), and the key gives its command once as it goes down (`ToggleInputKeyCombination`: `TryHighThrow` down, `FinishHighThrow` up), so a button held through the draw does not pull the pin; it must be pressed again once the grenade is idle.

## The throw

`BaseGrenadeHandsController.Throw` for the player (a bot's differs):

- **The strength** is `EFTHardSettings.GrenadeForce` (11.55 in the install; the recorder's header) times 0.66 for a throw from below, or 1 plus Strength's throwing buff (`StrengthBuffThrowDistanceInc`) for one from above.
- **The direction** is down the hand's drop bone (`-up` of the weapon root, the throw's point of release) five times over, plus, below half the hands' stamina, a random unit vector times how far under half it is; normalised. Without Throwing's elite buff the strength is also multiplied by `lerp(0.4, 1, handsStamina + 0.5)` (1 at half the hands' stamina and above). Since 0.103.0 the high throw's `1 + Strength's throw buff` and Throwing's elite go by the skills' levels (`skills.md`), as a knife's blow goes by Strength's melee buff (`1 +` it, the damage the log writes); a throw's stamina cost (`ConsumeAsMelee` of `GrenadeHighThrow` or `GrenadeLowThrow`, less Throwing's buff, nothing at its elite) is not built.
- **The force** is that direction times that strength, **plus the player's velocity**.
- **The place**: the grenade bone's place plus its turn times the grenade's offset (`GrenadeItself.Offset`); if a line from the body's forward surface to there meets the static world, the place is pulled back to 10 cm short of what it met. Its turn is the grenade bone's.
- **Recorded** (`courses2`, `2-grenade-high`, standing, the hands' stamina full, Strength's buff 0.16 in the header): the force's length is **13.398 = 11.55 × 1.16**; the second, thrown walking, 16.43, the walk's velocity added.
- The grenade is taken out of the inventory (`ThrowGrenade`, an inventory operation), the Throwing skill's action counted, and the grenade made (`CreateAndRegisterGrenade`): a copy of the prefab's `GrenadeItself` at that place and turn, on the `Default` layer, its collisions with the thrower's capsule ignored, given the force as an **impulse** (`AddForce(force, Impulse)`: its velocity becomes force over mass). Recorded: the velocity one physics step on is the force over 0.6, less that step's gravity, to the third figure.

**The grenade's body** (the F-1's, as the game set it up: `grenades_new` of `courses2`, recorder 0.10.0): a rigid body of **0.6 kg**, drag 0.1, angular drag 0.1, continuous dynamic collision, a sleep threshold of 0.005, a top spin of 7, a depenetration speed of 10; a capsule 3.5 cm in radius and 15 cm long along its z, of the material `metal` (bounciness 0.1, both frictions 0.4, both combined by their average). Each collision of any strength (an impulse over √0.2) plays its collision sound and may set off a grenade that explodes on contact (`MinTimeToContactExplode`, -1 for the F-1: never).

**The fuse** is the grenade's `explDelay` less the time already spent (0 for the player's): **3.5 s for the F-1**. Its item has a second field, `ExplDelay`, of 3; the game reads the first. Recorded: all six blasts 3.500 to 3.508 s after the grenade was made (a frame's length at most).

## The blast

`Grenade.Explosion`, `ExplosionSharedMethods.Explosion`:

- The explosion's effect (`ExplosionEffectType`, the F-1's `Grenade_new`) at the grenade, and the grenade object destroyed.
- **Who it reaches**: every player within the largest of twice the greatest distance (`MaxExplosionDistance`, the F-1's 7 m), the blindness's and the contusion's reach.
- **Fragments aimed at a player**: as many as the fragment count over the sphere's area at the player's distance, times 2 (`count / (4π d²) × 2`, at most 3), handed out in pieces of 0.3 to 2, each a shot of the grenade's fragment round from the blast at a point near the player's chest (0.8 m up, scattered by half a metre over the piece). **Random fragments** then make the rest up to 30 (or the count, if fewer): in random directions, three in four turned up for a blast on the ground (under 0.3 m above the terrain), down for one in the air (no terrain within 4 m). The fragments are the thrower's shots in the ballistics. Recorded: 30 a blast, the user too far to be aimed at.
- **Where it is** (read again 2026-10-17, night, `Grenade.Explosion`, `ExplosionSharedMethods.AffectPlayersShared`): the grenade's place raised 8 cm (`_defaultShift`); the effect is shown at the grenade's own place.
- **The shake** (`ForceEffector.GrenadeShake_CO`): for the player's own character, by `inverseLerp(3 × greatest distance, least / 2, distance)` (the F-1's 21 m and 1.5 m) where above nothing: its springs kicked a tenth of a second apart, the waits growing by a tenth each, the kicks shrinking by a quarter, for three quarters of that strength in seconds.
- **Damage to a player** within the greatest distance or the contusion's reach: for each of the body's hit colliders (and armour plates), a line from the blast to it; anything it meets that the fragment cannot pierce stops it (no damage to that collider; the contusion is then reduced by the wall), what it can pierce takes its level plus 2 from the fragment's penetration. The damage is `inverseLerp(MaxExplosionDistance, MinExplosionDistance, distance) × Strength` (the F-1's 7 m, 3 m, 80) times a circular ease-in of the penetration left, the largest of a part's colliders taken for that part, and armour worn for a part damaged by the `ArmorDistanceDistanceDamage` curve. Each collider's line is from the blast to its object's own place (not its shape's middle), and only the world's solid and terrain colliders are tried (`HighPolyWithTerrainMask`: not the bodies). The damage is health's own (`ApplyEnvironmentalDamage` → `Player.ApplyDamageInfo` → `ApplyDamage`), of the kind `GrenadeFragment` (`Grenade.GetDamageInfo`), as any damage with its bleeding's chances, pain and over-damage; armour takes none of it off the body, and is itself damaged by the armour curve (`ApplyExplosionDamageToArmor`: `(1 − inverseLerp(x, y, distance)) × z`, the F-1's 1 to 4 m, 25). **Contusion** (`DoContusion`): where a line reached the body, or the chest's line was stopped, `inverseLerp(y, x, distance) × z` of the contusion's figures (the F-1's 4 m, 1.5 m, 15) from the player's place (its feet); where every line was stopped, times the wall's absorption, `inverseLerp(0.4, 0.01, the wall's thickness)` (`WallContusionAbsorption`; the thickness from where the chest's line enters the wall to where the line back from the chest does); applied above 2, for that many seconds at a strength of 1 (health spec, "A contusion"). Players run by the game's bots are not contused. **The screen shakes** for the local player by how near it was (from three times the greatest distance in to half the least).

## The blast's sound (`Effects.EmitGrenade`, `Effect.Emit`, `Effect.PlaySound`, `BetterAudio.PlayAtPointDistant`, `SuperSourceDistant`; read 2026-10-18)

The grenade's effect (`ExplosionEffectType`: the F-1's `Grenade_new`; the install's grenades name five, `Grenade_new`, `Grenade_new2`, `Grenade_indoor`, `Flashbang` and a smoke's, which has no sound) is emitted at the grenade's own place, upward, as seen from outside (`EmitGrenade`: never the first person's sound), and its sound (`Effect.Sound`) plays 5 cm up from it, by the camera's distance from the grenade, not past the bank's rolloff.

- **A bank played from afar** (`Physical`, as those of `Grenade_new`, `Grenade_new2` and `Grenade_indoor`, 1000 m): through a source of two (`SuperSourceDistant`, `Audio/AudioSourceSuperSpatialBuiltinEQPrefab`: logarithmic from 1 m), the bank's two clips by the distance as its blend points say (`Grenade_new`: close to 10 m, close and distant to 30, distant to 60, distant and far to 200, far beyond), each share on its own source, **after the sound's travel** (`Delay`: the distance over 340 m/s), at the bank's random volume and pitch, through the mixer group of its source kind (`Grenades`: `Guns/Gunshots`). Its spatial blend is passed as `Mathf.Clamp(0.2, 0.9, distance / 30)`, the value first: 0.2 is under the least, 0.9, so **0.9 at any distance** (a tenth of it not placed).
- **Any other** (`Flashbang`'s `zarya`, 300 m): `PlayAtPoint` on its kind's source group (`Grenades` too), at once.

The banks' figures: `grenadeBank` (`Grenade_new`'s and `Grenade_indoor`'s, four clips in each list), `grenadeBank_rgn` (`Grenade_new2`'s, two), volume 1 and pitch 1 without spread; the `Grenades` group's preset in SPT's `settings.json` has an overall volume of 1.

## The blast's effect (`Effects.EmitGrenade`, `Effect.Emit`, `BasicParticleSystemMediator`, `ParticleSystemAdapter`; the effects' bundle; read 2026-10-18)

For a grenade, `Effect.Emit` lays a decal where it went off (the deferred decals where the hard settings have them, else `Decal`); then, the effect being seen from outside, it plays the **mediator's** effect at the grenade's place, plays each of the entry's **older emitters** (`Particles`) from the place moved `ParticlesShift` along the normal (each only where the camera's distance is within its `Distance`), adds a **light** from the pool (where `Light` is set, the camera within `LightMaxDist`) and plays the sound (above).

- **The mediator** (`BasicParticleSystemMediator`) holds copies of one effect and plays them in turn: the next copy moved to the place, turned to `LookRotation(normal)` (for a grenade, `Vector3.up`: the effect's z axis up), and played with every system under it (`Play(withChildren: true)`) but those another of them sets off: a sub-emitter never emits on its own (read from the engine's code, `UnityPlayer.dll` decompiled with Unity's public symbols: `ParticleSystem::MarkAllSubEmitters` sets each sub-emitter's flag of emission stopped, which `IsEmitting` tests, and `Play` on a system so marked only marks it playing, its own emission never started). `Grenade_new`'s and `Grenade_new2`'s have five copies, named `(1)` to `(5)`, listing the same systems with the same modules. A system of a copy played again while it still plays goes on as it was, only moved (`Play`: "If the Particle System is already playing, the system continues to play and this function has no effect", the 2022.3 scripting reference; the install is of 2022.3.43f1). The engine's `Play` agrees (decompiled): it sets the time to nothing and draws the start delay only for a system in its stopped state. `PROVISIONAL`: a system whose emission has ended with particles still alive taken as not yet stopped (where the engine stops one was not found).
- **`Grenade_new`** (the F-1, RGD-5, M67, the VOGs and the V40): seventeen systems under its root, each with its own place and turn below it. The fireball: `flash` (one particle of 3 m for half a second, `CustomFX/HDRFireParticle`), `flash_area` (one, drawn by nothing: its light alone) and `explosion_center` (four to seven of 0.2 to 1.5 m on a sheet of 12 by 7, `Particles/HSL Additive`, in the effect's own space). The smoke: the root (seven of 5 to 15 m from a cone, a fifth to half a second, on a sheet of 4 by 4, `Particles/Smoke Bumped`), `Effect Smoke Distorted` (15 to 25 of 4 to 6 m, out at 10 to 30 m/s, for 1 to 3 s), `Smoke Explosion stretched` (ten stretched along their way, from 0.84 m below the ground up within 18°, `Particles/Smoke Bumped`), and `long smoke` (10 to 20 of 1 to 4 m, for 10 to 30 s, from 0.3 s on, `Particles/Smoke Distorted`). The dust along the ground: `DustAngle10`, `DustAngle45` and `BlackParticlesAnge15` (two bursts each, stretched, on sheets of 6 by 12, `Global Fog/Alpha Blended Lighted`). The sparks and the ash: `Drop Sparks` (45 to 80 stretched, `Legacy Shaders/Particles/Additive`) and `grenade_ash` (20 to 50 bits, `Particles/SuperAdditive`). And pieces of concrete (`Drop Concrete`, `Drop Concrete no subs`: meshes that bounce off the world, the first setting off a puff of dust, `drop_dust`, where each hits: its sub-emitter on collision, taking the parent's duration), the shock wave (`DistortionWave`, a mesh drawn by `Effects/Explosions/DistortionFresnel`), the heat's shimmer (`HeatDistortion`, `Effects/Explosions/Distortion/ParticlesCutOut`) and two point lights, off, that the lights modules of `flash` and `flash_area` copy.
- **`Grenade_new2`** (the RGN and RGO): thirteen of the same kinds (`Effect SmallGrenade Expl` its root; no `Drop Concrete` with its dust, `Smoke Explosion stretched` or `flash_area`).
- **`Flashbang`** (the Zarya and the M7290): no mediator; seven older emitters (`ParticleSystemAdapter`, the kind a bullet's impact on a surface uses: each particle emitted with a lifetime, speed and size from ranges of the emitter's own (a table of draws made once and gone through in turn), its turn drawn or fixed, its colour the emitter's; its way by the entry's kind, the Flashbang's the normal plus a random unit vector, or a random way over half a sphere) and the pool's light (colour (1, 0.94, 0.82), a range of 35 m, an intensity of 12, a second, with shadows, within 350 m).
- **The order things are drawn in** (Unity's documentation): each system's renderer is a transparent thing of its material's queue (its shader's `Queue` tag: the smoke bumped 3001, the other drawn ones 3000); the engine draws such things by queue, then far to near by the distance of their bounds' middle, its `sortingFudge` added. Within a system, by its sort mode (`m_SortMode`): as born (none, and 3, the youngest in front) or far to near from the camera (1, by distance; the most of the explosion's). `PROVISIONAL`: a system's middle taken as its particles' mean place.
- **A stretched billboard** (render mode 1, Unity's documentation): drawn along the particle's way, its width its size, its length its size times `m_LengthScale` plus its speed times `m_VelocityScale`. `PROVISIONAL`: the particle at its head and the rest behind it (a negative length scale, as most of the explosion's have, then reaching ahead), the picture's across along it.

**The shaders**, each the game's own (read and disassembled, the variant with soft particles): every pass tests the depth (`ZTest LEqual`) and writes none; where a shader fades softly, its alpha is times the scene's depth less the particle's at that pixel times `_InvFade`, held within 0 and 1.

- `Particles/Smoke Distorted` (the fumes' shader: firing spec, "The muzzle's fumes").

**The concrete, its dust, the sparks and the flash's lights** (read 2026-10-19 with the engine's code: particles spec, "Meeting the world", "Set off by another", "Its lights"). `Drop Concrete` and `Drop Concrete no subs` (chips drawn as billboards by `Legacy Shaders/Transparent/Cutout/Bumped Diffuse`, in the G-buffer: the picture times `_Color`, cut where its alpha is under `_Cutoff`, its normal from the bump picture, lit there as any surface), `Drop Sparks`, `grenade_ash`, `Effect Smoke Distorted` and `long smoke` meet the world (dampen 0.2, no bounce, a twentieth of their life lost at each meeting, killed under 0.17 m/s); each chip of `Drop Concrete` that meets it sets off `drop_dust` there (taking the chip's system's duration). `flash` and `flash_area` light their one particle each with copies of the effect's two point lights (`Point Light`: colour (1, 0.82, 0.53), range 7 m, intensity 7.5; `Point Light area`: (0.98, 0.52, 0.23), 6.17 m, 2), each by its curves over the particle's life.

**The shock wave and the heat's shimmer** (their shaders read and disassembled 2026-10-20). Each shader has two passes: a grab of the frame as it stands (`GrabPass` with no name: the frame copied afresh for each thing drawn), then the thing drawn sampling that copy at a place moved by its own sums, blended `SrcAlpha OneMinusSrcAlpha`, the depth tested (`LEqual`) and not written, both faces drawn.

- **`DistortionWave`**: one mesh particle (Unity's built-in sphere: particles spec, "Drawn"), born 0.05 s after the blast where the effect is, of 7 to 8 m (the sphere's metre times that: 3.5 to 4 m in radius at its whole), living 0.3 s; its size over life from 0.33 to 0.87 at 23% of its life and whole at its end; its alpha from nothing to whole by 3% of its life, 0.145 at 48%, nothing at its end. Its material's queue is 1000: in the engine's deferred path a shader with no deferred pass is drawn among the opaque things of the forward pass, after the deferred light, so the wave's copy holds the lit opaque scene alone; the sky is drawn after it, only where nothing was, and every transparent thing (the smoke, the fire) after that. `Effects/Explosions/DistortionFresnel`: at each corner, from the way to the camera (in the mesh's own axes) and the corner's normal, `f = (1 − |n · v|)^_FresnelPow` and a weight `saturate((1 − _FresnelR0) f + _FresnelR0)`; at each pixel the copy is read where the pixel is on the screen moved by that weight times `_BumpAmt` pixels times the particle's alpha, to the right and down alike, and drawn times the particle's colour with the copy's alpha times the particle's. With the material's figures (`_FresnelPow` 11.6, `_FresnelR0` 59.9, `_BumpAmt` 5) the weight is whole everywhere: what is behind the sphere is shown as it is 5 pixels to the right and below, blended by the particle's alpha (twice where both of its faces are seen).
- **`HeatDistortion`**: billboards in the effect's own axes, born 0.1 s after the blast, two at once and two a second while it emits (0.5 s), of 10 to 20 m, each living 0.5 s, turned at random, on a sheet of 8 by 8 pictures run once over its life; its alpha from nothing to whole by 6% of its life and from whole at 78% to nothing at its end; queue 3000, among the transparent things (its sorting fudge −200). `Effects/Explosions/Distortion/ParticlesCutOut`: its picture (`DuDvHeat`, 1024 square, by the sheet's cell) read as two parts, its alpha times its red and its green, each from −1 to 1; `w` is the sum of the two parts' size less 0.01, times 5, held within 0 and 1; where `w` is under a half nothing is drawn (the cut-out); else the copy is read where the pixel is moved by the two parts times `_BumpAmt` pixels times `w`, the particle's alpha and the soft fade (the scene's depth less the particle's, times `_InvFade`, within 0 and 1), over the corner's distance from the camera in metres (`_BumpAmt` 50: 50 pixels at a metre), and drawn with an alpha of `w` times the copy's.
- `PROVISIONAL`: the copy's alpha taken as the frame's own (the scene's: whole).

**The scorch** (`Effect.Emit`, `DeferredDecalRenderer`, `DeferredDecalMeshHelper`; the decal renderer's figures from the install; its shader read and disassembled; 2026-10-20).
