# Skills

What Tarkov's skills change in the character and how they are earned, where each figure lives, and how far the app has them. Written 2026-10-22 and filled 2026-10-23 from three sources, each named where it is used. **The client's code** (`local\decomp\src`: `SkillManager`, `Skill`, `BaseSkill`, `WeaponSkill`, `Mastering`, and every place a buff is read), which holds the per-level figures and the rules, and **wins wherever the sources differ**. **The install's own figures** (`globals.json`: `config.SkillsSettings` and the `Skill*` keys), read from the install on 2026-10-23 and the same as the `4.1.6` tag of SPT's published `server-csharp`, read the day before. **The Tarkov wiki**, whose host refuses automated reading (HTTP 402 on 2026-10-23; shut by the network policy on 2026-10-22), so what is had of it came through a search engine's summaries of its pages and of guides that quote it: set beside the code in "The wiki beside the code"; a figure with no code behind it is `PROVISIONAL`. **Compared with the game: nothing.** The install's levels change what is heard (Covert Movement lowers the footsteps by 49 percent at the level recorded) and other things; this spec gives what each skill changes and how it is earned, the earnable skills being a switch, off as it comes.

## What a skill is

**A level** is the skill's progress over 100, whole (`BaseSkill.Level`; `SkillExpPerLevel` 100), the progress held between 0 and 5,100: levels 0 to 51, and 51 (above 50: `Skill.IsEliteLevel`) is **elite**. The character's progress lives in the profile (`Skills.Common`, each `Progress`). **A buff** is a figure the level sets (`Skill.UpdateRules`), by one of three rules (`SkillManager.FloatBuff`): *a level* (`PerLevel(f)`: the level times `f`), *to a top* (`Max(f)`: the level times `f / 50`, so `f` at level 50) or *its own* (`Custom`: a formula of the level). The rule is worked with the level held at 50; above 50 an **elite rule** runs after it, which either puts a figure in its place (`Elite(x)`) or turns on a yes-or-no buff (`BooleanBuff`, off below elite). A buff with no rule stays 0 (or its `Default`). A skill with no buffs is **locked** (`Skill.Locked`): shown, never earned.

**A boost to a skill** (`BaseSkill.Buff`, levels added for a while by the game's stimulants: built since 0.135.0, `health.md`, "Buffs") is added to the level the rules use (`SummaryLevel`: the level plus the boost, each buff held to what takes the skill from nought to 60 and rounded, at most 60 where the boosts add up above nought and 51 otherwise), so a boost can make a skill elite for its time. In the app the boosts at work are taken from the health each frame (`skills::stimulated`) and every buff is worked from the summary levels; with `SKILLS` off they do nothing.

**What the skill page shows** is each buff at the character's level, the figure a player reads ("49 percent": Covert Movement's 1.2% a level is 49% at level 41; the recorded profile has the skill at level 40, which is 48%, so the two are not yet settled). So what is carried is the per-level figure and the level, never the page's number.

**The skills the client has** (`SkillManager`'s list, `DisplayList`, 43, and two for bots; `GetSkill` looks only there): Physical (Endurance, Strength, Vitality, Health, Stress Resistance, Metabolism, Immunity), Mental (Perception, Intellect, Attention, Charisma), Combat (twelve weapon classes, Throwing, Aim Drills, Troubleshooting), Practical (Surgery, Covert Movement, Search, Mag Drills, Light Vests, Heavy Vests, Weapon Maintenance, Crafting, Hideout Management; and First Aid, Field Medicine, Night Ops, Silent Ops, Auctions, Clean Operations, Shadow Connections, Task Performance, all locked). **Made but left out of the list**, so that no profile's level reaches them and nothing earns them: Recoil Control (whose buff therefore stays 0), Sniping, Prone Movement, Lockpicking, Weapon Modding, Advanced Modding, Free Trading, Barter, and the five BEAR and five USEC skills (locked). **Memory** is gone from the client; the install still holds its figures (`Memory`: `AnySkillUp` 5, `SkillProgress` 0.4), which nothing reads.

## The skills system

The skills system: a page of the options tab where each skill's level is set from 0 to 51, a switch for the system as the others have, and the buffs applied everywhere this spec says a skill reaches; earned points are saved and the options tab follows a skill levelled. So: **the levels are set, never earned** here ("Earning", below); a host game may change them in play, and the tab shows the change and keeps it.

- **The levels** (`trp_sim::skills::SkillLevels`): each skill's progress, 0 to 5,100, a level a hundred, whole levels on the tab, a host's fractions kept. Set on the options tab's `SKILLS` page (a bar a skill, 0 to 51, `ELITE` at 51; `ALL TO 0`), kept in the app's file (`"skills"`: each skill not at 0 by the game's name, with its progress), never read from the profile. For a run: `--weapon-skill <0..51>` (every weapon class), `--strength`, `--endurance` (0 to 51), `--skill Name=level` (any, by the game's name); `--skill-at frame:Name=level` sets one in play on a drawn frame, as a host would.
- **In play** (the app's `skills::Skills`, a resource a host game changes): a change is put on at once on what reads it as it goes (`Skill.LevelChanged`), and on a weapon as it is next drawn, as the game's (`SyncWithCharacterSkills`, `UpdateWeaponVariables`); the tab shows it and the app's file keeps it (the tab's own changes are the run's at once, kept as the bar is let go). A run begun again after a death keeps the levels as they stand.
- **The switch** (`SKILLS`, under `SIMPLE`, on as the app comes, restart required): off, every buff is an untrained character's whatever the levels say.
- **The buffs, where they act** (`trp_sim::skills::Buffs`, each by the game's rule, held at 50, its elite above): Endurance on stamina, its two elites too (`StaminaSettings::set_skill_buffs`: the legs', the arms' and the breath's capacity, their recovery, a jump's cost); Strength on the top speeds and the jump (`set_strength`), the weight's limits (`WeightLimits`: the overweight limits times 1 and its lift, the inertia's raised by `InertiaLimitsStep` a level; `Movement::set_weight_limits`), aiming's cost, a high throw's strength, a knife's blow (its damage, as the log writes it), and at elite the weapons weighing nothing (the carried sum, the GEAR and HEALTH tabs' figure, whose maximum takes the lift too); the health skills on health (`HealthSkills::of`, Vitality's and Metabolism's elites too: a bleeding stopping by itself after its `EliteVitalityDuration`, no damage from strong dehydration or exhaustion); Covert Movement on the steps and the kit (`StepSounds::set_covert`: each place the game multiplies by it, with the game's soft surfaces and how quietly one walks; and the stance panel's noise level); a weapon's class on its reload and draw speeds, its ergonomics (the aiming speed, the sway and the ergonomic weight with them) and its recoil (`RecoilForce`: the forces less its share), Aim Drills on the aiming speed, from the draw (`HandsRig::take_skills`); Throwing's elite on a throw's strength; Mag Drills on a round's and a check's time, its elites (rounds quicker after the first; a magazine known on being put into one's own things); Surgery on health (as before). Every buff is 0, or leaves its system as it was, at level 0: the kept regression is unchanged to the byte (`after133`, `after132`'s).
- **Not acting yet**, what they act on not being built: Throwing's cost (a throw's stamina is not paid), Perception (the look's finding sphere), Troubleshooting (malfunctions), Light and Heavy Vests (nothing hits the player's armour), Weapon Maintenance (wear), Metabolism's ill effects' time, the mounted ergonomics (no mounting); and out of a raid, Intellect, Charisma, Crafting, Hideout Management. Mastering is by weapon family since 0.154.0 ("Weapon mastering").

## Weapon mastering

Weapon mastering, laid out as the skills are, by weapon family: a level per family (the install's groups) set in the options beside the skills' levels, the inventory's MASTERING page as the game's, each weapon reading its own family's level, and `--mastering` kept as an override for the whole run. ## The stiff draw

The rest of the skills are built, apart from Hideout Management and Crafting (`backlog.md`, its step 1). Read from the client:

- **Who has it** (`SkillManager.GetWeaponInfo`, `WeaponBuffsInfo.StiffDraw`): Aim Drills' elite (`DrawElite`) or the elite of the class of the firearm drawn (the class's own `DrawElite`). It is taken as the firearm's controller is made, that is as it is drawn, and given to the weapon's motion (`ProceduralWeaponAnimation.InitWeaponData`); a skills' change while it is held (`SyncWithCharacterSkills`) copies the other figures but not this one. The weapon's motion keeps it until the next firearm is drawn: hands that hold none (a rangefinder's, which aims) keep the last one's, and before any it is off.
- **When** (`ProceduralWeaponAnimation.IsAiming`'s setter): as the sights come up with it (the hands controller tells the motion only when its aim changes), the breath is held stiff until 3 s on by the engine's clock (`BreathEffector.StiffUntill`). Sights brought up again begin the 3 s afresh; sights let down do not end them.
- **What** (`BreathEffector.Process`, each physics step; it comes before a held breath and overrides it): with `r` the seconds left, the breath keeps `clamp(1 − r, 0.3, 1)` of itself, the floor 0.5 with a tremor or a broken arm: the floor for 2 s, then up to whole over the last second. The breath's depth is that share times the intensity (the stance's, and a mount's where the breath runs mounted), the small pushes on the roll and yaw are that share of their strength, and the tremor's pace is cut to it; the breath's pace and its reach to the camera stay as the last breath had them, as with a held breath. The wander from the hip is untouched. So tired arms still shake, by a third as much: the wiki's "steady aim at any stamina" is not the code's.
- **The clock** (mine): the game reads one `Time.time` for every physics step of a frame; ours counts the seconds left down by each frame's length after the frame's steps (`Breath::pass_frame`), so a frame's steps see the game's figure at a steady frame rate, and with frames of changing length the last frame's length stands for the next's. A sights' change an obstacle makes at a frame's end starts it a frame on, as the game's.
- **Built**: `trp_sim::Breath` (`set_stiff_draw`, `sights_up`, `pass_frame`, `stiffness`); `Hands::set_stiff_draw`, given at the draw by the app's `HandsRig::take_skills` (`WeaponBuffs::stiff_draw`), under the `SKILLS` switch. Measured in the simulation (`hands::a_stiff_draw_holds_the_breath_for_three_seconds`, the stock AKM standing): the weapon's rise and fall 0.30 of a plain rig's from 0.5 to 2 s after the sights come up, 1.00 from 6 to 8 s. **Not compared with the game**: no recording has an elite skill. ## How a skill is earned (the code's rules, the install's figures)

**An action gives points.** The game raises an action where something happens (a step's end, a shot, a reload), with an amount (metres, one, a count, seconds), and each skill that listens takes the amount times its factor (`SkillAction.Factor`). The factor is the skill's figure in the install times **`SkillProgressRate` 0.4**, or for the weapon classes and mastering times **`WeaponSkillProgressRate` 1**. A skill whose figure is a formula of the movement (`SkillDependency`) takes the formula's figure times 0.4.

**Points pass through the session's fatigue** (`Skill.UseEffectiveness`, `SkillManager.GetEffectiveness`), in steps of at most one point, each step times the skill's **effectiveness** of the moment, which goes by the points this skill has taken in the session: below `SkillFreshPoints` (1), `SkillFreshEffectiveness` 1.3; below that plus `SkillPointsBeforeFatigue` (1), 1; then `SkillFatiguePerPoint` 0.6 to the power 1 + the points − 2 (0.6 at 2, 0.36 at 3, 0.0101 at 10, 0.006 at 11; corrected 0.201.0), never under `SkillMinEffectiveness` 0.0001 (from 20). The session's points are counted after the fatigue (the step's worth), and the effectiveness is worked out again only when their whole part rises; the fatigue's 200 s run from the last award that left it at 1 or less, and none runs before the first such award (`_fatigueTimer` begins at the most a float holds). Once an award has left it at 1 or less, the fatigue lasts until **`SkillFatigueReset` 200 seconds** (the engine's clock) pass with no award to that skill: then the effectiveness is 1 again and the session's count at most 1. The count is kept in the profile between raids (`PointsEarnedDuringSession`; whether the server clears it after a raid: `PROVISIONAL`). Then the hideout's bonuses (`BonusController.Calculate`, none in the app).

**The first nine levels are cheaper** (`CalculateExpOnFirstLevels`, `CalculateLevelExp`): below level 9 a level takes (level + 1) × 10 points instead of 100 (10 to reach 1, 90 to go from 8 to 9; 450 to reach 9), and a point is worth 10 / (level + 1) of progress. **At elite the skill stops listening** (`UpdateRules`: above 50, `Unsubscribe`): nothing more is earned. **No skill falls**: the install's `SkillAtrophy` is false and the client reads no such rule.

**Some skills are fed by others** (`SkillManager.SkillProgress`, raised with each award as the action gave it, before the giver's fatigue; the fed share then passes the taker's own fatigue): Health takes 0.5 × 0.4 = 0.2 of Strength's, Endurance's and Vitality's points (`Health.SkillProgress`); Charisma 0.2 of Intellect's, Attention's and Perception's (`Charisma.SkillProgressInt`, `...Atn`, `...Per`); Surgery 0.2 of First Aid's and Field Medicine's (none: both locked); Intellect 0.2 of Lockpicking's (none) and a fifteenth of Crafting's (`CraftingPointsToInteligence` 15, times 0.4, no fatigue). The install's `DependentSkillRatios` (0.25 to Health, 0.2 to Charisma, 0.066 to Intellect) are not the client's: it reads none of them.

**The server's skills**: Crafting, Hideout Management and Weapon Maintenance cannot be earned in the client (`ClientAuthorizedSkill`: "can not be progressed locally"); the hideout and the repairs earn them.

## The wiki beside the code

Read on 2026-10-23 through search summaries of the English wiki's pages (each about 85 days old in the search index, so from about late July 2026; no page's own date seen) and of TarkovForge, a site with a page a skill (undated); a guide with invented elites (kappaguide) was set aside. The summaries garble a sign now and then (a "+" for a reduction): the code settles the sign. **Since the snapshot** the live game's skills level "up to 60% faster" (Battlestate, 2026-10-02, as quoted by a news site): the SPT client read is older, and the figures above are its own.

**The same in both**: Endurance's four figures, its elite's 70% and its breath free of energy; Strength's jump, carry, melee, throw and aiming figures and the weightless weapons; Vitality's bleeding odds; Health's fracture, energy and hydration; Stress Resistance's pain and the berserk; Metabolism's three; Immunity's poison, painkiller and the elite's two nine-in-tens; Perception's radius; Attention's three; Charisma's; the weapon classes' reload, swap, recoil and ergonomics, and the doubled mastering; Throwing's cost and its elite; Aim Drills' speed and sound; Troubleshooting's speed and the elite's known kind; Covert Movement's five, the elite as quiet everywhere at 60%; Surgery's four; the vests'; Weapon Maintenance's wear; Mag Drills' elites; every elite worth the per-level figure × 50, as the code holds the level at 50. The rules: 10 points for level 1 and 10 more a level up to 100 (550 to level 10), elite at 51, boosts to 60, the 200 s reset with the count back to 1, no atrophy (gone since 0.8, 2018). Recoil Control and Memory removed (the wiki: in 0.14.5, 2024-04-03), Heavy Machine Guns, Underbarrel Launchers and Melee doing nothing, the locked skills unbuilt: as the code. ## Earning

Every source of experience the game has is covered, with notices where the game gives them (healing's among them), the skill list following each award; the overall level is built and can be set in the skills menu; a switch `SIMPLE XP GAIN`, on as it comes, turns off Tarkov's diminishing returns so every award is whole; and experience counts as it is earned, not at a run's end. This ends the earlier "never earning" (above).

- **The rules** (`trp_sim::earning`): an action's skills and amounts (`awards`, the table above, every figure the install's `SkillsSettings` or the client's own where it has its own: the vests' 0.05 and 0.025); an award (`award`: nothing at elite, the fed skill's share first, then the fatigue, the cheaper first levels and the hold at 5,100), a level reached told once for an award that passes several (`SkillLevelChanged` with the last). Mastering: a point a hit (two where the class is elite by its summary level), no fatigue. Metabolism's actions from the health's events (`metabolism`): by the point, or with `SIMPLE XP GAIN` once an item used (below).
- **What raises them in the app** (`experience`): a walk's and a sprint's end on the ground by the game's pedometer (a sample a second of the level distance, credited to the state at the sample; a walk quiet where the step's covert efficiency is over 0.9 and the next state neither a sprint nor prone: `CovertNoiseLevel` 0); a throw; damage not self-inflicted, a bleeding, an intoxication, a pain off painkillers (the health's events); the seconds at low health (`LowEdgeHealth` begun to residual) and under a stimulant's ill effect; energy and hydration gained (a new event of the health, `EnergyGained`, `HydrationGained`, by the change after their limits), and an item's use over with its gains kept (`Fed`, the whole use's: told once, as the game's controller out of a raid applies an item); a body part restored (Surgery); a thing taken the first time, not ammunition or money (Perception); a search's finds (Attention, on a body Search too) and a search's end with its container still there; a magazine's rounds loaded and unloaded and its first check, in the inventory or in the hands; a magazine's reload begun (`MagazineSwap`), the rack that feeds (`RackBegun`), and the hands' own actions where the game's operations raise them (`FirearmEvent::Earn`: a tube's, a cylinder's and barrels' reloads begun, a tube's rounds committed one by one, a cylinder's end; a round into a chamber or out of one for the inventory, the chamber key's round to the rig among them, a tube's empty chamber, the open bolt shut; Mag Drills' rounds into a tube or a chamber), a malfunction's repair begun or a misfire put right; the player's bullet on a living dummy (the class of the weapon in hand, Aim Drills by the fast-aim timer, the family's mastering point).
- **Not raised, nothing on the range does it**: a knife's blow on a body (Strength, Melee: the knife meets no dummy), the vests (nothing hits the player's armour), Perception's raid end (no raid end), Intellect's and Attention's actions out of a raid, the hideout's.
- **The skills' buffs** follow a level reached (`skills::apply`, which now puts nothing on again for a change that leaves every buff as it was: points within a level). The options' file keeps what play earns at most every two seconds (`keep_in_play`).
- **The SKILLS tab** shows the points as they come (the list drawn again with them), each skill's leveling speed its own session's (the arrows and the tooltip's line), "Most recent" by when each last earned (`LastCall`), and the character's experience above (below).
- **The notices** (`SkillLevelUpNotification`: "{0} skill leveled up to {1}", the skill's name in the install's words; `MasteringLevelUpMessage`: "{0} mastering leveled up to {1}", the family's name as it is, its level the game's 0 to 2), each with a row of the HUD page's NOTIFICATIONS (`SKILL LEVEL UP`, `MASTERING LEVEL UP`).
- **The switches**: `EXPERIENCE` (FEATURES, CHARACTER; on, restart required; needs `SKILLS`): off, nothing is earned. `SIMPLE XP GAIN` (the same; on; needs `EXPERIENCE`): a departure from Tarkov on purpose: an award skips the session's fatigue (its whole worth, never 1.3 nor less: the tab shows no arrow), and the looting's and searching's share of a kind's experience is whole every time (not `loot_attempts`' falling one). **Metabolism earns once an item used**: eight points for each of energy and hydration the use gained and kept, nothing by a rate's gains (an MRE's energy rate after it, a stimulant's), as the game's controller out of a raid gives it; by the point and unheld, an MRE's 70 energy were 560 points, ten levels from 0, where the game's fatigue holds them to about 12.6.

**Compared with the game: nothing**; every rule from the client's code (`Skill.OnTrigger`, `UseEffectiveness`, `CalculateExpOnFirstLevels`, `SkillManager`'s actions and figures, `Player`'s raisers, `Pedometer`), the figures from the install; the tests set the code's figures beside the rules (`earning`, `experience`).
