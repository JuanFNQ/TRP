# The particle system

Unity's particle system (its "Shuriken"), as the game's effects use it and as this work runs it. Written 2026-10-18 for the muzzle's fumes (`firing.md`, "The muzzle's fumes"), the first of the game's effects to need it; the heat haze is on it too, drawn into the camera's distortion (`firing.md`, "The muzzle's heat haze"); a grenade's explosion is on it (`grenades-and-knife.md`, "The blast's effect"); the muzzle's particle prefabs are still to come (below, "Not built"). (The muzzles' sparks are no Unity particles but the game's own renderer: `firing.md`, "The muzzle's sparks".)

**Where the knowledge comes from.** The stored form is read from the install (every module's figures are in the bundles with their type trees: `probe dump <bundle> <its id>`). What each module does is **Unity's documentation** (the manual's pages for the particle system's modules, and the scripting reference for `ParticleSystem.Emit`, `EmitParams`, `MinMaxCurve`, `MinMaxGradient` and `Gradient`): the engine's own code is not published, so where the documentation leaves the rule open it is said here and marked `PROVISIONAL`. The game's own code is read where it drives a system (`MuzzleManager`, `MuzzleFume`).

## What a system is made of

A `ParticleSystem` object, beside a `ParticleSystemRenderer` on the same game object, the renderer naming a material (and a second for trails).

- **The system's own figures**: its duration (`lengthInSec`), whether it loops, its simulation space (`moveWithTransform`: 0 local, 1 world, 2 a custom transform's), its scaling mode, and its modules, each with an `enabled` flag.
- **The main module** (`InitialModule`, always on): the start values a particle is born with (lifetime, speed, colour, size, rotation, each a curve or a range: below), the gravity modifier and the most particles alive (`maxNumParticles`).
- **The modules over a particle's life**, each read at the particle's age as a share of its lifetime (0 at birth, 1 at its end): size (`SizeModule`), rotation (`RotationModule`, an angular speed), colour (`ColorModule`), the speed's limit (`ClampVelocityModule`), a force (`ForceModule`, in the world's or the system's axes), the turn by speed (`RotationBySpeedModule`: an angular speed by where the particle's speed is in its range), the picture's sheet (`UVModule`), and the rest (below, "Not built").
- **Its own emission** (`EmissionModule`): a rate a second and bursts, each at a time of the system's cycle with a count, a number of cycles (0 for ever), an interval between them and a chance; **its shape** (`ShapeModule`): where on it a particle is born and which way it goes (a cone's base, a sphere, half of one), with the shape's own place, turn and size, a share of the way at random (`randomDirectionAmount`), a share out from the middle (`sphericalDirectionAmount`) and a place scattered (`randomPositionAmount`).
- **The renderer**: how a particle is drawn (`m_RenderMode`: 0 a billboard facing the camera, others stretched, flat or a mesh), the billboard's alignment (`m_RenderAlignment`: 0 to the view), the order particles are drawn in (`m_SortMode`: 0 none), the least and most size on the screen (`m_MinParticleSize`, `m_MaxParticleSize`, as shares of the view), and its material.

**A value that can vary** (`MinMaxCurve`): by its mode (`minMaxState`) a constant (0, `scalar`), a curve over the share of life times `scalar` (1), between two curves (2), or between two constants (3, `minScalar` and `scalar`); where it is between two, each particle has its own draw for where between. Rotations are kept in radians (5 degrees a second is stored as 0.0873).

**A colour that can vary** (`MinMaxGradient`): a colour (0), a gradient (1), between two colours (2), between two gradients (3), or a random colour of a gradient (4). **A gradient** has up to eight colour keys and eight alpha keys apart (`key0` to `key7`, their colours and their alphas; `ctime`, `atime` their times in 65535ths; `m_NumColorKeys`, `m_NumAlphaKeys` how many are used), blended (`m_Mode` 0) or stepped (1); before the first key the first's value, after the last the last's.

## What happens to a particle

- **Born** (`ParticleSystem.Emit(EmitParams, 1)`, as the game's code emits): each value the call gives (place, velocity, lifetime, colour, size, rotation, its own random seed) is the particle's; any other is the main module's start value. A world-space system takes the place as it is.
- **A system played** (`Play`): after its start delay (drawn), each burst at its time of the cycle (again each interval, so many cycles, each by its chance) and the rate's share over each step (its fractions carried) give so many particles, each born on the shape with the main module's start values at the cycle's share (its speed along the shape's way); the duration ends the cycle, which starts again for a looping system; a system that loops not is done emitting. A world-space system's particles are born where its object puts them and stay there; a local one's are kept in its own axes and go where it goes. `PROVISIONAL`: the object's size moves where on the shape they are born and not their speed, as the scaling mode `Shape` does (`scalingMode` is not read); a cone's way leans out from its axis by its angle in proportion to how far out on the base the particle is born; a sphere's places are even over its volume, a cone's over its base's area, within the shell `radiusThickness` leaves.
- **The force** is a speed added each second, in its own axes (turned into the system's where they differ); `randomizePerFrame` draws it afresh each step. **The turn by speed** is an angular speed read from its curve where the speed is between its range's ends. **Rotation's direction** (`randomizeRotationDirection`): that share of particles turn the other way.
- **The sheet** (`UVModule`, its grid mode): the frame its curve gives at the share of life (its cycles repeating it), plus the start frame, of so many cells (a row's, where it goes by rows: one row, or one at random); frame 0 the top left, left to right then down. `PROVISIONAL`: with more than one cycle, the curve's share repeated (`fract(share × cycles)`) rather than the curve read at a repeated time.
- **Each step**, by the time passed: the particle ages; its velocity is limited (below); it moves by its velocity; it turns by its angular speed. It dies at the end of its lifetime. Gravity is the modifier times the world's gravity (none where the modifier is 0).
- **What is drawn**: its size the size it was born with times the size module's at its share of life; its colour the colour it was born with times the colour module's; its turn the one it was born with plus what it has turned since.
- **The speed's limit** (Limit Velocity over Lifetime): a speed above the limit (`magnitude`, a value over life) is taken down by the `dampen` share, and `drag` (a value over life; times the particle's size, its speed, or both, where `multiplyDragByParticleSize`, `multiplyDragByParticleVelocity` say) slows it. The documentation gives `dampen` as a share of the speed above the limit taken off without saying over what time; this work took it off over each 1/30 of a second (the speed above the limit falls as (1 − dampen) to the power of 30 times the time passed), and the engine does the same (decompiled 2026-10-18, `ClampVelocityModule::Update`; `findings.md`, "the engine's checks"). **The engine's drag** (the same reading): the speed less the drag times the time passed, never below nothing, the drag first times the particle's cross-section, π times half its size squared, where `multiplyDragByParticleSize`, and times the speed squared where `multiplyDragByParticleVelocity`. This work's drag is the engine's since 0.68.3 (until then a slowing of drag times the speed a second; no system drawn has a drag). `PROVISIONAL`: the size the cross-section is of taken as the particle's size as drawn.
- **External forces** act through wind zones and force fields only: none are in the range, and the module does nothing.
- **The most particles**: a particle emitted when the system already has its most alive is not made.

## Meeting the world (`CollisionModule`, of the world's kind)

Read from the engine's code (`UnityPlayer.dll` decompiled with Unity's public symbols, 2026-10-19: `CollisionModule::Update`, `WorldCollision`, `PerformPlaneCollisions`, `PhysXRaycast::ParticleIntersect`). The explosion's concrete, sparks, ash and smoke have it on (`type` 1, the world's; `quality` 0, high).

- **When**: each step, once the particles have moved, each particle is tried against the scene's colliders on the module's layers (`collidesWith`) over the move it has just made. At high quality the physics engine is asked: a sphere swept through its scene (a single sweep, asking for the surface's normal and, where the sphere starts inside a shape, the least way out of it).
- **The sphere**: its radius `r` is half the particle's size times the module's `radiusScale` (the module halves the scale it keeps before it uses it). The sweep is of a sphere of `r` less 0.151 of `r`, from where the particle was to where it is and 0.151 of `r` further.
- **Where it met**: the particle's place where the swept sphere's middle met the surface, drawn back along the way by that 0.151 of `r`; where the sphere began inside a shape, pushed out of it along the normal.
- **The response**: what was left of the move past the surface, and the velocity, are each reflected about the surface's normal, kept by one less the dampen (`m_Dampen` at the particle's share of life), and their parts along the normal kept by the bounce (`m_Bounce`): with no bounce a particle that strikes slides on along the surface. Its age is put on by the lifetime's loss (`m_EnergyLossOnCollision` times its lifetime). It dies where that ends its life, or where its speed coming in was below `minKillSpeed` or above `maxKillSpeed` (the engine compares their squares).
- **What it sets off**: each particle that met a surface on the step and was alive before it did sets off the system's sub-emitters of the collision kind (below).
- A local system's particles are met in the world's axes and put back into the system's.

`PROVISIONAL`: the medium and low qualities (a cache of the scene in cubes, `voxelSize`), the module's planes, its force on what it meets, `maxCollisionShapes`, `collidesWithDynamic` and `interiorCollisions` not built (none matters to the explosion's on the range); the layers not tried (every collider of the range taken as on them).

## Set off by another (`SubModule`)

A system names others as its sub-emitters, each with when (`type`: 0 a particle's birth, 1 its collision, 2 its death, among others), what it takes from the particle or the system (`properties`, as bits: 1 its colour, 2 its size, 4 its turn, 8 its lifetime, 16 the parent's duration) and a chance. A system so named never emits by itself (the engine marks it when the parent starts: `grenades-and-knife.md`, "The blast's effect"). Set off, it is moved to the parent's particle and emits its bursts' counts there, born on its own shape with its start values at the parent's share of its cycle where it takes the duration (at nought where not), each burst by its chance. `PROVISIONAL`: each burst's count at once, its rate and its own cycle not run for it; the kinds but the collision's not built (the explosion's one is of that kind).

## Its lights (`LightsModule`)

A system with lights lights a share of its particles (`ratio`) with copies of the light it names (an effect's own light, kept off: its colour, range and intensity), at most `maxLights` at once. Each copy stands at its particle; its colour the light's, times the particle's where `useParticleColor`; its range the light's times `rangeCurve` at the particle's share of life, times the particle's size where `sizeAffectsRange`; its intensity the light's times `intensityCurve`, times the particle's alpha where `alphaAffectsIntensity`. `PROVISIONAL`: with a ratio under one, every so-manyth particle in the order kept (`randomDistribution`, at random, not built); the explosion's two give their one particle each a light.

## Trails (`TrailModule`, its particles' mode)

Unity's documentation (the Trails module's page): each particle (of the `ratio` share) leaves a trail of the places it has been, a point dropped once it has moved `minVertexDistance` from the last, each kept for `lifetime` (a share of the particle's lifetime); drawn as a strip facing the camera, as wide as `widthOverTrail` along it (times the particle's size where `sizeAffectsWidth`), coloured `colorOverLifetime` at the particle's share of life times `colorOverTrail` along it (times the particle's colour where `inheritParticleColor`), its picture stretched over its length (`textureMode` 0), by the renderer's second material, dying with its particle where `dieWithParticles`. **Built** (0.87.0, for the RShG-2's fragments: `firing.md`, "A launcher's blasts"): the first point at the particle's birth; `PROVISIONAL`, the share along a trail by distance, its picture's 0 at the particle; the other modes (ribbons), `sizeAffectsLifetime`, a trail outliving its particle, `worldSpace` for a local system, not built.

## Drawn

A renderer that is off (`m_Enabled` 0 on the `ParticleSystemRenderer`) draws nothing, its system running all the same (the flashbang's shock wave: `grenades-and-knife.md`). A billboard is a square of the particle's size about its place, in the view's plane (alignment 0), turned in that plane by the particle's turn; its corners' picture coordinates run from 0 to 1 across and up (the sheet's cell where it has one); its colour the particle's. A stretched billboard (mode 1) lies along the particle's way (`grenades-and-knife.md`, "The blast's effect"). **A mesh** (mode 4) is the renderer's mesh (`m_Mesh`; with more, `m_Mesh1` to `m_Mesh3` by `m_MeshDistribution`) at the particle's place, scaled by its size, turned by its turn in the axes `m_RenderAlignment` gives (2, the system's own), each corner coloured the particle's colour; the explosion's shock wave is Unity's built-in sphere (`Library/unity default resources`, object 10207: 515 corners, a metre across, read from the install's own copy by a mesh's type tree borrowed from a bundle, the file having none). The material's shader draws it (each shader is the game's own, read and disassembled: the fume's in `firing.md`, the explosion's in `grenades-and-knife.md`). Within a system, the particles go by its sort mode: by distance far to near (1), the oldest in front (2), the youngest in front (3). With no sorting (mode 0) the particles are drawn in the order the engine keeps them, and the engine puts the last particle in a dead one's place (decompiled 2026-10-18, `ParticleSystem::KillParticles`: groups of four from the top, a group looked at again after any death); this work takes them away so too since 0.68.3 (`trp_sim::particles::kill`; until then it kept them as born, oldest first), and a renderer that sorts by age orders them by the life left (the engine's key). A system's renderer among the scene's other transparent things: by queue, then far to near (`grenades-and-knife.md`); `firing.md` says where the muzzle's are drawn.

## Built (2026-10-18, 0.66.0)

`trp_data::particles` (a system's figures, its renderer and its material read; `MinMaxCurve`, `MinMaxGradient`, `Gradient`), `trp_sim::particles` (a system run: emitted, stepped, what each particle draws as), and in the app the drawing of the fume's (`particles_view`). The modules read and run: the main module's start values and gravity, size, rotation and colour over life, the speed's limit and drag. A module that is on and not built is said so in the run's log when its system is read, and left out. The switch: `PARTICLES` among the options' features (restart required).

**Tested** (`trp_sim::particles`: `values_that_vary_and_gradients_go_by_their_mode`, `a_puff_slows_grows_fades_and_goes` (its speed above the limit the same however the time is cut), `no_more_than_the_most_are_born`; `the_muzzles_fume_system_is_read`, the install's). **Seen** (the muzzle's fumes: `firing.md`). **Not compared with the game.**

**Built** (2026-10-18, 0.68.0, for a grenade's explosion): a system played (start delay, bursts, rate, duration, looping), the shapes cone, sphere and half sphere, the force, the turn by speed and the direction of turning, the sheet's grid, a local system under an object placed and turned (`Placement`), stretched billboards and the sort modes (in the app's `explosion_view`); a whole effect read (a system, every one below it with its place, and its lights) and an effect of the effects' list by its name with its mediator's copies; each system's sub-emitters read (`SubModule`: which system, when, what it takes, its chance), and a system another of the effect's sets off left out of the effect's playing (it emits only when set off). **Tested** (`a_system_played_bursts_after_its_delay_on_its_shape`, `a_sphere_s_particles_go_out_from_its_middle`, `a_sheet_s_frame_is_its_cell`, `a_local_system_is_drawn_where_it_is`; `a_grenade_s_explosion_is_read`, the install's). **Seen** (the explosion: `grenades-and-knife.md`). **Not compared with the game.**

**Built** (2026-10-19 and 20, 0.85.0, for the explosion's second part): meeting the world on the game's physics engine (`trp_physics::World::sweep_sphere`, PhysX's single sweep; `trp_sim::particles::ParticleSystem::step_in` with a `World`), the sub-emitters of the collision kind (`emit_at`), the lights module (`lights`, drawn as Bevy's point lights), a mesh's particles (the renderer's mesh read, Unity's own from the install's `unity default resources`: `trp_data::unity::extract::mesh_of`, `trp_data::screen::build_object`). **Tested** (`a_particle_meets_the_ground`, `a_sub_emitter_is_born_where_it_is_set_off_and_particles_light`, `trp_physics`'s `a_swept_sphere_meets_the_ground_and_a_box`; the sphere read, `a_grenade_s_explosion_is_read`). **Seen** (the explosion: `grenades-and-knife.md`). **Not compared with the game.**

## Not built

The other shapes (a box, a circle, an edge, a mesh's, among others: a system of one is born at its middle, along its axis), velocity over life, inherited velocity, noise, triggers, the sub-emitters of the other kinds (birth, death), collision with planes and at medium and low quality, the sheet's sprites mode, a light given at random (`randomDistribution`), trails but each particle's own ("Trails", above), custom data, a mesh particle's turn of three axes and its other meshes (`m_Mesh1` to `m_Mesh3`), and the render modes horizontal and vertical (none draws nothing); a custom simulation space. A billboard's most size (`m_MaxParticleSize`) is kept by the heat haze's drawing alone. Each is in the documentation.
