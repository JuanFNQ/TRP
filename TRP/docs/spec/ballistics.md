# Ballistics

What a shot does after it leaves the weapon: where it starts and which way it goes, how it flies, what it meets, what each hit does to it, and what a hit shows and sounds. Read from the game's code on 2026-10-20 (`Player.FirearmController.Shot`, `AdjustShotVectors`, `InitiateShot`; `ProceduralWeaponAnimation.method_12`, `method_14`; `Weapon`'s `SpeedFactor`, `GetTotalCenterOfImpact`, `GetBarrelDeviation`, `CalculateShotDirectionForIron`; `AmmoTemplate.AmmoFactor`; `BallisticsCalculator`, `Shot`, `TrajectoryCalculator`, `TrajectoryCalculatorHistory`, `BallisticsCalculatorConstants`; `PhysicsExtensions.LinecastInBothSides` and its two casts; `BallisticCollider`, `BaseBallistic`, `BallisticPreset`, `TerrainBallistic.ApplyPresetValues`, `MaterialType`; `PrecalculatedRandoms` and its tables, `FastRndom`; `GameWorld.DoWorldTick`, `ShotDelegate`, `ClientGameWorld.ShotDelegate`; `EffectsCommutator.PlayHitEffect`; `Effects`, `Effects.Effect.Emit`, `PlaySound`; `DeferredDecalRenderer.DrawDecal`, `GetSingleDecal`), from the engine (Unity's own random numbers, read with Ghidra: below), from the install (the item database's ammunition and weapons, `globals.json`'s `Ballistic`, the hard settings' `ColliderPresets` and `DECAL_SHIFT`, the effects' bundle), and set beside recordings of 2026-10-18 where it says so (`local\traces\courses3`, recorder 0.11.0: `2-surfaces`, `2-far`, `2-through`, `2-other-weapons`; each shot, each bullet's place and speed every frame, each hit with the surface's figures and its seed). The ballistics, a dummy on the range and the impact effects.

## The random numbers

Every chance a bullet takes is decided by its **seed**, a whole number from 0 to 511 drawn when the shot is made (`Random.Range(0, 512)`, Unity's own generator, so not to be foretold), and three **tables of 512** the ballistics calculator fills once from the seed 0 (`PrecalculatedRandoms(512, 0)`; every calculator the game makes is given 0):

- **numbers between 0 and 1**: Unity's generator started at 0 (`Random.InitState(0)`), then 512 of `Random.value`;
- **directions**: started at 0 again, 512 of `Random.onUnitSphere`;
- **the "normal" table**: started at 0 again, 512 numbers each drawn (`Random.Range`) within a third of the span from −1 to 1, the thirds moving on as the place in the table passes 2.1%, 15.7%, 49.8%, 83.9% and 97.5% of it (so most lie near the middle); then, started at 0 a third time, shuffled from the end: for each place from the last down to the second, the number there swapped with the one at a place drawn from 0 up to it (`Random.Range(0, place + 1)`).

A draw is the table's entry at the seed modulo 512. A shot draws the same number for every chance it takes at one hit; what follows a hit is given a new seed, the old one shuffled by the game's own small xorshift (`FastRndom.RandomizeInt`: add one, xor with itself shifted left 7, then with itself shifted right 3, made positive).

**Unity's generator** (read from the engine, `UnityPlayer.dll` with Unity's public symbols: `Random_CUSTOM_InitState`, `Random_Get_Custom_PropValue`, `Random_CUSTOM_Range`, `RangedRandom`, `RandomUnitVector`, `Random_CUSTOM_get_insideUnitSphere_Injected`): four 32-bit words; the seed is the first and each next is the one before times 1812433253, plus one. A draw takes the first word xor itself shifted left 11, moves the words along by one, and makes the last from the old last `w` and that `t` as `w ^ (w >> 19) ^ t ^ (t >> 8)`. A number between 0 and 1 is the draw's low 23 bits times the 32-bit float with bits `0x34000001` (a hair over 1/8388608, so both ends can come). `Range(a, b)` for numbers is `(1 − t)·b + t·a`; for whole numbers `a` plus the draw modulo `b − a` (and the other way when `b < a`, `a` when equal). `onUnitSphere` draws twice: its height `1 − 2t₁`, its turn about the height `(1 − t₂)·2π` (the 32-bit float `0x40c90fdb`), its other two figures the turn's cosine and sine times `√(1 − height²)`. `insideUnitSphere` is such a direction times a third number to the power ⅓ (the float `0x3eaaaaab`).

**Compared** (`trp_sim::unity_random`, `the_ballistics_tables_give_the_recorded_penetration`): an M882 round (penetration 18, spread 0.8) fired with seed 15 left with a penetration of 17.785305 (`2-far`); the rebuilt normal table gives 17.78531. Its fragment's seed, 2322, is the shuffle of 15.

## A shot leaves the weapon

**Where from and which way** (`Shot`): from the weapon's **fireport** as it stands (the transform's own place, for a player drawn), along the fireport's own axes turned by the **local shot direction** (`LocalShotDirection`, the procedural animation's `ShotDirection`). That direction is the fireport's own **down** (`Vector3.down`, along the barrel) except while the sights are up on a sight that lines up (iron sights with `mod_align_rear` and `mod_align_front`, `AlignIfAvailable`): then, as the sights come up (`method_14`), it is worked out for the weapon's iron sight range (`IronSightRange`, at least 25 m for the point it aims at):

- in the weapon's own bone, the line through the two sights' bones gives a point that far along it from the front sight, less the fireport: `localPosition`;
- the weapon's default round (`DefAmmo`) is flown from nought along x at its initial speed times the weapon's speed factor (below), and the table of its flight searched for the step where its distance from the start, then its x, first passes the range; the height there is `drop`;
- the direction is `(0, −1, (localPosition.y − drop) / localPosition.z)` in the fireport's axes.

An optic does not change it: the optic's own picture is turned to the bullet's path at its zeroing distance instead (`OpticLookAt`; optics spec, "Zeroing").

**Moved back along it** (`AdjustShotVectors`): the start is taken back from the fireport by a fifth of the **weapon's length** (`WeaponLn`, the line the turn-away casts: weapon-handling spec, "Turn-away"). When the wide field of view's squeeze is on (`ShotNeedsFovAdjustments`, on but once a scope's optic has been turned to its zeroing point, until the sights next go down or the like: optics spec, "Zeroing"; and a ribcage scale below 1), the start and the direction are each put into the hands' root's own axes (`HandsHierarchy.Self`), their forward figure multiplied by the ribcage scale, and put back (the direction made a unit again).

**Spread** (no spread for a bot that shoots without it): the direction times 100 plus a point within a ball (Unity's `insideUnitSphere`, again its own generator) times the product of

- the weapon's **centre of impact** (`GetTotalCenterOfImpact` without the ammunition): its barrel's `CenterOfImpact` (the weapon's own if it has no barrel part), times one less the sum of its parts' `Accuracy` over 100;
- a revolver's **double-action penalty**, added to it where the hammer is down as the shot is made (`CylinderHammerClosed`, read at the fire mark): the weapon's `DoubleActionAccuracyPenalty`, times one less its class's skill's share (`DoubleActionRecoilReduce`: `WeaponDoubleActionRecoilReduceBuff`, 0.01 a level, half at 50, despite its name read by nothing else), times the first stock's `DoubleActionAccuracyPenaltyMult` (the first part of the class `Stock` in `Weapon.Mods`, the weapon's own slots before their parts' own; 1 without), multiplied in that order; nought with the hammer cocked. The hammer goes down at the shot's later mark (`AddAmmoInChamber`, double action only), so the first pull of double action from a cocked hammer takes none and each after it does; single action never does. In the install: the Rhino 50DS and 200DS 0.7, the RSh-12 1, the MTs-255-12 2.5 (its wooden stock 0.3 of it, the one part below 1), the M32A1 2.5 (single action only, so never); every other weapon's figure (1.5) is never read;
- the round's **ammunition factor** (`AmmoFactor`): `(100 + |ammoAccr|)/100` for an `ammoAccr` of nought or less, `100/(100 + ammoAccr)` above;
- the **overheat** multiplier: 1, or past `OverheatProblemsStart` a blend toward `MaxCOIIncreaseMult` by how far toward `MaxOverheat` the last shot's heat is;
- the **barrel's deviation** (`GetBarrelDeviation`): a curve of the weapon's durability through `DeviationCurve` and `DeviationMax` (its barrel part's, else its own; a maximum of nought is 100) that is 1 at full durability, times the repair buff's `WeaponSpread` (1 without one);
- the buff's `WeaponSpread` once more (1 where it is nought).

Made a unit, that is the shot's way. A double-barrelled weapon's second barrel fired with the first turns it further (`DupletAccuracyPenaltyX`, `Y`; not built).

**The bullet** (`BallisticsCalculator.CreateShot`, `Shot.Create`): a seed drawn (above), its **speed** the round's `InitialSpeed` times the weapon's **speed factor** (`SpeedFactor`: one plus the weapon's and its parts' `Velocity` over 100), its velocity that speed along the way; its damage, mass, diameter, ballistic coefficient, chances of penetrating (`PenetrationChanceObstacle`), ricocheting and fragmenting, fragment counts, armour damage share (`ArmorDamage` / 100) and life (`AmmoLifeTimeSec`) the round's; its **penetration** the round's `PenetrationPower` plus its `PenetrationPowerDiviation` times the normal table's entry at the seed; a chance of going on through (`DeviationChance`) of 1; the damage's degradation coefficient `globals.json`'s `Ballistic.GlobalDamageDegradationCoefficient` (0.5 in this install). A round of several projectiles (`ProjectileCount` above 1: buckshot) makes as many bullets (`CreateMultiProjectileShot`), the `i`th with the fragment number `2i`, each way the shot's times 100 plus the shotgun dispersion times the direction table's entry at a whole number drawn for it (`Random.Range(0, 512)`, Unity's generator), made a unit; then its own seed, drawn as any bullet's. Pellet by pellet: its entry's draw, then its seed's, with no draw between. The dispersion is the round's `buckshotDispersion`, which the shot sets just before to the weapon's `TotalShotgunDispersion`: its barrel's `ShotgunDispersion` (the weapon's own if it has no barrel part) times one less the sum of its parts' `Accuracy` over 100.

**Compared**: the recorded shots' start velocities are their speeds along their ways (AS VAL, MP-443, P226, MP-133); 385 m/s M882 from the MP-443 left at 378.301, a speed factor of 0.9826. **The pellets** (2026-11-07, `courses2`'s `5-pellets`, recorder 0.10.0: the MR-133, six shots of 7 mm buckshot, its shot's way recorded after the spread): each of the 48 ways is the shot's times 100 plus the MR-133's 1.55 times one entry of the direction table, within 4e-8 rad (any other entry at least 6e-5 off; the dispersion that fits each, 1.5498 to 1.5502). Each shot's sixteen draws, entry then seed for each pellet, are one run of Unity's generator: the low nine bits of each are sums of the generator's 128 bits (it is made of shifts and xors), so the 144 make equations, which agree on all six shots (rank 127: 17 to spare); taken each seed before its entry, none agree. Our shot (`Ballistics::shoot`), started from the state they give, makes every pellet's seed and fragment number the game's and its way within 7e-8 rad. Not compared: the shot's own spread (`insideUnitSphere`, the three draws before the first pellet's), whose way before it is not recorded.

## The flight

**The table of its flight** (`TrajectoryCalculator`, for a player's shot): steps of 0.01 s from the start, at most 1300 (13 s), made as they are first asked for. From a step's place `p` and velocity `v` (speed `s`), with the round's mass `m` (kg), diameter `d` (m) and ballistic coefficient `BC`:

- the **drag coefficient** of the **G1 standard projectile** at the speed's Mach number (speed over 343 m/s): the standard G1 drag table, as published, from Mach 0 to 5 (0.2629 at 0, 0.2558 at 0.05, … 0.4805 at 1.0, … 0.4988 at 5), found by the bracket `floor(s / 343 / 0.05)` of the list's places (nought where that is nought or less, the last entry past the end) and drawn straight between that place and the one before, by speed. The list's places are 0.05 Mach apart only up to Mach 0.6 (then 0.7, 0.725, …, and past 1.15 wider again), so above Mach 0.6 the place found is not the speed's and the line is drawn on past its pair: at Mach 1 the game takes 0.431 (its places at Mach 0.85 and 0.875, drawn on), where the table says 0.4805. That is the game's, and kept;
- the bullet's own factor `m × 0.0014223 / (d² × BC)`, and its frontal area `π d² / 4` times the air's density 1.2;
- the slowing `drag × factor × area × s² / (2m)` against the velocity, plus the gravity (`Physics.gravity`, −9.81 down);
- the next place `p + v × 0.01 + 0.00005 × a` and velocity `v + a × 0.01`.

A bot's shot (but a grenade launcher's) flies the same table without gravity.

**Where it is** (`PredictedTrajectoryCalculation`): at a time `t` since the shot, the place drawn straight between the two steps either side of it, and the velocity the earlier step's; past 13 s, or past the table, thrown on from the last step under gravity alone.

**Each frame** (`GameWorld.DoWorldTick`: the players, then the ballistics): every bullet in flight is moved on by the world's time step (`GameWorld.DeltaTime`: the time measured since the last frame, at most 1 s; not Unity's own frame time, so a recording's steps are read from its bullets' ages), its time since the shot grown by it; its old place and new are the frame's **segment**. **When a shot is made in the frame** decides whether it moves on in it: **automatic fire** (full or burst) is fired by the firing operation itself (`FireOnFullAutoOperation`: its start, then its events by the operation's own clock) in the player's update, before the ballistics, so its bullets move on in their own frame (recorded: every shot of the AS VAL's bursts in `2-far` is 0.008 s out on its own frame); **a single shot** is fired by the weapon animation's event (`OnFireEvent`), after the ballistics, so its bullets first move on the next frame (recorded: every single shot of `2-surfaces`, `2-through`, `2-other-weapons` and the MP-443's, at age 0 on their own frame). A bullet past its life (`AmmoLifeTimeSec`) is done without a hit. A bullet's hit makes the bullets that follow it; each is moved on in the same frame by what is left of the frame's step after the hit (`TimeLeftSinceFrameStart`).

**Compared** (2026-10-20, `cargo run -p trp-sim --bin bullets -- <trace>`): each recorded bullet made again with its recorded start, way, speed and seed and flown frame by frame, the world's step taken from the recorded bullets' ages: 278 bullets over 10,688 frames in flight (`2-surfaces`, `2-through`, `2-other-weapons`, both of `2-far`), its place within 0.05 mm of the game's on the short courses and within 1.4 mm on average (4.6 mm at most) over the long flights of `2-far` (up to 2 s). **The step the recording does not hold** (2026-11-07): where no bullet flew through a frame from the one before, the step is the player's (`clock_dt`, the movement's `LastDeltaTime`): the world gives the players its own clock (`SetDeltaTimeDelegate(() => DeltaTime)`) and steps them before the ballistics, and the two are the same on all 4,308 frames both give (`courses2` and `courses3`). `courses2`'s four recordings with shots (recorder 0.10.0, Factory): 326 bullets, within 0.01 mm but for two AKM rounds that flew their 5 s into the sky (4.9 mm at most). The made bullets' penetrations are the game's to 1e-3 (the normal table).

## What a segment meets

**Two casts** (`LinecastInBothSides`, on the bullets' layers: water, terrain, the high-poly and transparent colliders, dead bodies and the body parts' hit colliders): forward from the segment's start to its end, the nearest hit not ignored; and backward from its end to its start, the hit **nearest the start** not ignored, for a mesh collider cast again from just past it (1 mm on, toward the start) up to eight times, so the last face of a mesh is found. Unity's casts meet only the faces turned toward them, and nothing they start inside, so the backward cast finds where the bullet **leaves** a thing. Of the two, the forward hit is taken when its distance from the start is less than the backward hit's; that one is a **forward hit**, the other a **backward hit**.

**Ignored** (`IsHitIgnored`): for a bullet that is not a fragment, its shooter's own body parts (and a bot's own capsule); and for any bullet, a collider that belongs to the same ballistic thing as one this bullet or any before it hit, unless it is that very collider.

**What it hit** (`TryGetBallisticCollider`): the ballistic component on the collider's object or its parent; a collider without one counts as the calculator's **default hit body** (the prefab `defaultballisticcollider.bundle`).

## A hit

On a hit (`HandleCollision`):

- the share of the segment past the hit, `k`, is the frame time left; the time since the shot goes back by it; the speed is drawn back toward the segment's start speed by `k` of the difference between the new and the old;
- the hit's thing is the ballistic collider's (a body part's for a body), the hit **cosine** the dot product of the face's normal and the segment's way;
- the bullet's last place is the hit point;
- on a **forward hit** its damage and penetration are each multiplied by `r + (1 − r) × clamp01(degradation)`, `r` the speed over the initial speed (held within 0 and 1).

**Then** (`CreateFragments`), the first that holds:

1. **Deflected by heavy armour**: a body part's or plate's collider of a character, met at an angle between 42.5° and 80° from the face (the angle between the way back and the normal) with fewer than two ricochets before, which the character's armour deflects (body hits spec, built 0.93.0): a ricochet straight off the face.
2. **A ricochet** (`IsRicocheted`): a forward hit, the thing's ricochet chance at least the smallest float, the angle between 42.5° and 80°, the bullet's own chance above nought, and `(thing's chance + bullet's) × (1 − |cosine|)` above the number at the seed; and fewer than two ricochets before. The bullet that follows starts at the hit point along the velocity mirrored in the face, plus the thing's deviation times the direction table's entry at the seed (but for heavy armour), made a unit; with the shuffled seed, the fragment number one up, the speed 0.7 of the hit speed, the damage and penetration `min(1, chances summed) × 0.5` of the hit's, its chances of penetrating, ricocheting and fragmenting 0.2 of the bullet's, the fragment counts the bullet's.
3. **Through** (`IsTargetPenetrated`): a backward hit on a body part always; otherwise the thing's penetration chance at least the smallest float, the bullet's penetration above the thing's penetration level, and the chances summed above the number at the seed. Then:
   - **in pieces** (`IsBulletFragmented`): a forward hit, the chances of fragmenting summed above the number at the seed, the thing's and the bullet's each at least the smallest float: as many fragments as `FastRndom.Int(seed, MinFragmentsCount, MaxFragmentsCount − MinFragmentsCount)` (none, and nothing follows), each from 2 mm past the hit point along the bullet's first way, along its velocity plus the thing's deviation times the direction table's entry at a seed shuffled on for each; each at 0.7 of the hit speed over the most fragments (at most the hit speed), with damage and penetration `share × 0.5 / MaxFragmentsCount` of the hit's (`share` 1, or for a body part the penetration over its level over 100 plus the part's and the round's `PenetrationDamageMod`, held within 0 and 1), chances of penetrating and ricocheting 0.2 of the bullet's, none of fragmenting, no fragments of its own; through a body part's armour each also by the armour's factor (body hits spec);
   - **on, turned** (`IsBulletDeviated`, its chance at least the smallest float; a chain fence does not turn an explosive round): from 2 mm past the hit along its new way: the velocity's way plus, on a forward hit whose thing's deviation chance is above the number at the seed, the thing's deviation times the direction table's entry at the seed (not made a unit); with the shuffled seed, the same fragment number, the speed the hit speed (for a forward hit on a body part 0.8 to 1 of it by the part's penetration chance), the damage and penetration the hit's (on a forward body-part hit by the share above), the chances the bullet's (on such a hit 0.2 to 1 of them by the part's penetration chance), a chance of going on 0.08 less;
4. **Stopped** (`StopHit`) otherwise.

A bullet that hit is done; its state is the outcome's (`DeviationHit`, `RicochetHit`, `FragmentationHit`, `StopHit`).

**After the hit** (`ShotDelegate`, unless the bullet ran out of life or, for a body, its parts were met already through the same armour piece): the thing is told (`ApplyHit`: a body part's damage, body hits spec), an explosive round past its arming time explodes (not built), and the hit's effect is played (below).

## What a hit shows and sounds

(`EffectsCommutator.PlayHitEffect`, then `Effects.Emit`; read 2026-10-20 with the install's entries, decals and banks: `the_impacts_are_read`.)

- A hit within 2.5 cm on every axis of one already played this frame plays nothing (the list is emptied each late update).
- The place is the hit point moved along the normal by the hard settings' `DECAL_SHIFT`; the **volume** for a forward hit is `InverseLerp(64, 256, speed²)` (1 at 16 m/s and over), halved for each fragment number (`0.5 / n` past the first), and nought for a backward hit; a body's hit gives its own material and point of view, and is silent if the body says so.
- **A ricochet** off anything but a body plays the effects' first additional sound (`AdditionalSoundEffects[0]`) at the place, outdoors.
- **Seen** when the bullet is the player's own and not a fragment, else when the hit point is within the camera's picture (a hundredth past each edge).
- **The effect** is the effects' list's first entry naming the material (`Effects.EffectsArray`, `MaterialTypes`; the install's: generic soft for rubber, generic soft, fabric, cardboard and snow; concrete for asphalt, concrete and stone; wood for cardboard (taken already), thin and thick wood; metal for thin and thick metal and grates; plastic, glass, body, body armour, helmets, the visor, soil and forest soil, water and puddles, swamp, and none (`DefaultNoDecal`) their own; **gravel, tile, the grasses, mud, pebbles, chain fence, garbage, glass shattered, tyre and generic hard none**, so a hit there plays nothing: recorded, gravel and tall grass hits were silent where concrete, wood, metal and soil played theirs).
