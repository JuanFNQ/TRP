# Durability, malfunctions and overheating

## Durability

A weapon item keeps its durability and its greatest durability (`Repairable`); a weapon's template durability is always 100, whatever its record says, so "per cent" and "points" are the same for a weapon. **Each shot that fires** (a misfire does not) takes, after the shot's heat is added: 100 over the weapon's `OperatingResource`, times its `DurabilityBurnRatio`, times every part's `DurabilityBurnModificator` (every part in its slots, at any depth, the magazine with them; rounds are not parts) and the round's, times the heat's factor (from `DurReduceMinMult` 1 at `OverheatProblemsStart` 100 or below to `DurReduceMaxMult` 3 at `MaxOverheat` 200, by the last shot's heat), times one less Weapon Maintenance's reduction (0.005 a level). It stops at nought; a weapon at nought still fires. The AKM with its stock parts and PS: 0.0223 a shot when cool. **Hot shots wear the greatest durability too**: with the last shot's heat at 100 or more and the greatest durability at 55 or more, a wear of 0.1 to 0.2 by the heat (0.2 to 0.7 drawn at the top heat) comes off it, but only while the durability is below the greatest by more than that wear, and never below 55.

## The chance of a malfunction

Worked out at each shot, before it, from the round to be fired (the chambered one; the magazine's top for a weapon with no chamber), the magazine fitted and the weapon's heat now. Nought where the weapon allows none of the five kinds. Otherwise four parts: the **magazine's** (its `MalfunctionChance` times `MagazineMalfChanceMult`, nought with none fitted), the **round's** (its misfire and feed chances summed, times `AmmoMalfChanceMult`), the **heat's** (from `MinMalfChance` 0.5 at 100 to `MaxMalfChance` 9 at 200, nought below 100), each per mille, and the **durability's**, a probability, by the weapon's `BaseMalfunctionChance` B, its rate of fire FR (the template's) and W, the durability's whole part: above 59, `((B + 1)^(3 + (100 − W) / (20 − 10 / (FR/10)^0.322)) − 1) / 1000`; at 59 or below, `((B + 1)^(log10((101 − W)^((50 − W^1.286/4.8) / (FR^0.17/2.9815 + 2.1)))) − 1) / 1000`. A repair buff's `MalfunctionProtections` scales the durability's and the heat's (below, "Repair buffs"). Elite at Troubleshooting, a source the shooter has had a malfunction from with that weapon cuts its part to 0.3 (and, the game's flag for the durability being nought, any such malfunction cuts the durability's too). Between `DurRangeToIgnoreMalfs` 93 and 100 the durability's, the round's and the magazine's are nought (the heat's stays). The total is the durability's (to one at most) plus the other three over a thousand, rounded to five places. The AKM at 35 with FMJ: 0.016473 by the durability, 0.01674 in all.

## Which malfunction

**The heat's slide first**: a shot whose heat reached `StartSlideOverheat` (200) holds the slide until a shot leaves the heat at `FixSlideOverheat` (170) or below; while held, a shot with the heat above 170 and rounds in the magazine is a soft slide, with no draw spent. Otherwise one draw u from the malfunctions' own generator (each draw sets the engine's generator's seed to the generator's seed plus its count): above the total, none. **Its source** by the same u, along the parts in the order durability, round, magazine (only with rounds in it and fitted), heat. **Its kind**, by a second draw over the source's weights (the engine's generator just seeded): the durability's feed 0.1 (rounds in the magazine, a magazine fitted), misfire 0.1, jam 0.6, soft slide 0.2 (rounds in the magazine), and a hard slide only at W 5 or below (0.3 to 0.8 as W falls to nought); the round's misfire `AmmoMisfireWt` 0.7 over its two chances summed (PS: 4.02), feed 0.2, jam 0.1; the magazine's a feed; the heat's feed 0.35, jam 0.25, soft slide 0.7, hard slide as the durability's at W 5 or below, and no misfire. Each kind only where the weapon allows it; none left, no malfunction. The kinds and their numbers, which the animator takes: misfire 1, failure to eject (jam) 2, hard slide 3, soft slide 4, failure to feed 5.

**The rounds**: every shot attempt takes the chambered round out. With no malfunction the magazine's top goes into the chamber; with a failure to feed it is taken out of the magazine and out of the weapon (the shot fires; the round is kept with the weapon, and its repair throws it onto the ground as a thing); a misfire makes no shot, the chamber shows empty and that round is lost (its repair throws it out to be seen only); a jam or a slide fires and leaves the magazine as it was and the chamber empty until the repair.

## In the hands

**The shot.** A single shot rolls as its operation begins: a misfire makes no shot, the trigger and the animator's malfunction number take the hands to the misfire's state by its own transition; any other malfunction lets the fire state come by the animator's own way in (the game plays it only with none) and the shot is made at its `FiringBullet` mark, then the malfunction begins. In automatic fire each shot rolls: a misfire stops the run with no shot; any other's shot is made with its sound's tail at once (as a trigger let go) and the run stops. Either way the trigger is let go, blind fire ends, the animator is told the kind (`Malfunction` and `MalfType`), an automatic weapon's state is played outright (`MISFIRE`, `JAM`, `HARD_SLIDE`, `SOFT_SLIDE`, `FEED`), and the malfunction's operation runs: a failure to feed's case out at its `ShellEject` mark, the chamber shown spent at `DelAmmoChamber`, and at `MalfunctionOff` (0.03 s into the AKM's states) the `Malfunction` layer is on, the number −1, the kind held, the weapon at rest with it. The shooter knows of it at once (with elite Troubleshooting, its kind too).

**Which weapons can have which** (each template's `AllowMisfire`, `AllowJam`, `AllowFeed`, and `AllowSlide` for either slide; the roll offers no other kind, and a weapon's animator has no state for a kind it does not allow): of the install's 164 weapons, 24 allow none at all (among them the UZI, both AA-12s, the Rhinos, the launchers and the flare guns); the PPSh and the three M60s only overheat; the Mosins, the T-5000 and the TOZ-106 have no jam; the MP-18 and the MP-43s only a misfire (`local\malf_allow_s148.txt`, read 2026-10-30). **But only a shot that rolls can malfunction**: a weapon loaded by its barrels or its cylinder takes its rounds for the shot without the roll, so the MP-18's and the MP-43s' misfire never comes; the rocket, flare and one-shot weapons allow none anyway.

**Put away during a malfunction's operation** (its state, the look, the repair: each the game's `MalfunctionOperation`): the trigger is let go and the sights go down at once, and the put-away is made when the operation ends, in place of the rest. One asked for in the shot, before its mark hands over to the malfunction, is lost (the shot's switch into the malfunction does not carry it).

**While it lasts**: the trigger only clicks (`DRY FIRE DISARMED`) and reminds; **the reload key**, by how the weapon is loaded (read 2026-10-30): one that takes a magazine (127 weapons) is refused with a failure to feed and a magazine in, and reminds, before any magazine is looked for, and reloads with any other kind (a misfire put right by the reload's chambering); one filled a round at a time (22: the Mosins, the M700, the AXMC, the TRG, the SVT, the AVT, the SKSs, the tube shotguns) is refused with any malfunction and reminds (the misfire-or-slide look off first), before the rounds are looked for, but for a misfire on a weapon whose bolt need not be opened to load (the tube shotguns), whose filling gives the empty chamber its round at once, keeps the malfunction's layer off while it plays and puts the misfire right at the chamber's mark (the layer back if it is still there at the end); barrels and cylinders meet no malfunction; a weapon that takes a magazine and can also be filled is refused a magazine with any malfunction (none in the install); the look at the magazine is refused with a failure to feed and reminds; the inventory refuses a magazine in or out with a failure to feed (or any malfunction on such a weapon) and any round into or out of a malfunctioned weapon or its fitted magazine ("You must fix malfunctions first", `IncompatibleByMalfunction`; the menu's Reload, Load and Unload refuse so too, by a malfunction the shooter knows of). **The look** (`ExamineWeapon`, L) with a malfunction looks at it (`ExamineMalfunctionTypeOperation`: the sights down, the trigger off, blind fire ended; the AKM's `JAM_LOOK`, 0.825 s to its `MalfunctionOff`), after which its kind is known; one look does. **The check of the chamber** (`CheckChamber`, Shift+T) repairs a malfunction whose kind is known, else reminds; refused with the trigger held or in blind fire. **The repair** (`RepairMalfunction`): the malfunction is cleared at once; the animator's `MalfunctionRepair` on, the layer off, the number and the kind the malfunction's, `SpeedFix` the repair's speed (one plus Troubleshooting's 0.005 a level, times 0.9 with one arm damaged and 0.8 with both, times `MalfRepairHardSlideMult` for a hard slide); at its marks the chamber shown spent, the case or the fed round (onto the ground) or the misfired round (seen only) thrown out once, a round chambered from the magazine, and at `MalfunctionOff` the weapon at rest. **A misfire is put right without its repair** by a reload's chambering, a magazine put in, a draw, the chamber's round taken out.

**The heat's stray shot**: the first shot whose heat reaches `AutoshotMinOverheat` (170) draws once a delay of 0.05 to 3 s and a chance of 0.2; drawn, at rest with a round in, the weapon fires one single shot by itself that long after the shot, unless it malfunctions meanwhile (drawn again only after a shot below 170). **The rate of fire**: a shot's heat above `FirerateOverheatBorder` (140) draws a factor of 0.85 to 1.15, kept until a shot's heat is 140 or below; automatic fire's interval becomes 60 over the rate times it, its phase kept, and (the game's cached operation) stays so after the factor is gone until a new one is drawn or the weapon is drawn again.

## The heat

Each shot that fires adds `HeatFactorByShot` times the round's `HeatFactor` times every part's `HeatFactor` times `HeatFactorGun` (times `ModHeatFactor` 1), to at most 200; the heat then falls in a straight line from the last shot, at a rate the last shot's heat sets (`L − dt · L · CoolFactorGun · k / (L − (L/200 + 0.0338 C + 0.2925)^(9.667 + 0.0668 C))`, k `CoolFactorGunMods` times every part's `CoolFactor` times `ModCoolFactor`, C `MaxOverheatCoolCoef` 6): the AKM from 200 about 4.2 a second. Its time is the raid's clock. What it does: the wear above, the chance above, the slide, the stray shot and the rate above, and **the spread**: from 100 the shot's spread grows toward `MaxCOIIncreaseMult` 1.5 at 200, by the last shot's heat (the spread spec: `ballistics.md`, "Spread"; with it the barrel's deviation by the durability, `DeviationCurve` and `DeviationMax`).

**What is kept with the weapon**: the malfunction, its source, that it and its kind are known, the last shot's heat and its time, the slide held, the sources elite cut; not kept (drawn again): the rate's factor and the stray shot.

## The barrel's glow

Each part with a `HotObject` (`probe hot <weapon>`: the AKM's receiver, its integral barrel's box at (0, −0.88, 0) of 0.015 by 0.4 by 0.015; its muzzle brake; its gas block and handguard, whose `VisibleHeatAlpha` 0 makes them warm only in thermal vision) is given the weapon's heat each frame as a temperature `T = 10 · heat/220 + 30`. Where its material has the `USEHEAT` keyword, the surface shader adds a glow to what it emits: within an ellipsoid about the box's centre whose half-axes are the box's whole size, a strength `f = max(0, 1 − d) · ((T − 30) · 0.1)²` (so by `(heat/220)²`), its colour from `_HeatColor1` (red) toward `_HeatColor2` (orange) by f, times f, `HeatVisibleFactor` (1), `VisibleHeatAlpha`, and the reflection's weight `0.5 · (x + y · 0.5 · (1 − n·v)²)` of its `_SpecVals`. So it fades with the square of the heat as the heat falls in its straight line. The parts are given the heat only once it has moved by a hundredth from the last given (`WeaponPrefab.ManualUpdate`; the first frame always). The glow goes into what the surface emits, in the game's gamma-space numbers, so it is added to the surface's light as those numbers. **Which parts glow** (the install, `probe hot`): barrels, integral barrels, muzzle devices and silencers (`VisibleHeatAlpha` 1: the AKM's receiver and brake, the M4A1's 370 mm barrel and A2 flash hider, the AK-103's receiver and brake); handguards and gas blocks only in thermal vision (0). A part's glow fills an ellipsoid whose half-axes are its box's whole size: the AKM's brake, 0.011 across, hardly glows on its outside. **A weapon not in hand**: every weapon object switched on joins the overheat system (`WeaponPrefab.OnEnable`, `WeaponOverHeatSystem`), which gives each its weapon's heat every frame by the same rule (`CurrentOverheat`: the item's last shot's heat and time, the raid's clock, its parts' cooling as fitted), once it has moved a hundredth from that object's own last; and the factory hands its weapon to every weapon object it makes (`ObjectsFactory`: `InitMalfunctionState` or `RevertMalfunctionState`, each `InitHotObjects` first). So a weapon on the ground glows as it cools, and so does one slung on a body, seen by others. One's own character's slung weapons have their renderers off (`PlayerBody.SlotView`, `_isYourPlayer`; the model still on): no glow is seen of them in first person.

## The shimmer over a hot barrel

Each hot part whose `HotObject` has `UseHeatHaze` (the AKM's receiver, the AR-15's barrels) emits particles into one shared system (`Effects.MuzzleEffect.Heat`, the effects bundle's `HeatEffect`: at most 200, white and unturned at birth, drawn by `Particles/DistortAndBlur` with `_Distort` 0.744, `_Blur` 0.834, `_InvFade` 88 and `_TextureMovement` (0.4, 0.4, −0.4, −0.4) into the camera's distortion, as the muzzle's haze is: firing spec, "The muzzle's heat haze"), while the hard settings' `HEAT_EMITTER_ENABLED` is on and its weapon lies within 5 m of the camera (`WeaponOverHeatSystem.LateUpdate`, `HotObject.NeedProcessEffects`). **Given a heat** (with the glow, above), its share is the part's temperature less 30 over 5.6, so the heat over 123.2, and three curves are read there (`probe hot`; the AKM's flat at their keys): the count (0 to 20, so 20·(3s² − 2s³)), the life (0.15 to 0.3) and the size (0.01 to 0.14). **Each frame**, with the count c at least 4 (the AKM's from a heat of about 35.4): the whole steps of 1/c since it last emitted, n, and the count's whole number times n particles, each at an even draw in its box (`HeatHazeBounds`; the AKM's about (0, −0.88, 0), extent (0.009, 0.04, 0.009), in the receiver's axes), with half the part's own velocity over the frame, the size, and a life of the curve's plus a draw from 0.3 to 0.5; its last emission then moves on by the n steps, and its place is kept for the next frame's velocity (also when a heat is given and it was not kept for 30 frames: `PrepareForEffects`). Its last emission is set when it wakes and moves only while it emits, so the first frame past 4 makes up all the time below at once (the system's 200 hold it). At heat 123.2 and above: 20 every twentieth of a second, the system holding 200, 0.14 m large, living 0.6 to 0.8 s. The 5 m are measured from the weapon's object (the `WeaponPrefab`'s place), not the part's. A weapon on the ground shimmers as it glows; one's own slung weapons would still emit within 5 m in first person, the particles not being among the renderers turned off (read from code, not seen in the game).

## Repair buffs

Read 2026-11-09 from SPT's server in the install (`RepairController.RepairWithKit`, `RepairService.AddBuffToItem`, `ShouldBuffItem`, `GetItemSkillType`, `GetDurabilityMultiplier`, `AddBuff`; `WeightedRandomHelper.GetWeightedValue`, `RandomUtil.GetDouble`, `GetInt`, `GetPercentOfValue`), its `configs/repair.json`, and the game's code (`BuffComponent`, `RepairBuffAttribute`, `Weapon.GetBarrelDeviation`, `Weapon.OnShot`, `FirearmController.GetTotalMalfunctionChance`, `Shot`, `ArmorComponent.ApplyDamage`, `ApplyDurabilityDamage`, `ArmorRepairStrategy.RepairItem`, `CompactCharacteristicPanel.Show`, `GridItemView.UpdateInfo`, `ChangeRepairBuffStatus`, `TryGetBuff`, `RepairerParametersPanel.UpdateBuffState`, `GetBuffState`); the research brief `local\malf_rest_brief_s240.md`, section E.

**Drawn by the server after a kit's repair**, once a request (a weapon's one; an armour's one for each part the amount reaches, its plates and inserts and then itself). None while the character's level is under `MinimumLevelToApplyBuff` (3); none for a part the server does not buff: it buffs a weapon (by Weapon Maintenance) and a thing of the armour's, vest's, headwear's or plate's classes whose `ArmorType` is Light or Heavy (by Light or Heavy Vests; a built-in insert is of none of those classes); none while that skill's progress is under 1,000 (level 10). Otherwise its chance is `CommonBuffMinChanceValue + CommonBuffChanceLevelBonus × L × clamp((P / M) / max(0.01, ReceivedDurabilityMaxPercent), 0.01, 1)`: L the progress over 100 cut to a whole, P the request's first kit's points, M the part's record's `MaxDurability`. With the globals' 0.074, 0.0019 and 30 the share term runs from 0.01 to 0.033, so the chance is about 7.4 % a repair: Weapon Maintenance 10 paying 20 points (the AKM from 60) 0.07419; 51 paying 100, 0.07723. (The repair window's preview of it multiplies the whole sum by the share, which is why it says "very low".) One draw decides (`NextDouble() <= chance`). Won, a buff from its kind's table (`repairKit.weapon` for a weapon; `repairKit.armor` for armour, vests, headwear and plates alike): its rarity by the weights Common 5 to Rare 1; its kind by the kinds' weights (a weapon's WeaponSpread 1 and MalfunctionProtections 1, which, all being 1, are a uniform pick; an armour's DamageReduction alone, with no draw; the table's Rare WeaponDamage range has no weight and never comes); its value uniform in its range (Common: spread 0.90 to 0.99, protection 0.94 to 0.96, reduction 0.90 to 0.98; Rare: 0.80 to 0.90, 0.75 to 0.90, 0.80 to 0.90); a share uniform in 75 to 90 %, and its **threshold** that share of the part's durability after the repair, rounded to a whole with halves to the even. It replaces any buff the part had; a lost draw leaves the old one.

**Kept with the thing** (`BuffComponent`), and in force while its kind and rarity are set and its value is not 1. **What it does**: a MalfunctionProtections the heat's chance times its value right after the heat's lerp, and the durability's before elite Troubleshooting's cut (above); a WeaponSpread the weapon's barrel deviation times its value, and the shot's spread times it once more (ballistics spec, "A shot leaves the weapon"), so the cone shrinks by its square (the shot whose wear ends it is spread without it: `Shot` wears the weapon before it spreads the shot; TRP takes the item's buff as it takes the durability for the deviation), while the inspect window's ACCURACY (its MOA and its bar's change) takes it once; a DamageReduction the damage an armour piece leaves of a hit, blocked or gone through, times its value (body hits spec, "The damage"). **Its end**: the wear of a shot (asked only of a weapon that had durability to wear), or of a hit or a blast on the armour piece, that takes the durability under the threshold, or under 0.5 whatever the threshold, ends it for good (`TryDisableComponent`): the AKM worn 0.0186 a shot, repaired to 98 with a threshold of about 81, keeps it some 900 shots.

**Shown**: in the inspect window its own row, the last of the thing's compact rows (`MALFUNCTION PROTECTIONS COMMON BUFF` and its kin, in the install's words "REDUCES MALFUNCTION CHANCE (COMMON)", "REDUCES WEAPON SPREAD (RARE)", "REDUCES INCOMING DAMAGE (COMMON)"), its icon its attribute's, its value one less the value times 100 cut to a whole and " %"; its value drawn in the enhancement's blue (84, 193, 255), and under the mouse on it the tooltip "Enhanced by a repair kit (Durability left: n)" in `#54c1ff`, n the durability less the threshold to two places. A spread buff marks the ACCURACY row the same way. A thing not examined shows neither. **A thing's view** (in a grid or a slot), known, with a buff on it or on anything in it (a rare one first): its ground 209, 153, 45 at 32 of 255 where neither a malfunction nor a round its chamber refuses colours it, and in its bottom right column, over its value with the column's one unit between, the buff's attribute icon at its own size, white at 180 of 255. **The repair window**: "Enhanced" on its own teal panel, or "Rare enhancement" on the orange one, for the thing's own buff (an armour's own, not its plates'), after the window's other warnings.

**In TRP**: REPAIR draws it after the repair (`trp_sim::repair::buffs`; `repair_view`) by the app's generator (`PROVISIONAL`, as the repair's own draws: SPT's are `System.Random` and `RandomUtil`'s); the character's level is the options' SKILLS page's LEVEL, the skills' progress the page's (nought with `SKILLS` off). The order a fixed draw maps to is the weights' keys' sorted (reading the file loses its order; the odds are the weights' whatever the order). The item keeps it; the weapon in hand's logic is given it and gives it back (`wear`), the character's armour under a blast the same way (`blast_view`); each shot's spread takes the item's (`bullets`). **Switches**: the draw is the bench's repair's (`BENCHES`); the protection acts only on a roll (`MALFUNCTIONS`); the spread only on a bullet (`BALLISTICS`; the inspect window's MOA shows it all the same); the reduction only on a hit through armour (the dummies' armour, the only armour shot, is new and never has one; the character's own meets only blasts, which may end it); the end only by wear (`DURABILITY`). For a check, `--buffed spread|protection|reduction[:rare]` (`manual.md`).

## In the app

The weapon in hand's logic is given its item's durability and malfunction state as it is taken in hand (`Firearm::set_wear`; `wear`), and is told each frame the round it would fire next, its magazine's chance, the skills (Troubleshooting, Weapon Maintenance, the options' levels), the arms damaged (health's conditions) and the raid's clock (the simulation's own seconds since the run began); its wear and state are kept back on the item, so another weapon or the same one drawn again has its own. Its seed is the app's (one a weapon taken in hand); the game seeds each character's at random. The switches `DURABILITY`, `MALFUNCTIONS` and `OVERHEATING` (the options' SIMPLE features, restart required): off, the weapon does not wear, never malfunctions, stays cold (and no barrel glows). After a death and Enter, the run begins again with each item as it was, and the weapon in hand's logic is given its item's wear afresh (until 0.147.3 it kept the old run's malfunction, durability and heat). For a check, `--malfunction misfire|jam|feed|soft|hard` gives the first weapon in hand that malfunction at its next shot (a kind the weapon does not allow is refused, with a warning; so is the F1 panel's, whose buttons show only the kinds the weapon in hand allows) and `--heat <n>` takes it in hand that hot (`manual.md`). The heat's spread and the durability's deviation go into each bullet (`bullets`). The barrel's glow is a twin of each glowing part's mesh drawn over it with the part's own material and light, the glow added to that light as the game's numbers, by the bumped normal (`heat_view`, `heat_glow.wgsl`): exact under the game's shading (`TARKOV SHADING` with `CUSTOMS LIGHT`), which sums its light so; under Bevy's light the frame's numbers are taken by the same 2.2 power, near the sRGB curve the frame is shown by (`PROVISIONAL`). Until 0.204.0 it was added in linear light by the mesh's own normal, about half as red over a lit barrel. A part drawn on bones gets none (none of the install's weapons sampled has one). **Not in hand**: each weapon's model has the owner of its heat (`heat_view::HeatOwner`, on a rig's `Weapon_root` or on a model's root, its first object): the rig in hand its logic's heat, any other rig none, a weapon on the ground (`loose`) or slung on the body (`slung_view`) its item's (`heat_view::item_heat`: `current_heat` from the item's last shot's heat and time by the raid's clock and the item's own figures, none with `OVERHEATING` off), each given anew a hundredth on by its own last; its glowing parts read on the model's thread by the rig's reader (`trp_data::heat::read`), their twins on the world's layer. A slung model is drawn only from outside, and so is its glow; its shimmer is emitted only while it is shown, the game's first-person emission not built (read from code, not seen; the main session's call, 2026-11-08). The shimmer (`particles_view`, `trp_sim::muzzle::BarrelHaze`, on each shimmering part's entity): each part given its model's heat (above); the 5 m reach measured from the model's own object (a weapon's on the ground or slung: its first object, as the game's; the rig in hand's: its `Weapon_root`, not the hands' prefab's object), and the two systems drawn into the distortion's values in turn, the muzzles' first (`PROVISIONAL` both); the particles' draws by the app's generator. With `PARTICLES` and `OVERHEATING` (off, the weapon stays cold). **What the shooter is told beyond the notices**: the quick prompt's situation is pushed by the hands' events after the step that said them, by the weapon's state then (`gestures::phrase::of_hands_event`: the shot's malfunction, every reminder, a weapon drawn with one; `controller`), and again with the glow 150 ms on (`wear`, which also tells the battle screen, `MalfunctionShown`); the quick bar's raise and its pistols' glow are `battle_view` (`follow_stance`, `glow_quick`: the glow stepped by the frame's time whether the screen is up or not, ended by `HandsHeld::quickdraws`, a quick draw of the holster's weapon begun; `PROVISIONAL`: a malfunction made known while a glow runs begins it afresh), the red view `health_view::SlotMark` in both bars, the tooltip `Carried::weapon_warning`. No switch of their own: the bar and its glow are drawn only with `TARKOV UI`; with `MALFUNCTIONS` off no new malfunction comes, so nothing is raised, though a weapon that already has one is still drawn red, says its red tooltip and raises the prompt when drawn.
