## Durability

A weapon item keeps its durability and its greatest durability (`Repairable`); a weapon's template durability is always 100, whatever its record says, so "per cent" and "points" are the same for a weapon. **Each shot that fires** (a misfire does not) takes, after the shot's heat is added: 100 over the weapon's `OperatingResource`, times its `DurabilityBurnRatio`, times every part's `DurabilityBurnModificator` (every part in its slots, at any depth, the magazine with them; rounds are not parts) and the round's, times the heat's factor (from `DurReduceMinMult` 1 at `OverheatProblemsStart` 100 or below to `DurReduceMaxMult` 3 at `MaxOverheat` 200, by the last shot's heat), times one less Weapon Maintenance's reduction (0.005 a level). It stops at nought; a weapon at nought still fires. The AKM with its stock parts and PS: 0.0223 a shot when cool. **Hot shots wear the greatest durability too**: with the last shot's heat at 100 or more and the greatest durability at 55 or more, a wear of 0.1 to 0.2 by the heat (0.2 to 0.7 drawn at the top heat) comes off it, but only while the durability is below the greatest by more than that wear, and never below 55.

## The chance of a malfunction

Worked out at each shot, before it, from the round to be fired (the chambered one; the magazine's top for a weapon with no chamber), the magazine fitted and the weapon's heat now. Nought where the weapon allows none of the five kinds. Otherwise four parts: the **magazine's** (its `MalfunctionChance` times `MagazineMalfChanceMult`, nought with none fitted), the **round's** (its misfire and feed chances summed, times `AmmoMalfChanceMult`), the **heat's** (from `MinMalfChance` 0.5 at 100 to `MaxMalfChance` 9 at 200, nought below 100), each per mille, and the **durability's**, a probability, by the weapon's `BaseMalfunctionChance` B, its rate of fire FR (the template's) and W, the durability's whole part: above 59, `((B + 1)^(3 + (100 − W) / (20 − 10 / (FR/10)^0.322)) − 1) / 1000`; at 59 or below, `((B + 1)^(log10((101 − W)^((50 − W^1.286/4.8) / (FR^0.17/2.9815 + 2.1)))) − 1) / 1000`. A repair buff's `MalfunctionProtections` scales the durability's and the heat's (not built: no buffs). Elite at Troubleshooting, a source the shooter has had a malfunction from with that weapon cuts its part to 0.3 (and, the game's flag for the durability being nought, any such malfunction cuts the durability's too). Between `DurRangeToIgnoreMalfs` 93 and 100 the durability's, the round's and the magazine's are nought (the heat's stays). The total is the durability's (to one at most) plus the other three over a thousand, rounded to five places. The AKM at 35 with FMJ: 0.016473 by the durability, 0.01674 in all.

## Which malfunction

**The heat's slide first**: a shot whose heat reached `StartSlideOverheat` (200) holds the slide until a shot leaves the heat at `FixSlideOverheat` (170) or below; while held, a shot with the heat above 170 and rounds in the magazine is a soft slide, with no draw spent. Otherwise one draw u from the malfunctions' own generator (each draw sets the engine's generator's seed to the generator's seed plus its count): above the total, none. **Its source** by the same u, along the parts in the order durability, round, magazine (only with rounds in it and fitted), heat. **Its kind**, by a second draw over the source's weights (the engine's generator just seeded): the durability's feed 0.1 (rounds in the magazine, a magazine fitted), misfire 0.1, jam 0.6, soft slide 0.2 (rounds in the magazine), and a hard slide only at W 5 or below (0.3 to 0.8 as W falls to nought); the round's misfire `AmmoMisfireWt` 0.7 over its two chances summed (PS: 4.02), feed 0.2, jam 0.1; the magazine's a feed; the heat's feed 0.35, jam 0.25, soft slide 0.7, hard slide as the durability's at W 5 or below, and no misfire. Each kind only where the weapon allows it; none left, no malfunction. The kinds and their numbers, which the animator takes: misfire 1, failure to eject (jam) 2, hard slide 3, soft slide 4, failure to feed 5.

**The rounds**: every shot attempt takes the chambered round out. With no malfunction the magazine's top goes into the chamber; with a failure to feed it is taken out of the magazine and out of the weapon (the shot fires; the round is kept with the weapon, and its repair throws it onto the ground as a thing); a misfire makes no shot, the chamber shows empty and that round is lost (its repair throws it out to be seen only); a jam or a slide fires and leaves the magazine as it was and the chamber empty until the repair.

## In the hands

**The shot.** A single shot rolls as its operation begins: a misfire makes no shot, the trigger and the animator's malfunction number take the hands to the misfire's state by its own transition; any other malfunction lets the fire state come by the animator's own way in (the game plays it only with none) and the shot is made at its `FiringBullet` mark, then the malfunction begins. In automatic fire each shot rolls: a misfire stops the run with no shot; any other's shot is made with its sound's tail at once (as a trigger let go) and the run stops. Either way the trigger is let go, blind fire ends, the animator is told the kind (`Malfunction` and `MalfType`), an automatic weapon's state is played outright (`MISFIRE`, `JAM`, `HARD_SLIDE`, `SOFT_SLIDE`, `FEED`), and the malfunction's operation runs: a failure to feed's case out at its `ShellEject` mark, the chamber shown spent at `DelAmmoChamber`, and at `MalfunctionOff` (0.03 s into the AKM's states) the `Malfunction` layer is on, the number −1, the kind held, the weapon at rest with it. The shooter knows of it at once (with elite Troubleshooting, its kind too).

**While it lasts**: the trigger only clicks (`DRY FIRE DISARMED`) and reminds; a reload is refused with a failure to feed (or any malfunction on a weapon that loads its chamber alone too) and reminds; the look at the magazine is refused with a failure to feed and reminds; the inventory refuses a magazine in or out with a failure to feed (or any malfunction on such a weapon) and any round into or out of a malfunctioned weapon or its fitted magazine ("You must fix malfunctions first", `IncompatibleByMalfunction`; the menu's Reload, Load and Unload refuse so too, by a malfunction the shooter knows of). **The look** (`ExamineWeapon`, L) with a malfunction looks at it (`ExamineMalfunctionTypeOperation`: the sights down, the trigger off, blind fire ended; the AKM's `JAM_LOOK`, 0.825 s to its `MalfunctionOff`), after which its kind is known; one look does. **The check of the chamber** (`CheckChamber`, Shift+T) repairs a malfunction whose kind is known, else reminds; refused with the trigger held or in blind fire. **The repair** (`RepairMalfunction`): the malfunction is cleared at once; the animator's `MalfunctionRepair` on, the layer off, the number and the kind the malfunction's, `SpeedFix` the repair's speed (one plus Troubleshooting's 0.005 a level, times 0.9 with one arm damaged and 0.8 with both, times `MalfRepairHardSlideMult` for a hard slide); at its marks the chamber shown spent, the case or the fed round (onto the ground) or the misfired round (seen only) thrown out once, a round chambered from the magazine, and at `MalfunctionOff` the weapon at rest. **A misfire is put right without its repair** by a reload's chambering, a magazine put in, a draw, the chamber's round taken out.

**The heat's stray shot**: the first shot whose heat reaches `AutoshotMinOverheat` (170) draws once a delay of 0.05 to 3 s and a chance of 0.2; drawn, at rest with a round in, the weapon fires one single shot by itself that long after the shot, unless it malfunctions meanwhile (drawn again only after a shot below 170). **The rate of fire**: a shot's heat above `FirerateOverheatBorder` (140) draws a factor of 0.85 to 1.15, kept until a shot's heat is 140 or below; automatic fire's interval becomes 60 over the rate times it, its phase kept, and (the game's cached operation) stays so after the factor is gone until a new one is drawn or the weapon is drawn again.

## The heat

Each shot that fires adds `HeatFactorByShot` times the round's `HeatFactor` times every part's `HeatFactor` times `HeatFactorGun` (times `ModHeatFactor` 1), to at most 200; the heat then falls in a straight line from the last shot, at a rate the last shot's heat sets (`L − dt · L · CoolFactorGun · k / (L − (L/200 + 0.0338 C + 0.2925)^(9.667 + 0.0668 C))`, k `CoolFactorGunMods` times every part's `CoolFactor` times `ModCoolFactor`, C `MaxOverheatCoolCoef` 6): the AKM from 200 about 4.2 a second. Its time is the raid's clock. What it does: the wear above, the chance above, the slide, the stray shot and the rate above, and **the spread**: from 100 the shot's spread grows toward `MaxCOIIncreaseMult` 1.5 at 200, by the last shot's heat (the spread spec: `ballistics.md`, "Spread"; with it the barrel's deviation by the durability, `DeviationCurve` and `DeviationMax`).

**What is kept with the weapon**: the malfunction, its source, that it and its kind are known, the last shot's heat and its time, the slide held, the sources elite cut; not kept (drawn again): the rate's factor and the stray shot.

## What the shooter is told

**Made known at the shot**: 150 ms on, the glow (Examined, forced) and the interface's `MalfunctionExamined` sound. **Reminded** (the trigger, the chamber's check unknown, a reload or the magazine's look refused): the glow asked for, which shows only at the third ask within 2 s of the first (`ShowGlowAttemptsCount` 3; a glow shown puts the count far below, so no more in that window); only where it shows, a notice: "Fix malfunction: {kind} - {the check's key}" with the kind known, else "Malfunction! Examine your weapon - {the look's key}". **Looked at**: "Malfunction: {kind}", and the first time the TypeExamined glow. **A misfire put right without the repair**: the Repaired glow and "Malfunction: {kind}". **Repaired**: the Repaired glow. The words are the install's (`Notification/MalfunctionOccurred` "Malfunction: {0}", `MalfunctionRepairRequired`, `MalfunctionExaminationRequired`; the kinds' names `EMalfunctionState/...`: "Misfire", "Failure to eject", "Failure to feed", "Bolt jammed" for either slide); a key's name as the game's settings give it (its first binding, each key by its short name, `LShift`, " + " between: "T + LShift"). The character's voice lines are not built.

**The notice** (`NotifierView` in the preloader's scene, its default template; `probe notice`): one at a time from a queue, the next once the last has gone; a box 500 wide flush with the screen's right edge, 4 over its foot (the row's and the column's padding), black at 200/255 (`_defaultBackgroundColor`), the words white in Bender at 16 within it after a left padding of 35 (margins 4 above, 5 below; wrapped to 450), the alert's icon (`notification_icon_alert_red`, 30 by 29) in that padding, its right edge 33 in, centred; each with the notices' sound (`Audio/Interface Sounds/notification_exp`). It comes and goes by the template's animator (`Default`: speed 1): shown 2.667 s, its icon fading in over the first third of a second; then hidden over 0.25 s (all of it fading out, flat at both ends), its row folded away 0.3 s from 0.083 s into that; so 3.05 s in all. **The glow** (`BattleUIMalfunctionGlow`, `Preloader UI/MalfunctionGlow`; `probe glow`): a picture of 513 by 493 at the screen's lower right corner, hanging 40 past its right and its foot, added onto the screen (`UI/Additive`: its colour times its alpha times the glow's); each kind its picture (red Examined, yellow TypeExamined, green Repaired) and its curve of alpha over its seconds (Examined seven short flashes over 1.16 s, TypeExamined one pulse over 0.62 s, Repaired two over 0.51 s), times 0.5, or 1 with low health's effect on, by the wall clock. The game's setting `MalfunctionVisability` (on as made) is taken as on.

**The inventory**: a weapon with a malfunction the shooter knows of, its magazine with a failure to feed, and a round in it with any, are drawn red on a dark red ground (`GridItemView`: the picture red, the ground 83, 0, 0 at 63).

## The barrel's glow

Each part with a `HotObject` (`probe hot <weapon>`: the AKM's receiver, its integral barrel's box at (0, −0.88, 0) of 0.015 by 0.4 by 0.015; its muzzle brake; its gas block and handguard, whose `VisibleHeatAlpha` 0 makes them warm only in thermal vision) is given the weapon's heat each frame as a temperature `T = 10 · heat/220 + 30`. Where its material has the `USEHEAT` keyword, the surface shader adds a glow to what it emits: within an ellipsoid about the box's centre whose half-axes are the box's whole size, a strength `f = max(0, 1 − d) · ((T − 30) · 0.1)²` (so by `(heat/220)²`), its colour from `_HeatColor1` (red) toward `_HeatColor2` (orange) by f, times f, `HeatVisibleFactor` (1), `VisibleHeatAlpha`, and the reflection's weight `0.5 · (x + y · 0.5 · (1 − n·v)²)` of its `_SpecVals`. So it fades with the square of the heat as the heat falls in its straight line. The shimmer over a hot barrel (`HeatEffect`, `DistortAndBlur` particles) is not built.

## In the app

The weapon in hand's logic is given its item's durability and malfunction state as it is taken in hand (`Firearm::set_wear`; `wear.rs`), and is told each frame the round it would fire next, its magazine's chance, the skills (Troubleshooting, Weapon Maintenance, the options' levels), the arms damaged (health's conditions) and the raid's clock (the simulation's own seconds since the run began); its wear and state are kept back on the item, so another weapon or the same one drawn again has its own. Its seed is the app's (one a weapon taken in hand); the game seeds each character's at random. The switches `DURABILITY`, `MALFUNCTIONS` and `OVERHEATING` (the options' SIMPLE features, restart required): off, the weapon does not wear, never malfunctions, stays cold (and no barrel glows). For a check, `--malfunction misfire|jam|feed|soft|hard` gives the first weapon in hand that malfunction at its next shot and `--heat <n>` takes it in hand that hot (`manual.md`). The heat's spread and the durability's deviation go into each bullet (`bullets.rs`). The barrel's glow is a twin of each glowing part's mesh drawn over it with the glow added (`heat_view.rs`, `heat_glow.wgsl`): by the mesh's own normal, not its bumped one, and added in linear light to the frame's numbers (`PROVISIONAL` both; the game adds in its gamma-space numbers, the same over a dark barrel); a part drawn on bones gets none (none of the install's weapons sampled has one).
