## A weapon's build

A weapon is the item and every part in its slots, at any depth; the rounds in its chambers and magazines are no part of it (`EditBuildScreen.GetItemsInBuild`: what is a `Mod`). The game draws a weapon in the hands, in its picture and on the ground with the parts it has, and its handling (weight, ergonomics, recoil) goes by them.

**Built (0.96.0, `ADVANCED ATTACHMENT SYSTEM`)**: until 0.96.0 every weapon was drawn and handled as its stock preset whatever the inventory said of its parts. Now a weapon's rig of the hands, its picture and its model on the ground are asked for by its **build's key** (`trp_data::weapon::build_key`, the app's own way of naming a build: its template, then each part by the slot it is in and its template, the parts of a part in brackets after it, by their slots' names; `trp_sim::inventory::rig_key`): a weapon built as its stock preset by its template alone, as before, so nothing of a stock weapon changes; any other by its key, assembled from it (`assemble_key`). **A weapon in hand built anew** (a part put on or taken off in the inventory, or at the bench) keeps its old rig while the new one is made away from the frame, then the hands take the new one at rest in its place, with the rounds of its chambers, its cylinder's turn, its own chamber, its magazine's count, its fire mode and its hammer taken over (`Firearm::take_over`) and its magazine and chamber as its item has them (`PROVISIONAL`, the app's: the game changes the part on the weapon's own model and its logic goes on). Off, every weapon is its stock build.
