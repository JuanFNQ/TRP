# The workbench and the edit build screen

The range's workbenches and the edit build screen they open. Some attachments cannot be changed in a raid, so TRP adds a workbench, drawn with the game's workbench model, which opens the edit build screen for the weapon in hand: every attachment the weapon takes, the weapon turned about and edited as in the game, ASSEMBLE building it outright with nothing bought and no shopping cart, as there is no trading. The bench is the hideout's **level 3** one, put in place of the old clothing crate, whose contents go to the crates they belong in, and the screen's saved builds work.

**A departure from the game, on purpose**: the game opens its edit build screen only out of a raid (from the item's menu, the handbook, the hideout's weapon builds), works on a weapon in the stash, takes the parts from the stash or the flea market and puts what comes off into the stash; its workbench offers no such line. Here the bench on the range opens it for the weapon in hand, every part of the game is to be had, ASSEMBLE builds the weapon at once with what the build names, and what comes off goes. Everything else is the game's as read below.

Read from the game's code on 2026-10-20 (`EditBuildScreen`, `ItemObserveScreen`, `WeaponPreview`, `CameraViewporter`, `ModdingScreenSlotView`, `DropDownMenu`, `EditBuildManipulation`, `DropdownManipulation`, `WeaponAssembler`, `ItemFactory.CreateAllModsEver`, `Slot.CheckConditions`, `CheckConflictingItems`, `ItemFilterExtension.CheckItem`, `ItemFilter`, `ItemViewFactory.GetSlotModClass`, `GetModSlotName`, `EWeaponModType`, `EModClass`, `EllipseCalculator`, `Spacer`, `PreviewPivot`; `HideoutInteractionTrigger`, `HideoutPlayerOwner.HideoutAreaInteraction`, `InteractionContextHelper.GetAvailableActions` for an area), from the install (the hideout's scene `bunker_2.unity`, `probe sceneprops bunker_2.unity 10_workbench/level3`; the edit build screen in `CommonUIScene.unity`, `probe screen level44 editbuildscreen 3`; the English texts' `Editbuild` "EDIT BUILD" and `hideout_area_10_name` "Workbench").

## A weapon's build

A weapon is the item and every part in its slots, at any depth; the rounds in its chambers and magazines are no part of it (`EditBuildScreen.GetItemsInBuild`: what is a `Mod`). The game draws a weapon in the hands, in its picture and on the ground with the parts it has, and its handling (weight, ergonomics, recoil) goes by them.

**Built (`ADVANCED ATTACHMENT SYSTEM`)**: until 0.96.0 every weapon was drawn and handled as its stock preset whatever the inventory said of its parts. Now a weapon's rig of the hands, its picture and its model on the ground are asked for by its **build's key** (`trp_data::weapon::build_key`, the app's own way of naming a build: its template, then each part by the slot it is in and its template, the parts of a part in brackets after it, by their slots' names; `trp_sim::inventory::rig_key`): a weapon built as its stock preset by its template alone, as before, so nothing of a stock weapon changes; any other by its key, assembled from it (`assemble_key`). **A weapon in hand built anew** (a part put on or taken off in the inventory, or at the bench) keeps its old rig while the new one is made away from the frame, then the hands take the new one at rest in its place, with the rounds of its chambers, its cylinder's turn, its own chamber, its magazine's count, its fire mode and its hammer taken over (`Firearm::take_over`) and its magazine and chamber as its item has them (`PROVISIONAL`, the app's: the game changes the part on the weapon's own model and its logic goes on). Off, every weapon is its stock build.

## The edit build screen

`EditBuildScreen` is an `ItemObserveScreen`; the modding screen (`WeaponModdingScreen`, a weapon of the stash changed part by part) is the other. Its layout is the scene's (`Common UI/Common UI/EditBuildScreen` of `CommonUIScene.unity`): its caption and sub-caption at the top's middle; the preview panel over the whole screen (`Preview Panel`: `DragTrigger`, `CameraViewporter`), holding the weapon's preview (`Weapon Preview`, `WeaponPreview`), its camera (`Weapon Camera`), its lights (`Lights`: thirteen), the slots' container and the dropdown (`DropdownMenu`); the build's name field (`BuildNameInput`, top left); the buttons' column under it (`ButtonsPanel`: NEW, OPEN, SAVE AS, SELECT ITEM, FIND PARTS, PUBLISH, a gap, DELETE; under them SAVE and ASSEMBLE, each 51 by 50 with its hint); the ellipse the slots lie on (`Ellipse Rect`: the middle three fifths across, the screen's height less 430, 25 below its middle); the four toggles under the caption (`Toggle Group`: VITAL PARTS, FUNCTIONAL MODS, EQUIPMENT PARTS, ONLY AVAILABLE); the back button (bottom right); the characteristics' panel (`CharacteristicsPanel`, left of the middle); the windows (`OpenBuildWindow`, `SelectBodyWindow`); a loader in the middle. Opened, it plays `MenuModdingOpen`; shut, `MenuModdingClose`.

**The preview** (`WeaponPreview`): the weapon's model as its loot (`CreateCleanLootPrefab`: the item with its parts), under a pivot turned (0, −90, 0) for a weapon (an armour or a vest (−90, 180, 0)), placed by its `PreviewPivot` at minus its `pivotPosition` and scaled by its `scale` (with none, the pivot at its bounds' middle); the pivot under a rotator, under a parent at the preview object's height; the camera a copy of the scene's, under that parent, 1 m behind it (`ResetRotator(-1)`) looking along it, drawing over the whole preview panel (`CameraViewporter`: its viewport the panel's rectangle on the screen). **Turned by a drag** across the panel: its yaw less the mouse's sideways move times two times 0.3 (kept within a turn either way), its tilt plus its upward move times two times 0.3, the tilt's way flipped while the yaw's cosine is below nought, held within ±30° (`ItemObserveScreen.TranslateAxes`, `WeaponPreview.Rotate`); the left and right arrows turn it too (`EditorStatic.WeaponRotationSpeed`). Each new build shown is turned back to none. **The drag** (`DragTrigger`: `onBeginDrag`, `onEndDrag`) begins only once the mouse, its button down on the panel, has moved past the event system's drag threshold (a click is no turn; the threshold the game's one event system's, 5 pixels, read from the install: inventory spec, "With the mouse"); while it lasts the mouse is hidden and held (`ShouldLockCursor`: `LockCursor`, which puts the pointer in the window's middle) and the turn is the mouse's own axes, not the pointer's place. **No zoom** here: nothing of the edit build screen reads the wheel. The inspect window's preview (the same `WeaponPreview`) has one (`ItemInfoWindowLabels.Zoom`, `WeaponPreview.Zoom`): a scroll moves the camera along its axis by the wheel's delta times 0.12 m, toward the model for a turn up; moved in so that the model's bounds (`GetBounds`: every renderer that is on, but a lens's, `linza`, and the muzzle's jet, as the model was put in) are no longer wholly in the camera's frustum, it backs off 0.01 m at a time until they are; its place along its axis is then held between −2.7 and −0.45 m. A model larger than the view can so be brought whole into it by a turn in, never closer.

**The slots** (`CreateModSlots`, `CreateModSlotViews`): every slot of the weapon and of its parts at any depth but the chambers (`chamber…`, `patron_in_weapon…`, `camora…`), each with a view (the Resources' `Prefabs/UGUI/modding_screen_slot`) tied to its bone in the model (`ObjectsFactory.AttachMods`, `ContainerCollectionView.GetBoneForSlot`: the first object of its parent's own model named after the slot, its case aside, depth first and at any depth, `TransformTools.FindTransformRecursive`, looked for before any part is hung on that model and so never in a part's; a slot whose bone is not found has none). An object named `mod_…` is not always a slot's: the Mk47's stock slot (`mod_stock_001`) has its object under its model's own `mod_stock_000`, which is no slot of the Mk47, and 47 slots of 28 weapons and parts lie so (the UMP's and the Vector's scopes and stocks, the RSASS's barrel and handguard, the PKM's rear sight, the SCAR's folding stock's own slots; `probe slotbones`, 2026-11-06). A slot's **class** (`EModClass`): vital (`Master`) if its parent cannot do without it (`_required`); else by its name as the game's mod types have it (`EWeaponModType`): mount, stock, magazine, barrel, handguard, receiver, charging handle, pistol grip, launcher and magazine shaft are gear (`Gear`), the rest functional (a name not among the types counts as a tactical one). Its **colour**: functional (242, 242, 242), vital (16, 93, 165), gear (167, 249, 142), any of them red (214, 53, 53) while it is a vital part missing; a gear or vital slot shows its name (`GetModSlotName`, localized, in capitals: magazine, stock, foregrip, barrel, charge, receiver, shaft, pistol grip; else the slot's own name) on black, or black on red (179, 27, 27, 220 of 255) while missing. Its icon by class and whether it is filled (`Modding Screen Icons/icon_mod_{tactical,vital,gear}_big_{installed,nomod}`); its filters' icons by the kinds its filter names. **The toggles** show or hide the slots by class: VITAL PARTS the vital, FUNCTIONAL MODS the functional, EQUIPMENT PARTS the gear.

**Where the slots lie** (`UpdatePositions`, `CastPointsOnEllipse`, `EllipseCalculator`, `Spacer.SpaceOut`): each shown slot's bone is put on the screen (the preview camera's viewport point times the panel's size); the line from the ellipse's middle through it is cast onto the ellipse, and the point's length along the ellipse found (the calculator's tables of arc length by whole degrees, each degree's arc summed over forty steps); the slots sorted by length and spaced along the ellipse's whole length so no two are nearer than 110 units, groups that meet merged about their mean; each slot's view put at its spaced point, a line drawn from its bone's point to it. The build already the weapon's: the notice alone.
