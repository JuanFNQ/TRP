# The underbarrel grenade launcher

The underbarrel grenade launcher, the last of the weapon's smaller parts. It closes `backlog.md`'s "The character controller: what is left". Read on 2026-11-08 from the decompiled game, the install through the probe and SPT's database, and set beside the one recording that has a launcher, `courses3\1-launched`; the notes and every probe output are in `local\ubgl_s234\` (`hands.md`, `install.md`, `grenade.md`, `rec_states.txt`). The game's names: `Launcher` (the item), `LauncherTemplate.LinkedWeapon`, `UnderbarrelContainer`, `ToggleLauncherOperation`, `LauncherIdling`, `LauncherFire`, `LauncherReload`, `LauncherUtilityOperation`, `LoadAmmoToUnderbarrelWeapon`, `DischargeAmmoFromUnderbarrelWeapon`, `FirearmController.LauncherShot`, `ProceduralWeaponAnimation.IsGrenadeLauncher`.

## The launchers and where they fit

**Three items of the class `Launcher`** (`55818b014bdc2ddc698b456b`, "UBGL", a weapon part: `probe item Launcher`): the GP-25 Kostyor (`62e7e7bbe6da9612f743f1e0`), the M203 (`6357c98711fb55120211f7e1`) and the GP-34 (`5648b62b4bdc2d9d488b4585`). **The GP-34 is not in play**: its rarity is `Not_exist`, its name untranslated, SPT's item blacklist lists it, and its controller has eight bare states with no layers. The install has no launcher for the SCAR.

**A part, with a weapon behind it.** As a part each changes the rifle it is on as any part does: ergonomics −35 and recoil −8 % (GP-25), −41 and −10 % (M203); 1.4 and 1.36 kg; two cells by one; all three forbid folding the stock (`BlocksFolding`). Everything it does as a weapon comes from the weapon template its `LinkedWeapon` names (`Launcher.WeaponTemplate`, the "ghost"), never an item of the world: `639af924d0446708ee62294e` for the GP-25, `639c3fbbd0446708ee622ee9` for the M203 (both `GrenadeLauncher`, rarity `Not_exist`), the FN40GL itself for the GP-34. The two ghosts (`probe props`) share single fire, `ReloadMode` `OnlyBarrel`, one chamber, `RecoilAngle` 90, `RecolDispersion` 15, `RecoilCenter` (0, −0.47, −0.05), `RotationCenter` (0, −0.2, 0), `CameraSnap` 3.5, `AimPlane` 0.026, `Ergonomics` 51, `HipInnaccuracyGain` 0.16, `HipAccuracyRestorationDelay` 0.2, `DeviationMax` 15, no jams, feeds, misfires or overheating; and differ in `RecoilForceUp` 230 against 183, `RecoilForceBack` 500 against 450, `RecoilCamera` 0.027 against 0.024, `CenterOfImpact` 0.7 against 0.6, `DeviationCurve` 2.9 against 1.9, the calibre (`Caliber40mmRU` against `Caliber40x46`).

**The chamber.** One, `patron_in_weapon`. The GP-25 takes the VOG-25 alone and is caseless (`UseAmmoWithoutShell`); the M203 takes the seven 40x46 rounds, the FN40GL's own list, and keeps the spent case in its tube until the next load.

**Where.** A `mod_launcher` slot: on the AK rifles themselves for the GP-25 (AKM, AKMN, AKMS, AKMSN, AK-74, AK-74N, AKS-74, AKS-74N, AK-74M, AK-101, AK-103, AK-12, VPO-136, VPO-209; the AK-102, 104 and 105 have the slot with an empty filter), and on two AR-15 barrels for the M203 (the M4A1's 14.5" `55d3632e4bdc2d972f8b4569` and the BA Hanson 13.7"). 72 parts conflict with the GP-25 (foregrips, AK rails, aftermarket handguards) and 32 with the M203. **No stock preset carries one**; the Arena trader sells an M4A1 with the M203 and an AK-12 with the GP-25 (`install.md`, "Which builds carry one"). The rifle finds its launcher as the first of its slots holding a `Launcher`, whatever the slot's name (`Weapon.GetUnderbarrelWeapon`).

## The switch

**The key** is the slot key of the weapon in hand pressed again (`FirearmHandsInputTranslator.ChangeWeaponMode`: the first or second primary weapon's, the holster's, the quick holster's; the weapon in that slot must be the one in hand). The game has a key of its own for it (`EGameKey.ToggleLauncher`) that nothing binds. The same press goes on to the player's own slot handling, which stops blind fire and puts away a thing in the left hand before it finds the weapon already in hand and does nothing more.

**Who takes it.** Only the rifle's rest (`Idling`) with a launcher fitted, and the launcher's rest (`LauncherIdling`); and through them the looks built on each (the rifle's look, magazine check, chamber check and fire mode check, and the launcher's look and check), which the switch cuts short. Every other operation drops it: a shot, a reload, a malfunction, a fold, the bipod, the draw, the put-away, the switch itself, the launcher's shot, reload, load and unload. Nothing on the way tests the sights, the sprint, blind fire, a mount, the left shoulder, a malfunction of the rifle or a loaded launcher.

**The switch in** (`Idling.ToggleLauncher`, then `ToggleLauncherOperation.Start`): the rifle's animator told `LauncherReady` on and `LauncherID` −1; the launcher's animator enabled, made the hands' animator and source of marks, its `Active` on; the rifle item marked as on its launcher (`IsUnderBarrelDeviceActive`, kept with the item); the launcher's bones taken for the weapon's (the prop bone and the left elbow's goal: `UpdateLauncherBones`); blind fire switched off. The aim point is the launcher's from the press (`IsGrenadeLauncher` set at once). **It ends at the launcher's `LauncherAppeared`**: the left hand's grip found again, the weapon's handling and recoil taken from the launcher ("While the launcher is in use"), and the launcher's rest begun. A put-away asked for meanwhile is only noted, and made at the end (by the rest, which switches back first).

**The switch out** (`LauncherIdling.ToggleLauncher`): `LauncherReady` off and `LauncherID` −1 on the rifle's animator, the launcher's `Active` off, the item's mark off. **It ends at the launcher's `LauncherDisappeared`**: the rifle's bones back, the grip found again, the rifle's handling and recoil back, the aim point the rifle's again (the folding sights redone), the mount's point found again, the rifle's animator the hands' again, the launcher's disabled, and the rifle's rest begun (a noted put-away made then). There is no time limit on either: the mark ends it.

**What it leaves as it was.** The sights: nothing on either way puts them down, and the aim key does nothing while the switch plays. The fire mode (the launcher has none). The left shoulder (carried across, and its key works on the launcher's rest). A mount (neither tested nor left). The folding sights going in (the aim point's search returns before the folds; `optics.md`, "The sights a weapon has").

**At a draw** the rest goes straight on into the switch if the item is still marked as on its launcher; every put-away from the launcher switches back first and the put-away clears the mark, so only hands lost on the launcher (a death) leave it marked.

## Firing

**The trigger on the launcher's rest** (`LauncherIdling.SetTriggerPressed`): dropped in a movement state that gives the hands no say (the rifle's list: `firing.md`, "Pressing the trigger"); with anything in the launcher's chamber it starts the shot (`LauncherFire`); with nothing, the launcher's `Fire` follows the trigger (its controller's dry click, if it has one). No aim is needed by the code.

**The shot** (`LauncherFire`, a fire operation of its own, not the single barrel's nor the rocket's): the launcher's `FIRE` played at 0.2 of its clip at once, `Fire` with the trigger; the wait before another (60 over the **rifle's** `SingleFireRate`) and the second shot's queue are the rifle's, though nothing reads them on the way out. At `FiringBullet`, once: the rifle's animator's left-hand layer is reset (`LActions` weight 0, its `Idle` played; the gesture and hand-ready triggers reset); the round leaves the launcher's chamber (its model gone with a caseless round, else moved to the launcher's shell port and the case kept, `ShellsInWeapon` 1), `AmmoInChamber` 0; the shot is made (below). At `FireEnd` the trigger is let go, `Fire` off, and the launcher's rest begins. No malfunction, wear or heat. Nothing cuts it short; the sights may be raised or lowered in it without any test. A put-away asked for in it is planned, and the way out at `FireEnd` does not look at the plan (as read: lost; not recorded).

**The shot** (`Player.LauncherShot`): from the launcher's `fireport` (the first of the name below its `WeaponPrefab`, `FindFireport`), along the player's shot direction carried into the fireport's turn; in launcher mode the weapon's motion sets that direction to the fireport's own down (`ProceduralWeaponAnimation.FindAimTransforms`, with no sight's solution), so the grenade always leaves straight along the launcher's barrel, and the range acts only on where the camera looks (through the Sight layer's clips); moved back a fifth of the weapon's length (`WeaponLn`, the rifle's) and squeezed for a wide field of view as any shot (`AdjustShotVectors`); the spread the ghost's `CenterOfImpact` (no part's accuracy) times the round's `AmmoFactor` times the ghost's barrel deviation at full durability (the launcher has none), no heat; its speed the round's times the launcher's `SpeedFactor` (its own parts' and the ghost's `Velocity`: 1 on both launchers); the recoil's strength 1 plus the round's `ammoRec` over 100; the launcher's own muzzle effects (`InitiateShot` → its container's `FirearmsEffects.StartFireEffects`: the `MuzzleManager` of its own bundle over its own tree, the deepest of its effects, `default_muzzleflash_pistol` under its fireport, with its light), never the rifle's, and its shot sound (`gp25_main`, `m203_main`: one bank each, rolloff 500, three distance clips). **The rifle's muzzle leaves the launcher's out** (`MuzzleManager.UpdateJetsAndFumes`): an object with a `MuzzleFume` at or below the first `mod_launcher`, active up to it, is never one of the rifle's effects; both launchers' flash objects carry one (on the AKM it was the deepest effect of the whole tree, so the rifle's flash, and its length's reach, came from it). The round's flight and its blast: "The grenade".

**Recorded** (`1-launched`, five shots): `FIRE` begins on the press's frame at 0.2 of its clip (`hands_anim_time` 0.200 at 6.910, 14.682, 23.356, 28.452, 33.841 s), `Fire` on, `AmmoInChamber` 0 on that frame; the launcher's rest again 0.167 s later (1 less 0.2 of 0.200 s is 0.160 s, and the transition); the sights held up through all five.

## Reloading and unloading

**The reload key in launcher mode** (`FirearmHandsInputTranslator.Reload`): a mount is left; the rounds within reach that the launcher's chamber takes; none, the phrase for no ammunition. The rifle is asked first (`CanStartReload`: no blind fire, the left hand free, the rifle's magazine examined, no failure to feed waiting on the rifle's bolt, else its reminder). Then **the launcher's rest reloads only an empty chamber** (`LauncherIdling.ReloadGrenadeLauncher`): a live round in it and nothing happens, no exchange as the single barrel's (`more-weapons.md`, "One barrel loaded alone"); a spent case alone does not count. The sights go down; `LauncherReload` begins.

**The reload** (`LauncherReload`): the launcher's `Reload` triggered; the round (the first stack within reach, in the inventory's order) moved into the chamber at once, as the reload begins. Marks: `ShellEject`, the spent case thrown (the player's velocity times 0.33) and the cases cleared, `ShellsInWeapon` 0; `MagShow`, the round's model into the launcher; `AddAmmoInChamber`, the end: the launcher's rest, and a put-away noted meanwhile made. Nothing cuts it short. No skill's action is raised, and the launcher's animator is never given the reload's speed: it plays at its controller's own. **Recorded** (four reloads): `RELOAD` for 2.508 s from the press (9.765 to 12.273 s), `AmmoInChamber` 1 at 0.797 of it (the mark at 0.795); the sights raised again at 0.834, once the operation was over (27.310 s), the rest of the clip playing out.

**The chamber's key** (`UnloadChamber`, after the rifle's own tests: `firing.md`, "The magazine's and the chamber's keys") with the launcher in use takes the launcher's round out to the first free place of the vest's and the pockets' grids, the smallest grid first; with none, nothing. The hands play it as `DischargeAmmoFromUnderbarrelWeapon`: the sights down, `Discharge` on; `DelAmmoChamber`, the round's model gone; `MagShow`, `Discharge` off, `ShellsInWeapon` as the cases stand, `CanReload` off; `MagHide`, the end: `AmmoInChamber` 0, the rest. The rifle's skill counts the chamber's action.

**From the inventory screen** with the launcher in use: a round put into its chamber plays `LOAD ONE` (`LoadAmmoToUnderbarrelWeapon`: `LoadOne` on; `MagShow`; `RemoveShell`, the case's model gone; `AddAmmoInChamber`, the end, `AmmoInChamber` 1; the inventory's move completes at the end), one taken out the discharge above; anything else done to the weapon fails (the launcher cannot be taken off, nor the rifle's magazine moved). With the rifle in use, a round moved into or out of the launcher switches to it first and then loads or unloads it, and the hands stay on the launcher. As read the rifle's rest tells a load from an unload only by whether the move has a source, which a round from a grid always has (open: record a round dragged onto the launcher).

**What the launcher's model shows** (`UnderbarrelContainer`, read from the code; its points by `probe weapon <build> patron_in_weapon` and `shellport`): one round's model and one case's, apart from the rifle's. The round's hangs at the launcher's own `patron_in_weapon` (the first of the name below its `WeaponPrefab`, depth first: `InitTransformsForAmmo`; on both launchers a child of the model's root, empty in the bundle), a quarter turn about x as the rifle's (`ParentAmmoOrShellToTransform`), whole and unspent (`SetUsed`); it is put there as the hands are made with a round in the chamber (`InitShellsAndAmmo`, the item's cases at the port with it), and at the `MagShow` of the reload and of the load from the inventory (`SetRoundIntoWeapon`), though the reload's round is the chamber's from its start. It goes at the discharge's `DelAmmoChamber` (`DestroyPatronInWeapon`), and at the shot: gone for a round whose template drops its case (`RemoveShellAfterFire`: the VOG-25), else moved to the launcher's own `shellport` (the first of the name, depth first; the M203's stands where its chamber does, the GP-25 has none) and turned spent, its case's objects on (`MoveAmmoFromChamberToShellPort`). **The case leaves at a `ShellEject` of the reload or of a look** (`LauncherReload` and `LauncherUtilityOperation.OnShellEjectEvent`: `StartSpawnShell` with the player's velocity times 0.33, the cases cleared and `ShellsInWeapon` set either way): let go at the end of the frame from where the port has carried it, with the launcher's own throw (its container's `ShellExtractionData.ShotSettings`: the M203's ranges all nought and the port's move times 1, `probe script <its bundle> ShellExtractionData`) along its own port's forward (`SpawnExtractingCartridge`), and flown, timed out and heard as the rifle's (`firing.md`, "Spent cases"). The M203's `CHECK SHELL` (a look into a chamber holding only a case) plays its `ShellEject` at 0.682 of its 4.267 s, so a look throws the case and the reload after has none to throw. At the load's `RemoveShell` the case's model is taken away unthrown (`RemoveShellInWeapon`). The M203's discharge clips carry a `ShellEject` and a `RemoveShell` too, which the discharge hears as nothing (`DischargeAmmoFromUnderbarrelWeapon` has no handler for them: the base's `ProhibitedCall`). The clips move both points: the M203's reload carries `patron_in_weapon` with the left hand and lowers `shellport` out of the open tube before its `ShellEject` (`probe clips <its bundle> m203_ammo_in_wpn tracks shellport <time>`: 0.12 m below its rest at the mark's 0.82 s), which, the M203's ranges being nought, is the whole of the case's throw but the player's share.

## While the launcher is in use

**The aim point** is the launcher's `mod_aim_camera` alone (`ProceduralWeaponAnimation.FindAimTransforms` with `IsGrenadeLauncher`: no optic, no part's sight); the rifle's aim index is kept, and the scope key sets it to 0. **The weapon's place** each frame is the prop bone's (`UpdateWeaponBoneByLauncherWeaponBone`: the hands' `Weapon` transform set to `Weapon_anim_override`), so the launcher's clips carry the rifle. **The handling** is the ghost's (`InitWeaponData` with the launcher, at `LauncherAppeared`): its recoil figures and curves, its return speeds and damping, its hip accuracy, its camera snap, with the launcher's own parts' recoil (none) and the rifle's stock's; the rifle's skill buffs; but the ergonomics and the sway, the turn-away, the rotation centre, the recoil's pivot, the mount's centre and the aim planes stay the rifle's.

**The range** (the optic's calibration keys, `OpticCalibrationSwitchUp` and `Down`, in launcher mode): on the launcher's rest and its looks only, one step along a fixed list of 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 350 and 400 m (`Launcher.SightingRange`, the same for every launcher; the templates' figures are not read), the zeroing panel showing the figure; in the shot or the reload, nothing. The animator's `SightingRange` follows the figure at 400 m a second, whole metres a frame (the step's figure truncated), only while the launcher rests. At every draw the figure is put back to 100, but not the step it stands on (the next press goes from the old step). **Recorded**: `SightingRange` 0 throughout `1-launched`, no press: the Sight layer at its first clip.

**The rifle's own keys**: the fire mode key and its check do nothing (but remind of a malfunction of the rifle); the magazine check and the bipod do nothing; the chamber check and the look play the launcher's (the check refused for a caseless launcher with nothing in it, the look refused with the sights up; the looks' rule of 2.5 s without their first mark: `firing.md`); the reload and the chamber's key go to the launcher; the quick reload and the magazine's key find nothing to do. A malfunction of the rifle does not stop the launcher. Blind fire: the hands refuse it (the launcher's rest has none), though the body's parameter is still set (open). The lights' and the scope's modes work on the launcher's animator.

**What switches back first**: putting the weapon away and every change of weapon (the fast one too, after the switch's clip), and the backpack dropped. Death leaves the item marked (above).

## The grenade

A launched grenade is an ordinary shot of the ballistics (`InitiateShot`, `BallisticsCalculator.Shoot`), from the standalone launchers (`FirearmController.Shot`) and the underbarrel ones (`LauncherShot`) alike; only rockets and flares are made as things. It flies the bullets' table with gravity (`TrajectoryCalculator.NextForPlayer`), at the round's speed times the ghost's (76 m/s here). **Every hit** first does the round's damage as a bullet's (199, by its speed). **Its fuse** is armed by time: the shot's age at the hit must be at least `FuzeArmTimeSec` (VOG-25 0.05 s, about 3.8 m; M381 and M441 0.04 s; M386, M406, M433 0.26 s, about 20 m); before that the hit is only the material's own. A 40 mm round never ricochets (`RicochetChance` 0), though armour may turn it, and a chain fence stops it. **Armed, at its first hit**: the effect `smallgrenade_expl` at the hit, turned to the surface (`EmitGrenade(type, hit, normal, forward ? 1 : 0)`: its sound 5 cm out along the normal, and none for a hit coming out of a surface), with its scorch along the normal, and no material's hit besides (`ShowHitEffectOnExplode`; a later hit that sets it off again, of a bullet it turned into, plays no effect: `IsFirstHit`); then the hand grenade's own blast (`ExplosionSharedMethods.Explosion`, `grenades-and-knife.md`, "The blast" and "The fragments") from 0.08 m off the surface along its normal, by the round's `ExplosionStrength`, its fragments and its blast distances; its sound at 0.05 m off. **When**: in the world's ballistics tick (`DoWorldTick`: the players' tick, their move with it, before the ballistics'), so the blast finds the character where it now stands, where a thrown grenade's finds it before its move; its fragments are made on the hit's frame, the first nine moving on the next (recorded: nine at their age nought on the hit's frame, six on the next). **The big rounds' dummy** (12.7x108, .338 LM and the rest with `HasGrenaderComponent` and no blast: `GrenadeComponentIsDummy`, no blindness, no contusion reach and a greatest distance of nought): at their first hit their `big_round_impact` effect beside their own hit, no blast. **Already set beside the game** (`bin/bullets` on `1-launched`, unchanged, 2026-11-08): the five VOG-25s and their 75 fragments, 0.18 mm apart on average and 4.06 mm at most, 106 hits of 106 alike in state, speed, damage and age; the grenade left 7.3 degrees (±0.4) above the camera's forward at the 100 m step.

## The skills

`Launcher` (`ESkillId.Launcher`, tied to the underbarrel part's class) earns only by the grenade's hits (the shot names the launcher); `AttachedLauncher` is tied to no item and never earns nor applies (`skills.md`). The chamber's action of a load or an unload from the inventory counts for the rifle; the launcher's reload raises none; Recoil Control counts the ghost's recoil each shot. No buff reaches the launcher's switch or reload.
